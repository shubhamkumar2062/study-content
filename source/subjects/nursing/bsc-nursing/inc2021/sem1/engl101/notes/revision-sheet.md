# Last-night revision sheet: Communicative English

## Unit 1 — Communication

- **Communication** = sending and receiving a message so both share the same meaning; **feedback** makes it two-way.
- Process: sender → encoding → message → channel → decoding → receiver → feedback; **noise** and **context** act throughout.
- Types: verbal (oral, written), non-verbal, visual; formal/informal; downward, upward, horizontal; intrapersonal, interpersonal, group, mass.
- **7 Cs**: clear, concise, concrete, correct, coherent, complete, courteous.
- Barriers: physical, physiological, language, psychological, cultural, organisational, personal.
- LSRW: listening and reading = receptive; speaking and writing = productive.
- Communicative competence: grammatical, sociolinguistic, discourse, strategic.

## Unit 2 — LSRGW basics

- Hearing (physical) vs listening (active). Listening types: discriminative, comprehensive, critical, empathetic, appreciative; faults: selective, passive.
- 44 English sounds: 24 consonants + 20 vowels (12 pure + 8 diphthongs). Schwa /ə/ is the commonest vowel. Watch /v/–/w/, /s/–/ʃ/, /θ/–/t/.
- Word stress: **PA**tient, **ME**dicine, hyper**TEN**sion, in**JEC**tion; noun **RE**cord / verb re**CORD**.
- Content words stressed, function words weak. Falling tone: statements and *wh*-questions; rising: yes/no questions.
- Voice modulation = pitch, volume, pace, tone, emphasis. Pause separates ideas; silence gives space.
- Medical words: prefix + root + suffix (brady-card-ia; gastr-ectomy).
- 12 tenses. Notes: past for actions done ("gave"), present for current state ("is drowsy"); "has vomited twice since morning".
- Linkers: addition (moreover), contrast (however), cause (because), result (therefore), sequence (first, then, finally).
- Paraphrase = same meaning, own words. British spelling: haemoglobin, oedema, paediatric, diarrhoea.

## Unit 3 — Attentive listening

- Process: receiving → understanding → remembering → evaluating → responding.
- **SOLER**: Sit squarely, Open posture, Lean, Eye contact, Relaxed.
- Tools: encouragers, open questions, clarifying, paraphrasing, reflecting feelings, summarising, silence. Avoid false reassurance and judging.
- Situations: announcement (who, what, where, when), description, narrative (sequence), instruction (verbs, numbers, warnings), discussion, demonstration.
- Listen for gist, specific information, detail, attitude, inference.
- **Verbatim**: verbal/telephone orders and critical results → write and **read back**; patient's words in quotation marks; say numbers digit by digit.
- Lectures: signposts, key words, Cornell / outline / mind map, review within a day.

## Unit 4 — Speaking

- Formal, neutral, informal situations; register. Factors: setting, topic, relationship, attitude, language.
- Polite request: "Could you…, please?" Permission: "May I…?" Disagree: "I see your point, but…"
- Complaint handling: listen, empathise, apologise, resolve, follow up.
- Telephone: identify self and area, purpose, read back, note message (date/time, caller, message, action, taken by), document.
- **SBAR**: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation (ISBAR adds Identify). Handover: changes, risks, pending tasks, questions.
- Rounds: name, diagnosis, events, findings, results, concerns, questions.
- Case presentation: identification → complaints → history → examination → investigations → management → nursing care → education → summary.
- Presentation: intro (topic, outline) → 3–5 points → conclusion and questions; signposts, eye contact, pauses.

## Unit 5 — Reading

- Skimming (main idea), scanning (one detail), intensive (orders), extensive (pleasure), critical (evaluate). **SQ3R**: survey, question, read, recite, review.
- Context clues: definition, example, contrast, word parts.
- Abbreviations: only approved ones. Avoid U, IU, QD, QOD, trailing zero (5.0), missing leading zero (.5).
- Visuals: title → labels/units → time scale → trend → highs/lows → meaning.
- Complete medicine order: patient, date/time, drug, dose, route, frequency, duration, instructions, signature. Unclear → ask.
- Reports: result, units, reference range, flags (H/L), trend; radiology: findings + impression.
- Grammar: a/an by sound (an hour, a university); suffering **from**; discuss (no "about"); cope **with**; each … **has**.

## Unit 6 — Writing

- Judged on task fulfilment, coherence and cohesion, vocabulary, grammar.
- Documentation: factual, accurate, complete, timely, organised, legible, signed, confidential; single-line correction, never erase.
- History: identification, chief complaints, present/past/family/personal/socio-economic history.
- Notes: narrative, **SOAPIE**, **DAR**.
- Summary: main ideas, own words, about one-third length, title.
- Anecdotal record: one incident, objective, date/time/place, separate comment, signatures.
- Formal letter: sender's address, date, receiver, **subject**, salutation, body (purpose–details–request), **Yours faithfully**, signature, name.
- Reflection — **Gibbs**: description, feelings, evaluation, analysis, conclusion, action plan.
- Report: title, to/from/date, introduction, details, action, recommendations, signature; past tense, passive where suitable.
- Paper: narrow topic, literature, outline, own words + citation (no plagiarism), references. **Abstract**: IMRaD, 150–300 words, written last.
- CV: contact, objective, education, registration, experience, skills, training, achievements, references, declaration.

## Unit 7 — LSRW together

- Listen → read → think → speak → write → listen again.
- Critical thinking: source, evidence, fact vs opinion vs inference, bias, currency, verification; choose the safest evidence-based option.
- Instructions: title, things needed, numbered imperative steps, warnings, contact; **teach-back**.
- Group discussion: facts, listen, build on others, polite disagreement, stay on topic, summarise.
- Role play and demonstration with return demonstration.
- Error analysis: identify → cause → correct → error log. Types: grammatical, lexical, pronunciation, spelling, pragmatic, discourse.

## Exam checklist

- 50 marks, 2 hours: MCQ 8, essay 10, short 4 × 5, very short 6 × 2. Pass 40%.
- Letters and reports: full format first. Re-read every answer for tense, agreement and spelling.
