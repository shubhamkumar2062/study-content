# How to use this subject

Applied Psychology (PSYC120) is a first-semester course of 3 credits (60 theory hours). It is mostly descriptive: definitions, classifications, theories with their authors, and the role of the nurse. Marks come from writing well-organised answers with headings, a small diagram or table, a nursing example and a "role of the nurse" section.

Study one notes file a day: read it, close it, write the "Remember" list from memory, then answer that unit's questions without looking at the model answers.

## Exam pattern (university theory paper)

Applied Psychology is **not a separate paper**. The university theory paper is shared with Applied Sociology: **Section A Applied Sociology (37 marks) + Section B Applied Psychology (38 marks) = 75 marks in 3 hours**. Your mock paper here is Section B only.

| Part of Section B | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Multiple choice | 7 × 1 | 7 |
| Essay (long answer) | 1 × 10 | 10 |
| Short answers | 3 × 5 | 15 |
| Very short answers | 3 × 2 | 6 |
| **Total** | | **38** |

There is **no internal choice**: answer every question. Plan about 90 minutes for this section, leaving the rest of the 3 hours for Sociology. The pass rule and internal-assessment weight are set by the INC regulations and your university. This pattern follows INC B.Sc Nursing Regulations 2020, Appendix 3; universities can vary the details, so confirm with your university.

## How probability ratings work

Every written question has a rating:

- **High** — the topic carries many hours or appears often; prepare a full answer.
- **Medium** — asked every few years or as part of a larger question.
- **Low** — less likely, but good for MCQs and very short answers.

These ratings are estimates from syllabus weight and common patterns in nursing psychology papers. They are not a promise of what will be asked.

## Scoring tips for this subject

1. Start with a clear **definition** in one or two lines; name the psychologist if there is one (Maslow, Selye, Erikson, Pavlov).
2. Use **headings and numbered points**; examiners scan for structure.
3. Draw a **simple diagram**: Maslow's pyramid, motivation cycle, GAS stages, sensory process, memory stages, communication model.
4. Use a **table** for classifications and comparisons (types of conflict, defence mechanisms, illusion vs hallucination).
5. Give a **nursing example** for each point — a child in the ward, a patient before surgery, an older adult.
6. End every long answer with **"Role of the nurse"** in 5–8 points; most questions in this course ask for it directly or indirectly.
7. In very short answers (2 marks): definition + one example, nothing more.
8. Do not mix up similar terms: repression vs suppression, illusion vs hallucination, proactive vs retroactive interference, sensation vs perception, intelligence vs aptitude.
