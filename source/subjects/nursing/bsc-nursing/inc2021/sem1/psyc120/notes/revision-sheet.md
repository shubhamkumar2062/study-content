# Last-night revision sheet

**Psychology** = scientific study of behaviour and mental processes. Goals: describe, explain, predict, control. Wundt opened the first lab in 1879 (Leipzig).

**Methods:** introspection, observation, experiment (independent → dependent variable; experimental vs control group), case study, survey, interview, tests, correlation.

**Body–mind:** psychosomatic disorders (asthma, peptic ulcer, hypertension); biopsychosocial model. Heredity sets limits, environment shapes; twin and adoption studies.

**Brain:** frontal = judgement, personality; temporal = hearing, memory; limbic = emotion, memory; hypothalamus = hunger, thirst, temperature, sleep. Sympathetic = fight or flight.

**Sensation → perception.** Absolute threshold, adaptation, sensory deprivation vs overload (ICU).

**Mental health** = wellbeing, coping, productive work, contribution; a continuum. **Mental hygiene** = preserve, prevent, early treatment. NMHP 1982, DMHP 1996, Tele-MANAS 14416, Mental Healthcare Act 2017.

**Defence mechanisms** (unconscious, protect from anxiety): repression, denial, projection, rationalisation, displacement, regression, reaction formation, sublimation (healthy), suppression (conscious, healthy), compensation, conversion. Don't break defences suddenly.

**Conflicts (Lewin):** approach–approach, avoidance–avoidance, approach–avoidance, double approach–avoidance.

**Erikson:** trust, autonomy, initiative, industry, identity, intimacy, generativity, integrity. **Piaget:** sensorimotor, preoperational, concrete, formal. **Separation anxiety:** protest, despair, detachment. **Kübler-Ross:** denial, anger, bargaining, depression, acceptance.

**Personality** (Latin *persona*). Id–ego–superego. Types: Hippocrates (sanguine, choleric, melancholic, phlegmatic), Kretschmer (pyknic, athletic, asthenic), Sheldon (endo-, meso-, ectomorph), Jung (introvert, extrovert, ambivert), Type A/B. Big Five = OCEAN. Projective: Rorschach, TAT. Personality disorders: clusters A odd, B dramatic, C anxious.

**Good test:** reliable, valid, objective, standardised, norms, practicable.

**Attention:** involuntary, voluntary, habitual; span ≈ 6–7 items. **Perception:** Gestalt — figure–ground, proximity, similarity, closure, continuity. Illusion = misread real stimulus; hallucination = no stimulus.

**Intelligence:** Spearman g + s; Thurstone PMA; Gardner multiple. IQ = MA ÷ CA × 100. Average 90–110; below 70 + adaptive problems = intellectual disability (mild, moderate, severe, profound). Binet 1905; Wechsler deviation IQ.

**Aptitude** = potential in a specific area; achievement = present learning.

**Learning:** Pavlov (classical: UCS, CS, extinction, generalisation), Thorndike (trial and error; readiness, exercise, effect), Skinner (operant: reinforcement, shaping), Köhler (insight), Bandura (observation). Plateau; transfer of learning.

**Memory:** encoding → storage → retrieval. STM ≈ 7 ± 2 items, ~20–30 s. Ebbinghaus curve: fast early forgetting. Interference: proactive (old blocks new), retroactive (new blocks old). Improve: understand, chunk, mnemonics, spaced revision, self-test, sleep.

**Thinking:** inductive (specific → general), deductive (general → specific). Problem-solving steps = nursing process.

**Motivation cycle:** need → drive → behaviour → goal → relief. **Maslow:** physiological, safety, love, esteem, self-actualisation. Yerkes–Dodson: moderate arousal best.

**Emotion:** feeling + body change + appraisal + expression. James–Lange, Cannon–Bard, Schachter–Singer.

**Selye GAS:** alarm → resistance → exhaustion. Eustress vs distress. Coping: problem-focused vs emotion-focused.

**Attitude:** affective, behavioural, cognitive. Change: information, contact, persuasion, cognitive dissonance (Festinger). Scales: Likert, Thurstone, semantic differential.

**Communication:** sender, message, channel, receiver, feedback; 7 Cs. **Peplau:** orientation, identification, exploitation, resolution. Barriers: physical, physiological, psychological, language, cultural. SBAR handover.

**Survival:** time management, stress coping, resilience, work–life balance (prevent burnout).

**Self-empowerment:** self-awareness, SMART goals, assertiveness, learning, responsibility. Grooming: short nails, tied hair, clean uniform — infection control.

**Always end with role of the nurse.**
