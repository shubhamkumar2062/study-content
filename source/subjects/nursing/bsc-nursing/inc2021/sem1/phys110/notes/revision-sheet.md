# Last-night revision sheet — Applied Physiology

Values are approximate adult figures; laboratories and textbooks differ slightly — write "about".

## 1. General physiology

- Homeostasis = constant internal environment; mostly **negative feedback**; positive feedback: labour (oxytocin), clotting, LH surge.
- Transport: passive (diffusion, facilitated diffusion, osmosis, filtration) vs active (Na+–K+ pump: **3 Na+ out, 2 K+ in per ATP**; cotransport with Na+) vs vesicular (endo-/exocytosis).
- Total body water **60%** of weight: ICF 40%, ECF 20% (interstitial 15%, plasma 5%). Na+ main ECF cation (135–145 mmol/L); K+ main ICF cation (plasma 3.5–5).
- Isotonic 0.9% NaCl; hypotonic → cells swell; hypertonic → cells shrink.
- Cell cycle G1 → S → G2 → M; G0 = neurons, cardiac muscle.
- Wound healing: haemostasis → inflammation → proliferation → remodelling.

## 2. Respiratory

- Diaphragm ≈ 75% of quiet inspiration; expiration passive; intrapleural pressure −2.5 to −6 mmHg.
- Surfactant (type II cells) lowers surface tension; lack → respiratory distress syndrome in preterm babies.
- TV 500, IRV 3000, ERV 1100, RV 1200, VC 4600, TLC 5800 mL; dead space 150 mL; minute ventilation 6 L/min.
- Alveolar PO2 104 / PCO2 40; venous 40 / 45; arterial PO2 95–100.
- O2: 97% as oxyHb (1.34 mL/g Hb); CO2: 70% HCO3−, 23% carbamino, 7% dissolved; chloride shift.
- Right shift: ↑CO2, ↑H+, ↑temperature, ↑2,3-BPG (Bohr effect). CO binds Hb ~200× more than O2.
- Central chemoreceptors → H+ from CO2 (strongest drive); carotid/aortic bodies → low PO2.
- Cyanosis > 5 g/dL reduced Hb; hypoxia types: hypoxic, anaemic, stagnant, histotoxic.
- Cheyne–Stokes (waxing–waning + apnoea); Biot's (groups of breaths + apnoea).

## 3. Digestive

- Saliva 1–1.5 L/day, ptyalin; purely nervous control (VII, IX).
- Gastric juice pH 1–3.5; parietal → HCl + intrinsic factor; chief → pepsinogen; G cells → gastrin; phases cephalic 30 / gastric 60 / intestinal 10%.
- HCl: carbonic anhydrase + H+–K+ ATPase; alkaline tide.
- Pancreatic juice pH ~8; secretin → bicarbonate; CCK → enzymes + gall bladder; enterokinase → trypsin.
- Bile salts emulsify fat, form micelles; 90–95% recycled (enterohepatic). Vitamin K, A, D, E need bile.
- Glucose/galactose with Na+ (SGLT1); fats → chylomicrons → lacteals; B12 + intrinsic factor → terminal ileum.
- Movements: peristalsis (propels), segmentation (mixes), mass movements (gastrocolic reflex); defaecation centre S2–S4.

## 4. Circulation and blood

- SA node 70–80/min → AV node (0.1 s delay) → His → bundle branches → Purkinje. Heart cannot be tetanised.
- Cardiac cycle 0.8 s (systole 0.3, diastole 0.5); S1 = AV valves close; S2 = semilunar valves close.
- SV 70 mL, EF 55–70%, **CO = SV × HR ≈ 5 L/min**; Frank–Starling law.
- **BP = CO × peripheral resistance**; 120/80; pulse pressure 40; MAP ≈ DP + ⅓PP ≈ 93.
- Baroreceptors (carotid sinus IX, aortic arch X) → short term; RAAS, ADH, ANP, kidney → long term.
- Pulse 60–100/min; pulse deficit in atrial fibrillation.
- Coronary flow 225–250 mL/min (4–5% CO), mainly diastole; O2 extraction ~70%.
- Pulmonary circuit low pressure (25/8), hypoxia → vasoconstriction.
- Postural hypotension: fall ≥ 20 systolic / ≥ 10 diastolic within 3 min of standing.
- Blood 5–6 L, pH 7.35–7.45, plasma 55%; albumin → oncotic pressure.
- RBC 4.5–5.5 million/µL (M); Hb 13–17 (M), 12–15 (F) g/dL; life **120 days**; erythropoietin (kidney), iron, B12, folate.
- WBC 4,000–11,000/µL: N 50–70, L 20–40, M 2–8, E 1–4, B 0–1%. Platelets 1.5–4 lakh/µL, life 7–10 days.
- Haemostasis: spasm → platelet plug (vWF, ADP, TXA2) → coagulation → retraction → fibrinolysis (plasmin).
- Intrinsic XII → XI → IX (+VIII); extrinsic III + VII; common X → prothrombin activator → thrombin → fibrin; XIII stabilises. Vitamin K: **II, VII, IX, X**. Haemophilia A = VIII.
- BT 1–5 min; CT 4–10 min; PT ~11–16 s (INR); aPTT ~25–40 s.
- ABO: O universal donor, AB universal recipient; Rh− mother + Rh+ baby → haemolytic disease of newborn.
- IgG crosses placenta; IgA in milk/secretions; IgM first; IgE allergy. B cells → antibodies; T cells (thymus) → cell-mediated.

## 5. Endocrine and senses

- Anterior pituitary: GH, TSH, ACTH, FSH, LH, prolactin. Posterior: ADH, oxytocin (made in hypothalamus).
- GH excess: gigantism / acromegaly; ADH lack: diabetes insipidus.
- T3/T4: ↑ BMR, brain growth → cretinism if lacking; iodine lack → goitre; calcitonin ↓ Ca.
- PTH ↑ Ca (bone, kidney, vitamin D); low Ca → tetany, Chvostek, Trousseau. Serum Ca 9–10.5 mg/dL.
- Insulin (β) lowers glucose; glucagon (α), adrenaline, cortisol, GH raise it. Renal threshold 180 mg/dL.
- Adrenal cortex: aldosterone (salt), cortisol (sugar, stress), androgens; medulla: adrenaline (fight or flight).
- Pineal → melatonin (circadian); thymus → T cells.
- Temperature control: hypothalamus; heat → vasodilation, sweating; cold → vasoconstriction, shivering.
- Accommodation: ciliary muscle contracts, lens more convex, pupil constricts, convergence.
- Rods: dim light, rhodopsin, vitamin A; cones: colour, fovea. Visual cortex area 17.
- Myopia → concave; hypermetropia/presbyopia → convex; astigmatism → cylindrical.
- Hearing: eardrum → ossicles → oval window → cochlea → organ of Corti → VIII → temporal lobe (41, 42); 20–20,000 Hz.
- Taste: VII (ant. 2/3), IX (post. 1/3); smell → limbic system, no thalamic relay.

## 6. Musculoskeletal

- Osteoblasts build, osteoclasts resorb; vitamin D, PTH, calcitonin, GH, sex hormones.
- Fracture healing: haematoma → inflammation → soft callus → hard callus → remodelling.
- Synovial joints: hinge, pivot, ball and socket, condyloid, saddle, plane.
- OA = cartilage wear; RA = autoimmune synovitis; gout = urate crystals.
- Contraction: ACh → AP → T tubules → Ca2+ from SR → troponin → cross-bridges (ATP) → sliding filaments; ATP needed to detach → rigor mortis.
- Cardiac: intercalated discs, syncytium, long refractory period. Smooth: calmodulin, slow sustained tone.

## 7. Renal and reproductive

- Renal blood flow 1200–1300 mL/min (20–25% CO); **GFR 125 mL/min = 180 L/day**; urine 1–1.5 L/day.
- NFP = 60 − (18 + 32) = 10 mmHg; autoregulation 80–180 mmHg (myogenic, tubuloglomerular feedback).
- PCT reabsorbs 65% Na+/water, all glucose; loop = countercurrent; ADH → water; aldosterone → Na+ in, K+ out.
- Urine: pH ~6, SG 1.010–1.025, no glucose/protein. Oliguria < 400 mL/day.
- Micturition: stretch at 300–400 mL → pelvic nerves → S2–S4 → detrusor contracts; external sphincter voluntary (pudendal).
- Menstrual cycle 28 days: menstrual 1–5, proliferative (oestrogen), ovulation day 14 (LH surge), secretory (progesterone); luteal phase 14 days.
- Oogenesis: arrest prophase I (before birth) → secondary oocyte at ovulation, arrest metaphase II → completed at fertilisation.
- Fertilisation in ampulla; capacitation, acrosome reaction, zona reaction; father decides sex.
- Implantation day 6–7; hCG keeps corpus luteum; pregnancy test.
- Breast: prolactin = milk production; oxytocin = let-down; colostrum rich in IgA.
- Spermatogenesis 64–74 days, 2–3 °C below body temperature; FSH → Sertoli; LH → Leydig → testosterone.
- Semen: seminal vesicle 60% (fructose), prostate 30% (alkaline).

## 8. Nervous system

- RMP −70 mV; threshold −55; Na+ in (depolarisation), K+ out (repolarisation); all-or-none; saltatory conduction.
- Medulla: cardiac, vasomotor, respiratory centres; pons: pneumotaxic; cerebellum: coordination (ataxia, intention tremor).
- Areas: motor 4, Broca 44, sensory 3-1-2, auditory 41-42, visual 17, Wernicke (temporal).
- Dorsal column: fine touch, proprioception; lateral spinothalamic: pain, temperature.
- UMN: spastic, brisk reflexes, Babinski +; LMN: flaccid, areflexia, wasting.
- Spinal nerves 31 pairs (8, 12, 5, 5, 1); dorsal root sensory, ventral motor.
- Sympathetic T1–L2, NA; parasympathetic III, VII, IX, X, S2–S4, ACh.
- Hypothalamus: temperature, hunger, thirst, ADH, pituitary, ANS, emotion, circadian. Hippocampus: memory; amygdala: fear.
- Semicircular canals: rotation; utricle/saccule: position, linear acceleration.
- Pain: fast Aδ (sharp), slow C (burning); gate control; endorphins; referred: heart → left arm, diaphragm → shoulder tip, appendix → umbilicus.
- Knee jerk L2–L4, ankle S1–S2, biceps C5–C6, triceps C6–C7.
- CSF: choroid plexus, 500 mL/day, 150 mL volume; lateral → III → aqueduct → IV → subarachnoid → arachnoid villi. Protein 15–45, glucose 50–80 mg/dL.
- BBB: tight junctions + astrocytes; absent at area postrema; leaky in newborns.
- GCS 3–15 (E4 V5 M6).
