# Last-night revision sheet: Nursing Foundation I

(Examined in the Nursing Foundations I & II paper at the end of the second semester.)

## Health and health care (Unit 1)

- **WHO 1948**: complete physical, mental and social well-being, not merely the absence of disease or infirmity.
- **Dimensions**: physical, mental, social, emotional, spiritual, intellectual, vocational, environmental.
- **Maslow**: physiological → safety → love/belonging → esteem → self-actualisation.
- **Suchman's stages of illness**: symptom experience → sick role → medical care contact → dependent role → recovery.
- **Prevention**: primordial; primary (promotion + specific protection); secondary (early diagnosis, screening); tertiary (disability limitation, rehabilitation).
- **Care levels**: primary (SC, PHC) → secondary (CHC, district hospital) → tertiary (medical colleges). PHC 30,000 / CHC 1,20,000 (plains).

## History and profession (Unit 2)

- **Florence Nightingale**: 12 May 1820 – 13 August 1910; Kaiserswerth; Crimean War 1854 (Scutari); St Thomas' school 1860; *Notes on Nursing* 1859; statistics; 12 May = International Nurses Day.
- **India**: Charaka's four pillars; first school of nursing, Madras 1871; TNAI 1908; B.Sc Nursing 1946; INC Act 1947.
- **Functions**: independent, dependent, interdependent. **Metaparadigm**: person, environment, health, nursing.
- **Ethics**: autonomy, beneficence, non-maleficence, justice, veracity, fidelity, confidentiality, accountability.

## Communication and records (Unit 3)

- **Elements**: referent, sender, message, channel, receiver, feedback, context.
- **Therapeutic**: active listening, open questions, silence, clarifying, reflecting, restating, summarising.
- **Blocks**: false reassurance, advice, "why", changing the subject, judging, jargon.
- **Peplau phases**: (pre-interaction), orientation, working, termination.
- **SBAR**: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation.
- **Records**: date, time, signature; facts not opinions; single line through errors; no erasing; no blank lines; incident report kept out of the chart.
- **Systems**: source-oriented, POMR, SOAP(IE), PIE, focus (DAR), narrative, charting by exception, electronic.

## Vital signs (Unit 4)

| | Adult | Newborn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature (oral) | 36.5–37.5 °C | — |
| Pulse | 60–100/min | 120–160/min |
| Respiration | 12–20/min | 30–60/min |
| BP | below 120/80 mmHg | — |
| SpO2 | 95–100% | — |

- Axillary ≈ 0.5 °C lower, rectal ≈ 0.5 °C higher than oral. °F = °C × 9/5 + 32.
- **Fever** = set point raised; **hyperthermia** = set point normal, heat loss fails. Hyperpyrexia > 41 °C; hypothermia < 35 °C.
- **Stages**: onset (chills) → course (fastigium) → defervescence (crisis or lysis).
- **Types**: continuous (<1 °C swing), remittent (>1 °C, never normal), intermittent (back to normal — malaria), relapsing.
- **Tepid sponge**: lukewarm water, axillae and groins, slow strokes, stop if shivering, recheck after 30 minutes.
- **Heat**: vasodilation, relaxes. **Cold**: vasoconstriction, numbs, reduces swelling. About 20 minutes (rebound).
- **Pulse**: no thumb; full minute if irregular; apical = 5th ICS left MCL; **pulse deficit = apical − radial**.
- **Breathing patterns**: Cheyne–Stokes (cycles + apnoea), Kussmaul (deep, fast — DKA), Biot's (irregular + apnoea), orthopnoea (needs to sit up).
- **BP**: cuff bladder 40% width / 80% length; palpate first; inflate 30 mmHg above; deflate 2–3 mmHg/s; Korotkoff I = systolic, V = diastolic. Narrow cuff → high; wide → low; arm below heart → high.

## Infection control and safety (Unit 5)

- **Chain**: agent → reservoir → exit → transmission → entry → susceptible host.
- **Stages**: incubation → prodromal → illness → convalescence. **Inflammation**: redness, heat, swelling, pain, loss of function.
- **HAI**: ≥ 48 h after admission.
- **Medical asepsis** = clean; **surgical asepsis** = sterile (kills spores). Autoclave 121 °C, 15 lb/in², at least 15 min.
- **WHO 5 moments**; rub 20–30 s; wash 40–60 s.
- **PPE on**: gown → mask → goggles → gloves. **Off**: gloves → goggles → gown → mask → hand hygiene.
- **BMW Rules 2016**: Yellow — anatomical, soiled, chemical, expired medicines; Red — contaminated plastics (tubing, catheters, syringes without needles); White translucent — sharps; Blue — glass and metal implants. Untreated waste not beyond 48 h.
- **Fire**: RACE (Rescue, Alarm, Contain, Extinguish/Evacuate); PASS (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep).
- **Morse Fall Scale**: ≥ 45 high risk.
- **Restraints**: last resort; order + consent; tie to bed frame, quick-release knot, two fingers; check often; release about every 2 h; document.
- **Rubber goods**: cold water, dry, powder, store flat. Never shake linen.

## Comfort, sleep, pain (Unit 6)

- **Beds**: closed (awaiting admission), open (ambulant), occupied, post-operative (fan-fold, vomit protection, emergency tray), cardiac (sitting up, cardiac table), fracture (fracture boards, cradle), amputation (divided covers, tourniquet ready).
- **Positions**: Fowler's low 15–30°, semi 30–45°, high 60–90°; orthopnoeic; Sims' (left side, right knee up — enema); dorsal recumbent; lithotomy; knee–chest; Trendelenburg (head low).
- **Sleep**: N1, N2, N3 (deep), REM; ~90-minute cycles; melatonin; insomnia, narcolepsy, sleep apnoea, parasomnias.
- **Pain**: whatever the patient says it is (McCaffery); PQRST; NRS 0–10, VAS, Wong–Baker FACES, FLACC; Pasero sedation scale for opioids.
- **Gate control theory**: Melzack and Wall 1965. **WHO ladder**: non-opioid → weak opioid → strong opioid ± adjuvants.
- **PCA**: lockout interval; only the patient presses. **TENS**: not over the front of the neck or with a pacemaker.
- **Rights**: right patient, drug, dose, route, time, documentation (+ reason, response, refusal). Doses only as prescribed.

## Admission, mobility, teaching (Unit 7)

- **Admission**: welcome, ID band, orient, baseline vital signs and weight, history, valuables signed, inform doctor, record.
- **Discharge planning starts at admission**: METHOD (Medications, Environment, Treatment, Health teaching, Outpatient follow-up, Diet).
- **LAMA**: inform doctor, explain risks, signed form. **Abscond**: search, inform, record.
- **Body mechanics**: wide base, low centre of gravity, load close, legs not back, pivot not twist, push not lift.
- **Immobility**: pressure ulcers, contractures, osteoporosis, DVT, orthostatic hypotension, hypostatic pneumonia, constipation, UTI/stones, depression.
- Wheelchair at 45° on the strong side, brakes on; walk on the weak side; cane in the strong hand; crutches — weight on hands, not axillae; up with the good, down with the bad.
- **Cast**: palms, elevate, 5 Ps (pain, pallor, pulselessness, paraesthesia, paralysis); unrelieved pain = report (compartment syndrome).
- **Teaching**: cognitive, affective, psychomotor; return demonstration; teach-back.

## First aid (Unit 8)

- **Aims**: preserve life, prevent worsening, promote recovery. **DRSABCD**. Call **112 / 108**.
- **Bleeding**: direct pressure ≥ 10 min, elevate, add pads; tourniquet only for life-threatening limb bleeding (note time). Nosebleed: lean forward, pinch.
- **Shock**: lay flat, raise legs, warm, nothing by mouth.
- **Fracture**: immobilise joint above and below. **Sprain**: RICE. **Dislocation**: don't reduce.
- **Choking**: adult 5 back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts; infant back blows + chest thrusts.
- **CPR (adult)**: 100–120/min, 5–6 cm, full recoil, 30:2, AED early. Child 5 cm, infant 4 cm; two rescuers 15:2.
- **Unconscious**: AVPU, GCS 3–15; recovery position if breathing. **FAST** for stroke. Fits: don't restrain, nothing in the mouth.
- **Burns**: cool running water ~20 min; no ice/butter/toothpaste; don't burst blisters. Rule of nines: head 9, arm 9 each, front 18, back 18, leg 18 each, perineum 1. Palm ≈ 1%.
- **Poisoning**: don't induce vomiting; keep the container. **Snake bite** (Big Four: cobra, krait, Russell's viper, saw-scaled viper): immobilise, no cut/suck/tight tourniquet, hospital. **Dog bite**: soap + running water ~15 min, anti-rabies vaccine.
- **Frostbite**: warm gently, never rub. **Heat stroke**: cool fast, ambulance.
- **Triage**: red immediate, yellow delayed, green minor, black dead/expectant. NDMA under the DM Act 2005.
