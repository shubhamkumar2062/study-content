## Pathology: must-know comparisons

**Nephritic vs nephrotic syndrome**

| Feature | Nephritic | Nephrotic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proteinuria | Mild | Massive (>3.5 g/day) |
| Haematuria | Prominent (smoky urine) | Absent or mild |
| Oedema | Periorbital, moderate | Generalised, severe |
| Hypertension | Yes | May be present |
| Serum albumin | Near normal | Very low |
| Serum lipids | Normal | High |
| Classic cause | Post-streptococcal GN | Minimal change (children), membranous GN (adults) |

**Acute kidney injury (AKI) vs chronic kidney disease (CKD)**

| Feature | AKI | CKD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onset | Days to weeks | Months to years |
| Kidney size | Normal or enlarged | Shrunken, small |
| Anaemia | Mild or absent | Severe (↓ erythropoietin) |
| Bone disease | Absent | Renal osteodystrophy |
| Reversibility | Usually reversible | Irreversible |
| Casts | Granular (ATN), RBC (GN) | Waxy, broad casts |

## Body cavity fluids

**Transudate vs exudate**

| Feature | Transudate | Exudate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specific gravity | <1.012 | >1.020 |
| Protein | <3 g/dL | >3 g/dL |
| Appearance | Clear | Cloudy |
| Causes | Heart failure, nephrotic, cirrhosis | Infection, TB, malignancy, pancreatitis |

**Light's criteria:** Exudate if any one: (1) Fluid/serum protein ratio >0.5; (2) Fluid/serum LDH ratio >0.6; (3) Fluid LDH > ⅔ upper normal serum LDH.

**SAAG (ascites):** ≥1.1 g/dL → portal hypertension (cirrhosis); <1.1 g/dL → TB, malignancy.

## Mendelian inheritance — comparison

| Pattern | % risk per child | Sex affected | Parent status | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Autosomal dominant (AD) | 50% | M and F equally | One parent affected | Huntington, Marfan, NF1 |
| Autosomal recessive (AR) | 25% affected | M and F equally | Both parents carriers | CF, sickle cell, PKU, thalassaemia |
| X-linked recessive (XLR) | 50% of sons | Males predominantly | Carrier mother | Haemophilia A, DMD, G6PD deficiency |
| Mitochondrial | All children of affected mother | M and F (from mother only) | Mother affected | MELAS, MERRF, LHON |

## Cancer genetics

| Gene / mechanism | Syndrome / cancer |
| --- | --- |
| TP53 (TSG) | Li-Fraumeni syndrome; most commonly mutated gene in cancer |
| BRCA1 / BRCA2 (TSG) | Hereditary breast and ovarian cancer |
| APC (TSG) | Familial adenomatous polyposis → colorectal cancer |
| RB1 (TSG) | Retinoblastoma (prototype two-hit model) |
| KRAS (oncogene) | Pancreatic (95%), colorectal, lung cancer |
| BCR-ABL, t(9;22) | Philadelphia chromosome → CML; treated with imatinib |
| MYC, t(8;14) | Burkitt lymphoma |

**Knudson's two-hit hypothesis:** TSG cancer requires mutation of both alleles; in hereditary cancer, the first hit is inherited in every cell, so the second somatic hit is enough to cause cancer (earlier onset).

## Genetic counselling — key points

- **Non-directive:** Counsellor presents facts; client makes their own decision.
- **Process:** Multiple sessions; not a single event.
- **Steps:** Confirm diagnosis → draw pedigree → calculate risk → discuss options → support decision.
- **PCPNDT Act (India):** Foetal sex determination for sex selection is illegal; mandatory registration of prenatal diagnostic centres.
- **MTP Act (amended 2021):** Termination up to 24 weeks (specific categories); no upper limit for foetal conditions incompatible with life (medical board required).
- **Right not to know:** Must be respected for predictive testing (Huntington's, BRCA).
