## Contraception — key facts

**OCP mechanism:** Inhibit LH/FSH → no ovulation; thicken cervical mucus → sperm blocked; thin endometrium.

**OCP absolute contraindications:** Age > 35 AND smoker; migraine WITH aura; personal history of DVT/PE; oestrogen-dependent cancer; active liver disease; breastfeeding in first 6 weeks.

**Missed OCP:** > 12 hours late → take missed pill + barrier method for 7 days.

| Method | Key point |
| --- | --- |
| COCP | Oestrogen + progestogen; CI: smoking/migraine/DVT |
| POP (minipill) | Progestogen only; 3-hour window; safe if oestrogen CI |
| Emergency — levonorgestrel | Within 72 hours; 1.5 mg oral |
| Emergency — ulipristal | Within 120 hours; 30 mg oral |
| Emergency — copper IUD | Within 120 hours; most effective |
| DMPA injection | 150 mg IM every 3 months |
| Implant | Etonogestrel; 3 years; immediate efficacy |

## Magnesium sulfate (pre-eclampsia/eclampsia)

- **Loading:** 4 g IV over 15–20 min
- **Maintenance:** 1–2 g/h IV infusion
- **Toxicity monitoring — HOURLY:** patellar reflex (lost first) → RR < 12/min → urine < 25 mL/h
- **Antidote at the bedside:** calcium gluconate 10 mL 10% IV slowly

## Emergency drugs (mnemonic: AADAN)

- **A**drenaline — anaphylaxis 0.5 mg IM outer thigh; cardiac arrest 1 mg IV
- **A**tropine — bradycardia; organophosphate poisoning (large IV doses)
- **D**igoxin toxicity signs — nausea, yellow-green visual haloes, bradycardia; antidote: Digibind (Fab)
- **A**denosine — SVT conversion; 6 mg IV rapid bolus + 20 mL saline flush
- **N**oradrenaline / dopamine / dobutamine — vasopressor/inotropes in shock

## Vaccines

**Live attenuated (CI in pregnancy/immunocompromise):** BCG, MMR, OPV, Rotavirus, Varicella

**Inactivated:** IPV, Hepatitis A, Influenza

**Toxoid:** Tetanus (TT), Diphtheria (DT/Td)

**Subunit/conjugate:** Hepatitis B, HPV, Hib, PCV, Meningococcal

**Cold chain:** 2–8°C for most; OPV at −20°C.

## Anticancer drug toxicities

| Drug | Specific toxicity | Management |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cyclophosphamide | Haemorrhagic cystitis | MESNA + hydration |
| Methotrexate | Mucositis, hepatotoxicity | Leucovorin rescue |
| Doxorubicin | Cardiomyopathy (cumulative) | Echo monitoring; dose limit |
| Bleomycin | Pulmonary fibrosis | PFTs; minimise oxygen |
| Vincristine | Peripheral neuropathy | No IM injections; never intrathecal |
| Cisplatin | Nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity | Aggressive IV hydration |

**Shared toxicities:** Bone marrow suppression, nausea, alopecia, mucositis, gonadal toxicity.

**Febrile neutropenia (ANC < 500 + fever ≥ 38°C):** Emergency — blood cultures then IV antibiotics within 1 hour.

**Vesicant extravasation:** STOP infusion; aspirate; cold pack (most); antidote if available; document and photograph.
