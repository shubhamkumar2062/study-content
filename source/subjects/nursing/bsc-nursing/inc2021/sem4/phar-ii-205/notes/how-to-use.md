# How to use this guide

PHAR(II)205 Applied Pharmacology II is a Semester 4 subject of the INC 2021 B.Sc Nursing curriculum. It directly continues PHAR(I)205 from Semester 3, completing the full pharmacology course. This semester covers the remaining INC drug groups — reproductive, emergency, immunological and nutritional drugs — and concludes with the fundamental principles of prescribing that govern the nurse's professional practice.

## Exam pattern

PHAR(II)205 is **not examined in a standalone paper**. It is examined as **Section A of a combined 3-hour, 75-mark university paper** shared with Pathology II and Genetics (PATH(II)210):

| Section | Subject | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Pharmacology II (PHAR(II)205) | 38 |
| B | Pathology (Part I + Part II) | 25 |
| C | Genetics | 12 |
| **Total** | | **75** |

Confirm the exact format with your university — INC Regulations 2020, Appendix 3 sets the framework but universities print papers in slightly different styles.

**Section A pharmacology question pattern** (approximate, based on a 38-mark allocation):

| Part | Type | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 long essays (from 3 given; answer any 2) | 10 marks each | 20 |
| Short answers (from about 9 given; answer any 7) | 5 marks each | 35 short — but this section only 18 of 35 goes to pharmacology |
| MCQs / very short answers | 1–2 marks each | Remaining |

Confirm with your college; INC examinations vary in Section C split.

## Scoring tips

1. **Begin every answer with the drug class and mechanism before listing drug names.** Examiners reward organised pharmacological thinking.
2. **End every answer with 2–3 nurse's responsibilities.** INC exam questions in pharmacology almost always carry marks for nursing application.
3. **Tables earn marks quickly** — use a table for drug comparisons (oxytocin vs misoprostol vs ergometrine; OCP vs POP; types of contraception; antidotes).
4. **State adverse effects and contraindications** for every drug group — these are frequently asked.
5. **Use the class → drug → mechanism → uses → adverse effects → nurse frame** as your backbone for any long essay.
6. **Learn doses where they are clinically critical** — adrenaline 0.5 mg IM; MgSO4 4 g loading + 1–2 g/h maintenance; oxytocin 10 IU IM for PPH.

## Probability ratings

- **High** — appears in most examination papers in some form
- **Medium** — appears every few papers, or as a component of a larger question
- **Low** — less frequent but useful for MCQs
