# How to use this guide

PROF230 Professionalism, Professional Values and Ethics is a Semester 4 subject of the INC 2021 B.Sc Nursing curriculum. It builds on the professional orientation begun in earlier semesters and deepens the student's capacity for ethical reasoning in complex clinical situations. The subject prepares nurses to practice with integrity, to handle morally difficult situations, and to understand the rights of patients and the obligations of nurses.

## Exam pattern

PROF230 follows the standard INC 75-mark end-semester university paper format:

| Part | Type | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Section A | 20 MCQs | 20 × 1 = 20 |
| Section B | Long essays — answer any 2 of 3 | 2 × 10 = 20 |
| Section C | Short answers — answer any 7 of 9 | 7 × 5 = 35 |
| **Total** | | **75** |

Confirm the exact format and any internal choice variations with your college.

## How the units are arranged

The four unit files cover the full INC 2021 syllabus for PROF230:

| Notes file | Topics covered |
| --- | --- |
| Unit 1 | Professionalism and professional development; regulatory bodies (INC, State Nursing Councils); legal aspects of nursing (INC Act, State Nursing Acts, Consumer Protection Act, various laws affecting nursing practice) |
| Unit 2 | Professional values in nursing (caring, commitment, accountability, integrity, advocacy, self-determination); professional socialisation; professional image and appearance; continuing nursing education |
| Unit 3 | Ethics and bioethics — definitions; philosophical theories (utilitarianism, deontology, virtue ethics, care ethics); bioethics principles (autonomy, non-maleficence, beneficence, justice); additional principles (fidelity, veracity, confidentiality); professional codes of ethics (ICN, INC, ANA) |
| Unit 4 | Ethical issues in practice — informed consent, refusal of treatment, advance directives, DNR; end-of-life decisions (euthanasia — Indian law); confidentiality dilemmas (HIV, POCSO); resource allocation; research ethics; Mental Healthcare Act 2017; privacy and dignity; whistleblowing; MORAL model; hospital ethics committees |

## Scoring tips

1. **Define every term first.** Examiners of ethics subjects expect precise definitions: "Ethics is…", "Autonomy is…", "Informed consent is…". One well-phrased definition at the start of an answer earns opening marks.
2. **Apply principles to the scenario.** Many exam questions present a clinical situation. Name the relevant principles (autonomy, beneficence etc.) by name, explain how they apply, and state what the nurse should do.
3. **Use the four-principles framework as your backbone** for any ethics long essay — discuss each principle in turn, and note where they conflict in the scenario.
4. **Cite relevant Indian laws** where asked. PROF230 examinations frequently test: Mental Healthcare Act 2017, POCSO Act 2012, Common Cause judgment 2018, Transplantation of Human Organs Act, Consumer Protection Act. Know the year of each.
5. **Mention the ICN Code of Ethics by name** and name the four elements when asked about professional codes.
6. **For short answers (5 marks):** Give a one-line definition, then 3–4 bullet points of content. No more than half a page.
7. **For MCQs:** Know the exact wording of principles, the names of philosophers (Kant, Bentham, Mill, Aristotle, Gilligan), and the Indian legislation years.

## Probability ratings

- **High** — appears in most examination papers in some form
- **Medium** — appears every few papers, or as a component of a larger question
- **Low** — less frequent but useful for MCQs
