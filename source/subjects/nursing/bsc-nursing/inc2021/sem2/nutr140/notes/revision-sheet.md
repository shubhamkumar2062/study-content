# Last-night revision sheet — Applied Nutrition and Dietetics

## 1. Basics
- **Nutrition**: science of food and its relation to health. **Nutrient**: chemical in food needed for energy, growth, repair, regulation.
- **Malnutrition** = under-nutrition, over-nutrition, imbalance, specific deficiency. India has a **double burden**.
- **Macronutrients**: carbohydrate, protein, fat. **Micronutrients**: vitamins, minerals. **Organic**: CHO, protein, fat, vitamins; **inorganic**: minerals, water.
- Foods by function: **energy-giving, body-building, protective**. Basic four groups: cereals; protein foods; vegetables and fruits; fats, oils, sugar.

## 2. Energy and macronutrients (ICMR-NIN 2020)
- kcal/g: carbohydrate **4**, protein **4**, fat **9**, alcohol **7**. 1 kcal = 4.184 kJ.
- Energy: man **2110/2710/3470**, woman **1660/2130/2720** (sedentary/moderate/heavy); pregnancy **+350**; lactation **+600** (0–6 m), **+520** (7–12 m).
- **BMR**: energy at complete rest; ↑ thyroxine, fever (~13% per °C), growth, pregnancy, cold, lean mass; ↓ starvation, age, hypothyroidism, sleep.
- Protein RDA **0.83 g/kg/day** (EAR 0.66): man **54 g**, woman **45.7 g**; pregnancy **+9.5 g** (2nd), **+22 g** (3rd trimester); lactation +16.9 g then +13.2 g.
- **Essential amino acids (8)**: isoleucine, leucine, lysine, methionine, phenylalanine, threonine, tryptophan, valine (+ histidine in infants). Cereals lack **lysine**, pulses lack **methionine** → combine. Cereal : legume : milk ≈ 3 : 1 : 2.5.
- Visible fat **20–50 g/day**; EFAs = **linoleic (n-6)** and **alpha-linolenic (n-3)**; trans fats most harmful.

## 4. Balanced diet and life cycle
- **Balanced diet**: variety of foods meeting all needs + small safety margin. Principles: adequacy, balance, variety, moderation, calorie control, nutrient density, individualisation.
- **RDA** covers nearly all healthy people; limitations: healthy only, reference weights, not individual need. **EAR** for assessing groups.
- **Food exchange**: interchangeable portions (cereal 30 g ≈ 100 kcal, 3 g protein; pulse 30 g ≈ 100 kcal, 6 g protein).
- Nutritive value = **(nutrient per 100 g × amount) ÷ 100** (IFCT).
- **IYCF**: breastfeed **within 1 hour**, **exclusive 6 months**, complementary **from 6 months**, continue **2 years+**.
- **Anaemia in pregnancy**: Hb **< 11 g/dL** (severe < 7). Iron-rich foods + vitamin C, no tea with meals; IFA under AMB (counsel: after food, black stools harmless, keep away from children).
- Elderly: fewer calories, same protein, soft food, fluids, fibre.

## 5. Deficiency disorders
- WHO: **wasting** WFH < −2 SD (acute); **stunting** HFA < −2 SD (chronic); **underweight** WFA < −2 SD. NFHS-5: 35.5% / 19.3% / 32.1%.
- **SAM** (6–59 m): WFH < −3 SD **or** MUAC < **11.5 cm** **or** bilateral pitting oedema. MAM: MUAC 11.5–12.5 cm.
- **Kwashiorkor**: oedema, flaky paint skin, fatty liver, moon face, apathy. **Marasmus**: severe wasting, no oedema, old-man face, alert, hungry.
- **Ten steps**: hypoglycaemia, hypothermia, dehydration (ReSoMal), electrolytes, infection, micronutrients (iron later), cautious feeding (F-75), catch-up (F-100/RUTF), stimulation, follow-up.
- **Xerophthalmia**: XN night blindness → X1A → X1B Bitot's → X2 → X3A/X3B keratomalacia → XS → XF.
- Childhood obesity: BMI-for-age > +2 SD (5–19 y).

## 6. Therapeutic diets
- Modified normal diet — **consistency** (clear liquid → full liquid → soft → normal), **nutrients**, **meal pattern**.
- **If the gut works, use it**: oral → enteral (NG, gastrostomy) → parenteral.
- **NG feeding**: upright, **confirm tube position every time**, slow feed, flush before and after, upright 30–60 min after.
- **Obesity**: −500 kcal/day, high fibre, low fat, exercise; Indian BMI obese ≥ 25, waist ≥ 90 (M) / 80 (F).
- **Diabetes**: complex, low-GI carbohydrate, high fibre, no sugar, regular meals timed with medicines.
- **CVD**: less saturated/trans fat, salt < 5 g, DASH pattern, high fibre.
- **CKD**: limited protein (more if on dialysis), restrict Na, K (leaching), P, fluid (urine + ~500 ml).
- **Liver**: hepatitis — high CHO, adequate protein; cirrhosis — adequate protein, late evening snack, low salt for ascites, no alcohol.
- **Diarrhoea**: ORS, continue feeding/breastfeeding, zinc (children), extra meal after recovery. **Constipation**: fibre + fluids + activity.
- **Post-op**: early feeding; clear → full → soft → normal; high protein, vitamin C, zinc.

## 7. Cookery and food safety
- Pressure cooking/steaming save nutrients. Wash before cutting; big pieces; minimum water; cover; no soda; don't discard cooking water; don't overcook or reheat.
- Germination ↑ vitamin C; fermentation ↑ B vitamins; parboiling saves thiamine.
- Danger zone **5–60 °C**; cooked food not over 2 h at room temperature. Pasteurisation: 63 °C/30 min or 72 °C/15 s.
- Adulteration: metanil yellow (HCl → pink); papaya seeds in pepper (float in spirit); argemone → **epidemic dropsy**; khesari → **lathyrism**; starch in milk (iodine → blue).
- **PFA Act 1954 → FSSA 2006 (in force 2011), FSSAI**. AGMARK, ISI (BIS), "+F" logo.
- **WHO five keys**: keep clean; separate raw and cooked; cook thoroughly; safe temperatures; safe water and raw materials.

## 8. Assessment and programmes
- **ABCD**: anthropometry, biochemical, clinical, dietary (24-h recall, **FFQ**, diary, weighment).
- MUAC: midpoint of left upper arm. BMI = kg/m²; Indian: 18.5–22.9 normal, 23–24.9 overweight, ≥ 25 obese.
- Nutrition education: know the people, simple local messages, demonstration, participation, evaluation.
- **National Nutrition Policy 1993**; NFSA 2013; **POSHAN Abhiyaan 2018** (Poshan 2.0).
- **ICDS** (2 Oct 1975, MWCD): 6 services — supplementary nutrition, pre-school education, nutrition and health education, immunisation, health check-up, referral.
- **AMB** (2018): 6 beneficiary groups × 6 interventions × 6 institutional mechanisms.
- **WIFS** (2012): adolescents 10–19, weekly supervised IFA, biannual deworming.
- **MDM** (1995) → **PM POSHAN** (2021): hot cooked meal, government and aided schools.
- **NGCP 1962 → NIDDCP 1992**: universal iodised salt, monitoring, education.
- **Vitamin A**: 9 months–5 years, with MR vaccine then every 6 months (dose per NHM schedule).
- Nurse's role: identify, assess, deliver, educate, coordinate, record, evaluate.
