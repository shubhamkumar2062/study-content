# Last-night revision sheet

If you have only an hour: write out the **steps of NG tube feeding, catheterisation, enema and IM injection with reasons** once each, do the drip-rate and dose-calculation formulas twice, draw the GCS table and the Rule of Nines, and then read the tables below.

**Assessment and nursing process**

| Item | Key fact |
| --- | --- |
| Examination methods | Inspection, palpation, percussion, auscultation, olfaction; abdomen: inspect → auscultate → percuss → palpate |
| Percussion notes | Resonant lung; hyper-resonant emphysema; tympanic stomach; dull liver; flat muscle |
| Stethoscope | Diaphragm high-pitched; bell low-pitched |
| Apex beat | Left 5th intercostal space, midclavicular line |
| Nursing process | Assessment → Diagnosis → Planning → Implementation → Evaluation (ADPIE), cyclic |
| Diagnosis | PES: Problem, Etiology ("related to"), Signs ("as evidenced by"); types: problem-focused, risk, health promotion, syndrome |
| Goals | SMART; short-term and long-term |
| Interventions | Independent, dependent, collaborative; care direct and indirect |
| Theories | Nightingale environment; Orem self-care deficit (wholly compensatory, partly compensatory, supportive-educative); Roy adaptation (physiological, self-concept, role function, interdependence) |

**Basic needs and procedures**

| Topic | Must-know |
| --- | --- |
| BMI (Asian) | 18.5–22.9 normal, 23–24.9 overweight, ≥ 25 obese |
| NG tube | Length nose–earlobe–xiphisternum; confirm by aspirate pH ≤ 5.5 or X-ray; feed upright 30–45°, flush before and after |
| TPN | Central line, asepsis, pump, monitor glucose, do not stop suddenly |
| Pressure ulcer | Stages 1–4 + unstageable + deep tissue; Braden 6–23 (≤ 18 risk); Norton 5–20 (≤ 14 risk); turn 2-hourly |
| Perineal care | Front to back; replace foreskin |
| Urine | 1–2 L/day; oliguria < 400 mL; anuria < 100 mL; SG 1.005–1.030 |
| Catheter | Asepsis; female 5–7.5 cm, male 17–22 cm; inflate balloon after urine flows; bag below bladder; closed system |
| Enema | Left lateral; ~40 °C; can 30–45 cm high; tube 7.5–10 cm |
| Stoma | Pink-red and moist = healthy; flange 2–3 mm larger |
| Lab values | Hb M 13–17, F 12–15 g/dL; WBC 4,000–11,000; platelets 1.5–4.5 lakh; FBS 70–99 mg/dL; HbA1c ≥ 6.5% diabetes |
| Specimens | Midstream sterile; catheter sample from port; 24-h urine — discard first, keep last; sputum early morning before antibiotics |

**Oxygen and fluids**

| Topic | Must-know |
| --- | --- |
| SpO2 | 95–100% normal; accuracy falls with poor circulation, movement, nail polish, CO poisoning |
| Oxygen devices | Cannula 1–6 L/min; simple mask ≥ 5 L/min; Venturi fixed %; non-rebreather 10–15 L/min |
| Oxygen safety | Prescribed, humidified, no smoking or flames, no oil/grease |
| Suction | Only on withdrawal, 10–15 s per pass, pre-oxygenate |
| Chest drain | Bottle below chest, tube under water, tidalling, do not clamp routinely |
| Electrolytes | Na 135–145; K 3.5–5.0 mEq/L; pH 7.35–7.45; PaCO2 35–45; HCO3 22–26 |
| ROME | Respiratory opposite, Metabolic equal |
| IV fluids | Isotonic NS, RL; hypotonic 0.45% NaCl; hypertonic 3% NaCl, D10, DNS |
| Drip rate | drops/min = volume × drop factor ÷ minutes; mL/h = volume ÷ hours |
| Air embolism | Clamp, left side head down, oxygen |
| Blood | Two-person check; NS only; slow for 15 min and stay; reaction → stop, keep vein open with NS through new set, inform |

**Medicines**

```text
Amount to give = (Desired ÷ Have) × Quantity    (same units first)
1 g = 1,000 mg; 1 mg = 1,000 µg; 1 L = 1,000 mL; 1% = 1 g in 100 mL
Example (invented): want 150 mg, have 250 mg in 5 mL → 150/250 × 5 = 3 mL
```

- 10 rights: patient, drug, dose, route, time, documentation, education, refusal, assessment, evaluation; three label checks.
- Angles: ID 10–15°, SC 45°, IM 90°; ventrogluteal preferred, vastus lateralis infants, deltoid small volumes, avoid dorsogluteal (sciatic nerve).
- Z-track for irritant IM drugs. Never recap. White container for sharps; red for contaminated plastics; yellow for soiled and anatomical waste; blue for glass.
- Needle-stick: bleed freely, wash with soap and water, report, PEP ideally within 2 h, not after 72 h.
- Eye drops — lower sac; ear drops — adult pinna up and back, child < 3 down and back.
- Controlled drugs double-locked with a register; vaccines and insulin 2–8 °C.

**Sensory, dying, psychosocial**

| Topic | Must-know |
| --- | --- |
| Sensory | Reception → perception → reaction; deficit, deprivation, overload, poverty |
| GCS | E4 V5 M6 = 15; ≤ 8 coma; fall of 2 = urgent |
| Unconscious care | Lateral position, nothing by mouth, eyes protected, turn 2-hourly, talk to patient |
| Kübler-Ross | Denial, anger, bargaining, depression, acceptance |
| Rando | Recognise, react, recollect, relinquish, readjust, reinvest |
| After death | Algor (cooling), livor (pooling), rigor (stiffening) — last office before rigor |
| Euthanasia | Active illegal in India; passive and living wills only under Supreme Court guidelines (2011, 2018) |
| Self-concept | Personal identity, body image, role performance, self-esteem |
| Stress (Selye) | GAS: alarm → resistance → exhaustion; LAS: reflex pain, inflammation |
| Culture | Leininger transcultural nursing (Sunrise model); acculturation vs assimilation |

**First aid**

| Emergency | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Collapse | DR CAB; CPR 100–120/min, 5–6 cm, 30:2 or hands-only; AED |
| Choking adult | 5 back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts; infant 5 back blows + 5 chest thrusts |
| Bleeding | Firm direct pressure; tourniquet only for life-threatening limb bleeding |
| Shock | Lie flat, legs raised (no injury), warm, nothing by mouth |
| Fracture | Immobilise joints above and below; RICE for sprains |
| Burns | Cool running water ≥ 20 min, cling film, no ice/oil/toothpaste; Rule of Nines (head 9, arm 9, front 18, back 18, leg 18, perineum 1) |
| Poisoning | No vomiting; keep container; hospital |
| Snake bite | Reassure, immobilise, hospital; no tourniquet, cut or suck |
| Dog bite | Soap and running water 15 min; anti-rabies vaccination |
| Heat stroke | Rapid cooling, call 112/108 |
| Triage | Red immediate, yellow delayed, green minor, black dead/expectant |
