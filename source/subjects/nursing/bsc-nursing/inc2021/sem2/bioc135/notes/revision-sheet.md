**Unit by unit**

| Unit | Must-know facts |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Carbohydrates | End products glucose, fructose, galactose; SGLT-1 (glucose + Na — basis of ORS); glycolysis net 2 ATP, no O2 needed; insulin the only glucose-lowering hormone; type 1 autoimmune/absolute deficiency, type 2 insulin resistance; DKA = ketones + acidosis; OGTT: 3 days normal diet, overnight fast, 75 g glucose in 250–300 mL water, samples up to 2 h; lag curve (gastrectomy, hyperthyroid), flat curve (malabsorption, hypothyroid, Addison's), renal glycosuria; mini GTT = fasting + 2 h; GCT = 50 g, non-fasting, 1 h; HbA1c = glycated Hb, 2–3 months' average; hypoglycaemia below 70, Whipple's triad |
| 2 Lipids | MUFA one double bond (oleic); PUFA linoleic, linolenic, arachidonic; essential FA linoleic + α-linolenic (deficiency phrynoderma); trans fat ↑ LDL ↓ HDL; bile salts emulsify, pancreatic lipase digests; chylomicrons in lymph; β-oxidation in mitochondria with carnitine; ketone bodies acetoacetate, β-hydroxybutyrate, acetone (Rothera's misses β-HB); cholesterol → bile acids, steroid hormones, vitamin D3; chylomicron dietary fat, VLDL liver fat, LDL bad, HDL good; LDL = TC − HDL − TG/5; atherosclerosis: foam cells → fatty streak → plaque |
| 3 Proteins | Essential: PVT TIM HALL (arginine, histidine semi-essential); leucine and lysine purely ketogenic; pepsin, trypsin (enteropeptidase), rennin; transamination needs B6; urea cycle only in liver; glycine → haem, creatine; tyrosine → catecholamines, thyroxine, melanin; tryptophan → serotonin, niacin; PKU (mousy odour), alkaptonuria (urine blackens), albinism (tyrosinase); albumin keeps oncotic pressure; Bence Jones in myeloma; electrophoresis pH 8.6 — albumin fastest; M band myeloma; β–γ bridging cirrhosis; α2 ↑ nephrotic |
| 4 Enzymes | Isoenzymes: same reaction, different structure, mobility, tissue, Km, heat stability; CK-MB heart, MM muscle, BB brain; LDH-1 heart (flip after MI), LDH-5 liver; ALT most liver-specific; ALP + GGT ↑↑ obstruction; GGT ↑ alcohol; MI: troponin (best, stays 7–14 days), CK-MB (normal in 2–3 days), AST, LDH (last); CK and aldolase in muscle disease (Duchenne); ALP in rickets, Paget's; PSA and tartrate-labile ACP in prostate cancer |
| 5 Acid–base | pH = −log [H⁺]; buffers (seconds) → lungs (minutes) → kidneys (days); bicarbonate main blood buffer, Hb for CO2, phosphate in urine; HCO3⁻ : H2CO3 = 20 : 1; kidney reabsorbs HCO3⁻, excretes titratable acid and NH4⁺; ROME; met. acidosis — DKA, lactic, renal failure, diarrhoea; met. alkalosis — vomiting, NG aspiration, diuretics; resp. acidosis — COPD, respiratory depression; resp. alkalosis — hyperventilation, altitude |
| 6 Haem, jaundice, function tests | RBC 120 days → haem → biliverdin → bilirubin (on albumin) → liver conjugation (UDP-glucuronyl transferase) → bile → urobilinogen → stercobilin; haemolytic: indirect ↑, no urine bilirubin, urobilinogen ↑; obstructive: direct ↑, urine bilirubin +, urobilinogen absent, clay stool, ALP ↑↑; hepatic: biphasic, ALT/AST ↑↑; Fouchet's (pigments), Hay's (salts), Ehrlich's (urobilinogen); TSH best thyroid screen |
| 7 Immunochemistry | 2 heavy + 2 light chains, disulphide bonds; variable region binds antigen, Fc for effector functions; IgG most, crosses placenta; IgA secretory (milk); IgM pentamer, first response, IgM in newborn = intrauterine infection; IgD B-cell receptor; IgE allergy, worms; ELISA: enzyme + substrate → colour (OD); indirect detects antibody (HIV), sandwich detects antigen (HBsAg); above cut-off = reactive; confirm positives; window period |

**Haem breakdown in one line**

```text
RBC → Hb → haem → (haem oxygenase) biliverdin → (reductase) bilirubin + albumin → liver:
glucuronide (conjugated) → bile → gut bacteria → urobilinogen → stercobilin (stool) / urobilin (urine)
```

**Nursing links to quote**: fluoride tube for glucose; prepare correctly for OGTT and lipid profile; avoid haemolysis (falsely high K⁺, LDH, AST); record time of chest pain for troponin; PSA before rectal examination; ABG — Allen test, heparinised syringe, no air bubbles, pressure 5 minutes; fresh, light-protected urine for bile pigments; phototherapy care in neonatal jaundice; counselling and confidentiality in HIV testing; recognise hypoglycaemia and Kussmaul breathing.
