# How to use this guide

Applied Microbiology and Infection Control including Safety is a third-semester course of the INC 2021 B.Sc Nursing curriculum (2 credits theory, 40 hours; 1 credit practical, 40 hours of lab/experiential learning). It has two halves taught by different teachers but examined in **one 3-hour, 75-mark university paper**:

- **Section A: Applied Microbiology** — 20 hours, **37 marks**.
- **Section B: Infection Control and Safety** — 20 hours, **38 marks**.

Internal assessment adds 25 marks. You must pass theory with at least 50%. Because the two sections are written in one sitting, prepare both — a weak half pulls down the whole paper.

**How the units are arranged.** The INC syllabus has 4 units in Section A and 11 short units in Section B. To keep each notes file a useful size, units are split or joined as follows:

| Notes file | Section | INC unit(s) | Hours (theory) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unit 1 Introduction and general characteristics of microbes | A | I + first half of II | 3 + about 5 |
| Unit 2 Staining, culture media and culture methods | A | Second half of II | about 5 |
| Unit 3 Pathogenic organisms, rodents and vectors | A | III | 4 |
| Unit 4 Immunity, hypersensitivity, serology and immunization | A | IV | 3 |
| Unit 5 HAI and bundles, isolation and PPE, hand hygiene | B | I + II + III | 2 + 3 + 1 |
| Unit 6 Disinfection and sterilization, specimen collection, BMW and laundry, antibiotic stewardship | B | IV + V + VI + VII | 1 + 1 + 2 + 2 |
| Unit 7 Patient safety indicators, incidents, IPSG | B | VIII + IX | 3 + 1 |
| Unit 8 Safety protocols and employee safety | B | X + XI | 2 + 2 |

Units 1–4 are Section A and Units 5–8 are Section B. Study one notes file a day; then answer that unit's questions and MCQs without looking.

**University paper pattern (75 marks, 3 hours)** — as given in INC Regulations 2020, Appendix 3. There is no internal choice: every question must be answered. Your university may print it differently; confirm with your university.

| Section | MCQ | Essay (long) | Short | Very short | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: Applied Microbiology | 6 × 1 = 6 | 1 × 10 = 10 | 3 × 5 = 15 | 3 × 2 = 6 | **37** |
| B: Infection Control including Safety | 7 × 1 = 7 | 1 × 10 = 10 | 3 × 5 = 15 | 3 × 2 = 6 | **38** |
| **Paper total** | 13 | 20 | 30 | 12 | **75** |

The mock test "University paper" follows this pattern. The app draws questions for each section from the whole question bank, so a Section A slot may sometimes show an infection-control question; in the real paper each section keeps to its own half. Use the unit filter to practise one section at a time.

Time plan for the real paper: MCQs 10 minutes, each essay about 30 minutes, each short answer 12–15 minutes, each very short answer 4–5 minutes, and 10 minutes to revise.

**Probability ratings**

- **High** — a favourite; comes up in most papers in some form. Learn the full answer.
- **Medium** — appears every few papers or as part of a bigger question.
- **Low** — less common, but useful for MCQs and very short answers.

These ratings are estimates from syllabus weight and the usual style of B.Sc Nursing microbiology and infection control papers. They are not a promise of what will be asked.

**Scoring tips for this subject**

1. **Start every essay with a definition**, then classification, then points with headings, then a **nursing role** section at the end. Examiners reward structure.
2. **Draw simple labelled diagrams**: bacterial cell, growth curve, streak plate, immunoglobulin, chain of infection, fishbone diagram, 5 moments. Pencil, straight label lines, a title.
3. Use **tables** for comparisons: Gram-positive vs Gram-negative, active vs passive immunity, IgG/IgA/IgM, contact vs droplet vs airborne precautions, sterilization vs disinfection, BMW colours.
4. For organisms, follow one order: **morphology → source and transmission → diseases → laboratory diagnosis → prevention → nursing care**.
5. Learn the numbers examiners like: autoclave 121 °C, 15 lb/in², 15–20 minutes; hot air oven 160 °C for 2 hours; hand rub 20–30 s, hand wash 40–60 s; head of bed 30–45°; cold chain 2–8 °C; untreated BMW not stored beyond 48 hours; HIV PEP within 72 hours (ideally 2), for 28 days; hepatitis B vaccine at 0, 1, 6 months.
6. Keep BMW colour coding exact (yellow, red, white translucent, blue) — it is asked almost every year as an essay, short answer or MCQ.
7. In Section B, answers are about **what the nurse does**. Write action points (verbs first: "check", "label", "report") rather than theory.
8. For very short answers (2 marks), give a one-line definition plus one example or one fact. Don't write a page.
9. MCQs come from small facts; read the "Remember" list of every notes file and the revision sheet the night before.

**Practical (lab/experiential) part**: the INC course includes 40 hours of lab and experiential learning — demonstrations of staining and culture, vaccine storage visits, hand hygiene and PPE re-demonstration, BMW and CSSD visits, mock drills. Practicals are assessed internally; this pack covers the theory paper.
