# How to use this guide

Mental Health Nursing I is a fifth-semester subject of the INC 2021 B.Sc Nursing curriculum. The INC gives it **3 credits (45 hours of theory)**. It introduces the science and practice of psychiatric nursing: how the mind can become ill, how the nurse assesses and communicates therapeutically, the medicines and therapies used, and the nursing management of the major psychiatric disorders you will meet throughout your career.

## Exam pattern

Mental Health Nursing I is examined at the end of Semester 5 as a **75-mark written paper (3 hours)**. The INC Regulations 2020 (Appendix 3) give the following pattern:

| Part | Type | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Part A | 20 multiple-choice questions (1 mark each) | 20 |
| Part B | Long essays — answer **2 from 3** (10 marks each) | 20 |
| Part C | Short answers — answer **7 from 9** (5 marks each) | 35 |
| **Total** | | **75** |

There is **internal choice** in Parts B and C (you answer fewer questions than are set). Read every question before choosing: the one you know best may not be the first one listed.

Confirm the final question-paper format with your university, as individual universities sometimes print the paper differently within the INC framework.

## How the units are arranged

The INC syllabus for Mental Health Nursing I has **seven units**, and each is covered in its own notes file.

| Notes file | INC unit | Content covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit 1 Introduction to mental health nursing | I | Mental health/illness continuum, history of psychiatric care, mental health team, nurse's roles, stigma |
| Unit 2 Principles and concepts of mental health nursing | II | Therapeutic relationship, communication techniques, stigma, mental health promotion, MHCA 2017 |
| Unit 3 Mental health assessment and therapeutic nurse-patient relationship | III | Psychiatric history, MSE (all 9 domains), Peplau's phases, risk assessment |
| Unit 4 Treatment modalities and therapies | IV | Antipsychotics, antidepressants, mood stabilisers, anxiolytics; ECT; milieu therapy; rehabilitation |
| Unit 5 Nursing management of schizophrenia and psychotic disorders | V | Dopamine hypothesis, positive/negative symptoms, subtypes, nursing diagnoses and interventions |
| Unit 6 Nursing management of mood disorders | VI | Depression, Beck's triad, suicide risk, ECT, bipolar disorder, postpartum depression, SAD |
| Unit 7 Nursing management of neurotic, stress-related and somatoform disorders | VII | GAD, panic disorder, phobias, OCD, PTSD, somatoform and dissociative disorders |

Study one notes file per sitting. After reading, draw the main diagrams (the dopamine pathway, Beck's triad, MSE domains) from memory, then answer that unit's questions and MCQs without looking back.

## Probability ratings

- **High** — a favourite question; comes up in most papers in some form. Learn the full answer with a structure.
- **Medium** — appears every few papers, or as part of a larger question.
- **Low** — worth knowing for MCQs and short answers.

These are estimates from syllabus weightings and common nursing exam patterns. They are a guide, not a guarantee.

## Scoring tips for this subject

1. **Start every long-essay answer with a one-line definition.** Examiners reward a crisp, accurate definition before you expand.
2. **Use a consistent answer structure for nursing-management questions:** definition → aetiology → clinical features (table) → assessment (MSE) → medical management (drugs with class and side effects) → nursing diagnoses → nursing interventions → health education → summary. This frame fills 10 marks without having to invent a structure under pressure.
3. **For drug questions, always give four things:** class, mechanism, example drugs, and two nursing points (monitoring, patient teaching). Never just list drug names.
4. **Tables are your friend.** Examiners find them easy to mark. Use tables for comparisons (positive vs negative symptoms, therapeutic vs non-therapeutic communication, mania vs depression), drug comparisons, MSE domains, and risk assessment features.
5. **Quote the MHCA 2017 by name and year.** Mention specific provisions such as advance directive, involuntary admission by magistrate's order, prohibition of chaining and unmodified ECT. This immediately marks the answer as current.
6. **ECT nursing care is a three-part answer:** before, during and after. Each part must be present. Examiners count the three parts.
7. **In suicide questions, give the assessment framework first (ideation → plan → intent → means), then the nursing interventions.** Show you know how to assess risk before you describe management.
8. **For the MSE**, use the mnemonic **A B S M A T P C I J** to ensure you cover all nine domains. Giving all nine with one-line descriptors is worth more than writing three paragraphs on only three domains.
9. **Short answers (5 marks) need five clear points** — one per mark. Use numbered or bulleted points so the examiner can count them easily.
10. **The night before,** read the "Remember" list at the end of each notes file and complete the Quick MCQ test.
