# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, learn these six topics with full pathway diagrams and energetics: **glycolysis**, **TCA cycle**, **β-oxidation of palmitic acid**, **urea cycle**, **ETC with inhibitors and uncouplers**, and **enzyme inhibition with Lineweaver–Burk plots**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

## Pathways at a glance

| Pathway | Site | Rate-limiting enzyme | Key points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Glycolysis | Cytosol | Phosphofructokinase-1 | Irreversible: hexokinase, PFK-1, pyruvate kinase; fluoride inhibits enolase |
| PDH | Mitochondria | PDH complex | TPP, lipoic acid, CoA, FAD, NAD+ |
| TCA cycle | Mitochondrial matrix | Isocitrate dehydrogenase | 3 NADH, 1 FADH2, 1 GTP per turn; amphibolic; malonate inhibits SDH |
| HMP shunt | Cytosol | G6PD | NADPH + ribose-5-P, no ATP |
| Glycogenesis / glycogenolysis | Cytosol | Glycogen synthase / phosphorylase | cAMP–PKA switches to breakdown |
| Gluconeogenesis | Mitochondria + cytosol | Pyruvate carboxylase, PEPCK, F-1,6-bisphosphatase, G6Pase | 6 ATP equivalents per glucose |
| β-Oxidation | Mitochondria | Carnitine acyltransferase I | Malonyl-CoA inhibits CPT-I |

## ETC

Complex I (rotenone, amytal) → CoQ → Complex III (antimycin A) → cyt c → Complex IV (cyanide, CO, azide) → O2. ATP synthase: oligomycin. Translocase: atractyloside. **Uncouplers**: 2,4-DNP, thermogenin, high-dose salicylate → O2 used, no ATP, heat. NADH → O2 ≈ −220 kJ/mol.

## Bioenergetics

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS; ΔG°′ = −2.303 RT log K′eq = −nFΔE°′. ATP → ADP: −30.5 kJ/mol (−7.3 kcal). PEP −61.9, 1,3-BPG −49.3, creatine-P −43.1. cAMP: adenylyl cyclase makes, phosphodiesterase breaks (caffeine, theophylline inhibit).

## Amines

- Tyrosine → (tyrosine hydroxylase) DOPA → (decarboxylase) dopamine → (β-hydroxylase) noradrenaline → (PNMT, SAM) adrenaline; urinary VMA.
- Tryptophan → 5-HTP → serotonin → N-acetylserotonin → melatonin; urinary 5-HIAA.
- Histidine → histamine; glutamate → GABA.

## Nucleic acids

- DNA: antiparallel, right-handed, A=T (2), G≡C (3), 10 bp and 3.4 nm per turn.
- Replication semiconservative (Meselson–Stahl); helicase, primase, DNA polymerase (5′→3′), ligase; Okazaki fragments.
- Transcription: RNA pol II → mRNA; cap, poly-A, splicing.
- Code: 64 codons, AUG start, UAA/UAG/UGA stop, degenerate, wobble.
- Inhibitors: rifampicin (RNA pol), actinomycin D; aminoglycosides, tetracycline (30S); chloramphenicol, macrolides (50S); diphtheria toxin (eEF-2).
- Gout: urate crystals; allopurinol, febuxostat, probenecid, colchicine.

## Enzymes

- v = Vmax[S]/(Km + [S]); Km = [S] at Vmax/2.
- 1/v = (Km/Vmax)(1/[S]) + 1/Vmax.

| Inhibition | Km | Vmax | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Competitive | ↑ | Same | Statins, methotrexate, malonate |
| Non-competitive | Same | ↓ | Hg2+, Pb2+ |
| Uncompetitive | ↓ | ↓ | Phenylalanine on placental ALP |
| Irreversible | — | ↓ | Organophosphates, aspirin |

- IUB: oxidoreductase, transferase, hydrolase, lyase, isomerase, ligase.
- Isoenzymes: LDH-1 > LDH-2 in MI; CK-MB in MI.
- Therapeutic: streptokinase, alteplase, L-asparaginase, hyaluronidase, pancreatin, dornase alfa.
- Coenzymes: B1 TPP, B2 FAD, B3 NAD, B5 CoA, B6 PLP, B7 biotin, B9 THF, B12 cobalamin.

## Numbers (about; ranges vary by laboratory)

Fasting glucose 70–100 mg/dl; diabetes FPG ≥ 126, 2-h ≥ 200, HbA1c ≥ 6.5%; renal threshold for glucose about 180 mg/dl; bilirubin 0.2–1.0 mg/dl, jaundice visible above about 2 mg/dl; uric acid about 3–7 mg/dl; total cholesterol desirable below 200 mg/dl; serum creatinine about 0.6–1.2 mg/dl; red cell life about 120 days.
