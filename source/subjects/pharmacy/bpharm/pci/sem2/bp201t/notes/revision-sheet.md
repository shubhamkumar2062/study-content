**One-line facts**

- Myelin: oligodendrocytes in CNS, Schwann cells in PNS; saltatory conduction at nodes of Ranvier.
- Glutamate excites, GABA inhibits the brain; glycine inhibits the spinal cord.
- CSF flow: lateral ventricles → foramen of Monro → third ventricle → aqueduct → fourth ventricle → Luschka and Magendie → subarachnoid space → arachnoid villi.
- Medulla: cardiac, vasomotor and respiratory centres; pyramids cross here.
- Cerebellar lesion: ataxia, intention tremor, past-pointing (same side).
- Pain and temperature: lateral spinothalamic (cross in cord); fine touch and position: dorsal column (cross in medulla).
- UMN lesion: spastic, brisk reflexes, Babinski positive; LMN lesion: flaccid, wasting, reflexes lost.
- Parietal cells: HCl + intrinsic factor; proton pump = H+/K+ ATPase; acid driven by ACh (M3), gastrin and histamine (H2).
- Pepsinogen → pepsin by HCl; trypsinogen → trypsin by enterokinase.
- B12 and bile salts absorbed in terminal ileum; iron and calcium in duodenum.
- Creatine phosphate + ADP ⇌ creatine + ATP (creatine kinase).
- Surfactant from type II cells; CO2 is the strongest stimulus to breathe.
- Bohr effect: CO2 and H+ release O2; Haldane effect: O2 releases CO2.
- ADH → aquaporin-2 (water); aldosterone → Na+ kept, K+ lost.
- RAS: renin (JG cells) → angiotensin I → ACE (lungs) → angiotensin II.
- Second messengers: cAMP, IP3/DAG/Ca2+; insulin uses tyrosine kinase; steroids act on genes.
- GH excess: gigantism (child), acromegaly (adult); ADH lack: diabetes insipidus.
- Carbimazole blocks thyroid peroxidase; iodine deficiency → goitre.
- Low PTH → tetany (Trousseau's and Chvostek's signs).
- Adrenal cortex: glomerulosa (aldosterone), fasciculata (cortisol), reticularis (androgens).
- Cushing's: moon face, buffalo hump; Addison's: hypotension, pigmentation.
- Melatonin rises in darkness; thymus matures T cells.
- Leydig cells → testosterone (LH); Sertoli cells → spermatogenesis (FSH), inhibin.
- Oestrogen → proliferative phase; progesterone → secretory phase; LH surge → ovulation.
- Fertilization in ampulla; cortical reaction blocks polyspermy; hCG keeps the corpus luteum.
- Ferguson reflex: cervical stretch → oxytocin → stronger contractions (positive feedback).
- Down syndrome trisomy 21; Turner 45 XO; Klinefelter 47 XXY.
- Haemophilia and colour blindness: X-linked recessive; sickle cell and thalassaemia: autosomal recessive.
