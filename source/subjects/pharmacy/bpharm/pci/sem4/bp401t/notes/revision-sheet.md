# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, learn these five answers in full: **resolution of racemic mixtures**, **conformations of n-butane and cyclohexane**, **pyrrole (aromaticity, basicity, synthesis, reactions)**, **pyridine (basicity, Hantzsch synthesis, electrophilic vs nucleophilic substitution)** and **Beckmann rearrangement with mechanism**. Then run through the tables below.

**Stereochemistry in ten lines**

| Idea | Key point |
| --- | --- |
| Chiral | Not superimposable on mirror image; no σ plane, no centre i, no Sn axis |
| Specific rotation | [α] = α / (l × c); l in dm, c in g/mL |
| Enantiomers | Mirror images; same physical properties, opposite rotation |
| Diastereomers | Not mirror images; different physical properties; include cis/trans and meso relationships |
| Meso | Stereocentres + internal plane of symmetry → inactive (internal compensation) |
| Racemate | 50:50 enantiomers → inactive (external compensation); ee = (major − minor)/(total) × 100 |
| D/L | Relative, compared with glyceraldehyde (OH right = D) |
| R/S | CIP priority by atomic number; lowest group away; clockwise = R |
| Reactions | SN2 inversion; SN1 racemisation; no bond broken = retention |
| Resolution | Mechanical (Pasteur), biochemical (Penicillium glaucum), diastereomeric salts (brucine, strychnine, quinine / tartaric acid, camphorsulphonic acid), chiral chromatography |
| Asymmetric synthesis | Partial: chiral reagent/auxiliary/catalyst/enzyme (Marckwald, McKenzie). Absolute: circularly polarised light |

**Geometrical and conformational**

- Needs restricted rotation + two different groups on each sp2 carbon. E/Z by CIP; syn/anti for oximes.
- trans: higher mp, more stable, lower dipole. Maleic (cis) → anhydride on heating; fumaric (trans) does not.
- Syn addition (cold KMnO4): maleic → meso-tartaric; fumaric → (±)-tartaric. Anti addition of Br2: cis-2-butene → (±); trans → meso.
- Ethane: staggered lower than eclipsed by about 12 kJ/mol. Butane: anti > gauche (+3.8 kJ/mol) > eclipsed > fully eclipsed.
- Cyclohexane: chair > twist-boat (+23) > boat (+29) > half-chair (+45 kJ/mol). Substituents equatorial (1,3-diaxial strain).
- Biphenyl atropisomers: bulky ortho groups and each ring unsymmetrically substituted.
- Stereospecific: product decided by starting stereoisomer. Stereoselective: one product preferred.

**Aromaticity and basicity**

- Aromaticity: benzene > thiophene > pyrrole > furan (resonance energy about 150 > 122 > 88 > 67 kJ/mol).
- Reactivity to electrophiles: pyrrole > furan > thiophene > benzene > pyridine.
- pKa of conjugate acid: piperidine 11.1 > imidazole 7.0 > isoquinoline 5.4 > pyridine 5.2 > quinoline 4.9 > aniline 4.6 > pyrazole/thiazole 2.5 > pyrimidine 1.3 > oxazole 0.8 > pyrrole (about −3.8).
- Pyridine weaker than piperidine: sp2 lone pair (more s character). Pyrrole almost non-basic: lone pair in the aromatic sextet; protonates on C-2.

**Last checks**

- Stereospecific Beckmann: the group **anti** to OH migrates — use it to assign oxime configuration.
- Clemmensen for acid-stable; Wolff–Kishner for acid-sensitive (furan, pyrrole, acetals).
- Indole is attacked at C-3, but pyrrole at C-2 — a favourite MCQ.
- Thiophene is removed from benzene by shaking with conc. H2SO4; indophenin test (isatin + H2SO4, blue).
- Barbituric acid = diethyl malonate + urea (NaOEt). Caffeine = 1,3,7-trimethylxanthine.
