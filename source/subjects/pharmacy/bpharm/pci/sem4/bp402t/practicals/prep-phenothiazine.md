# Preparation of phenothiazine

**Aim:** To prepare phenothiazine by the fusion of diphenylamine with sulphur, and to report the yield and melting point.

> Quantities are typical for a student batch. Follow your own lab manual and your teacher where they differ. Hydrogen sulphide is evolved: the whole fusion must be done in a fume cupboard.

## Principle

Phenothiazine is a tricyclic system in which two benzene rings are joined by a sulphur atom and a nitrogen atom. It is prepared by heating **diphenylamine with sulphur**, using a trace of **iodine** as catalyst. Sulphur enters both positions ortho to the nitrogen bridge and the central six-membered ring closes, with hydrogen sulphide being evolved.

```text
(C6H5)2NH  +  2 S   --I2, heat-->   phenothiazine  +  H2S
```

The ring formed here is the nucleus of chlorpromazine and the other phenothiazine antipsychotics; the drug is made by alkylating the ring nitrogen of the corresponding 2-chloro compound.

## Requirements

Diphenylamine 10 g, sulphur (powdered) 4 g, iodine 0.1 g, round-bottomed flask 100 ml with air condenser, sand bath or heating mantle, thermometer, toluene or ethanol for recrystallisation, Buchner funnel, melting point apparatus.

## Procedure

1. Mix 10 g of diphenylamine, 4 g of powdered sulphur and about 0.1 g of iodine in a dry 100 ml round-bottomed flask and fit an air condenser.
2. Heat the mixture in a fume cupboard on a sand bath, raising the temperature slowly to about 180 °C. Hydrogen sulphide is given off; lead acetate paper held at the mouth of the condenser turns black.
3. Continue heating at this temperature until the evolution of hydrogen sulphide stops, usually about two hours. The melt turns dark.
4. Allow the melt to cool until it is still warm, then add about 40 ml of toluene and warm to dissolve the product.
5. Filter the hot solution to remove unchanged sulphur and tarry matter.
6. Cool the filtrate in ice; greenish-yellow crystals of phenothiazine separate. Filter under suction and wash with a little cold solvent.
7. Recrystallise from toluene (or ethanol), dry in a desiccator and record the weight and melting point.

**Worked example.** 10 g of diphenylamine (molecular weight 169.22) is 0.0591 mol. One mole gives one mole of phenothiazine (molecular weight 199.27), so the theoretical yield is 0.0591 × 199.27 = 11.78 g. If 7.1 g of dry product is obtained, the yield is (7.1/11.78) × 100 = **60%**.

## Precautions

1. Carry out the fusion only in a fume cupboard; hydrogen sulphide is toxic and has a foul smell.
2. Raise the temperature gradually, since frothing occurs when the gas is evolved.
3. Do not let the melt solidify completely before adding the solvent, or it becomes very difficult to remove from the flask.
4. Filter the hot solution to remove unchanged sulphur, which would otherwise contaminate the crystals.

## Viva questions

1. **What is the role of iodine?** It is the catalyst; it helps sulphur attack the aromatic rings, so the reaction goes at a lower temperature and in a shorter time.
2. **How do you know the reaction is proceeding?** Hydrogen sulphide is evolved, which blackens lead acetate paper; the end of gas evolution signals completion.
3. **Which positions of diphenylamine are attacked?** The two positions ortho to the nitrogen, so that a six-membered central ring containing S and N is formed.
4. **Name three drugs built on this nucleus.** Chlorpromazine, trifluoperazine and thioridazine (promethazine is the antihistaminic member).
5. **Which position of the ring carries the substituent in antipsychotics, and why?** Position 2, with an electron-withdrawing group such as Cl or CF3; it increases potency and makes the molecule asymmetric.
6. **Why does the solid darken on storage?** Phenothiazine oxidises in air and light to coloured sulphoxide and other products, so it is stored in a well-closed, light-resistant container.
