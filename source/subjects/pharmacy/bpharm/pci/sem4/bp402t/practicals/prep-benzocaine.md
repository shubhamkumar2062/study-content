# Preparation of benzocaine

**Aim:** To prepare benzocaine (ethyl p-aminobenzoate) from p-aminobenzoic acid and ethanol, and to report the yield and melting point.

> Quantities are typical for a student batch. Follow your own lab manual and your teacher where they differ. Concentrated sulphuric acid is highly corrosive: add it slowly to the cooled alcohol, never the reverse.

## Principle

Benzocaine is the ethyl ester of p-aminobenzoic acid and is a local anaesthetic of the ester type. It is prepared by **Fischer esterification**: the carboxylic acid is heated with an excess of ethanol in the presence of a catalytic amount of concentrated sulphuric acid. The reaction is reversible, so a large excess of alcohol is used to drive it forward.

```text
H2N-C6H4-COOH  +  C2H5OH   ⇌   H2N-C6H4-COOC2H5  +  H2O
   (p-aminobenzoic acid)            (benzocaine)
```

During the reaction the amino group is protonated by the acid and the product stays in solution as the sulphate salt; neutralisation with sodium carbonate at the end liberates the free base, which separates as a solid.

## Requirements

p-Aminobenzoic acid 5 g, absolute ethanol 50 ml, concentrated sulphuric acid 5 ml, 10% sodium carbonate solution, round-bottomed flask 250 ml, water condenser, water bath, litmus paper, Buchner funnel, melting point apparatus.

## Procedure

1. Place 5 g of p-aminobenzoic acid and 50 ml of absolute ethanol in a 250 ml round-bottomed flask and swirl to dissolve.
2. Cool the flask in cold water and add 5 ml of concentrated sulphuric acid slowly down the side with swirling. A precipitate may appear and then dissolve on heating.
3. Fit a water condenser and reflux the mixture on a water bath for about one and a half to two hours.
4. Cool the mixture and distil off most of the excess ethanol on a water bath (or pour the mixture into about 100 ml of water).
5. Add 10% sodium carbonate solution **slowly** with stirring until the mixture is just alkaline to litmus; carbon dioxide is evolved and benzocaine separates as a white solid.
6. Filter under suction, wash with cold water until free of alkali and press dry.
7. Recrystallise from aqueous ethanol (dissolve in the least hot ethanol, then add hot water until turbid and cool). Dry and record the weight and melting point.

**Worked example.** 5 g of p-aminobenzoic acid (molecular weight 137.14) is 0.0365 mol. One mole gives one mole of benzocaine (molecular weight 165.19), so the theoretical yield is 0.0365 × 165.19 = 6.03 g. If 4.2 g is obtained, the yield is (4.2/6.03) × 100 = **70%**.

## Precautions

1. Add sulphuric acid slowly to the cooled alcoholic solution, never water or alkali to the acid directly.
2. Use absolute (dry) ethanol; water shifts the equilibrium back towards the acid.
3. Neutralise slowly, as carbon dioxide froths; stop as soon as the mixture is just alkaline, since excess alkali can hydrolyse the ester.
4. Wash the product free of alkali and dry it well before taking the melting point.

## Viva questions

1. **What is the role of sulphuric acid?** It is a catalyst that protonates the carbonyl oxygen and makes the carbon more electrophilic; it also removes water, helping the equilibrium.
2. **Why is excess ethanol used?** The esterification is reversible, so an excess of one reactant drives the equilibrium towards the ester (Le Chatelier's principle).
3. **Why does the product dissolve during the reaction and separate on neutralisation?** In acid the amino group is protonated and the salt is water soluble; sodium carbonate frees the base, which is insoluble.
4. **Why is sodium carbonate used instead of sodium hydroxide?** It is mild enough to neutralise the mineral acid without hydrolysing the ester back to the acid.
5. **To which class of local anaesthetics does benzocaine belong, and what is its main use?** The ester class; it is used for surface anaesthesia of skin and mucous membranes, in lozenges and in ointments.
6. **Why is benzocaine only a surface anaesthetic?** It has no basic side-chain nitrogen, so it is very poorly water soluble and cannot be given by injection like procaine or lignocaine.
