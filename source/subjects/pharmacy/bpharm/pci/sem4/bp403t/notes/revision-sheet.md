# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, learn these five topics with full diagrams and one worked numerical each: **types of colloids with zeta potential and protective action**, **non-Newtonian flow and thixotropy with viscometers**, **flocculated vs deflocculated suspensions with Stokes's law and sedimentation volume**, **particle size methods with derived properties of powders**, and **orders of reaction with Arrhenius-based accelerated stability testing**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**One-line contrasts examiners like**

| Pair | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Lyophilic vs lyophobic | Solvated, spontaneous, reversible, stable to electrolytes vs charge-stabilised, made by special methods, coagulated by small amounts of electrolyte |
| Nernst vs zeta potential | Surface to bulk vs shear plane to bulk; zeta controls stability |
| Electrophoresis vs electro-osmosis | Particles move vs liquid moves |
| Coacervation vs coagulation | Two liquid phases vs solid aggregate |
| Peptization vs protective action | Precipitate back to sol vs lyophilic colloid shielding a lyophobic sol |
| Plastic vs pseudoplastic | Yield value vs none; both shear thinning |
| Thixotropy vs rheopexy | Slow recovery after shear vs faster gel formation with gentle shaking |
| Capillary vs rotational viscometer | Single point, Newtonian only vs multipoint, any system |
| Elastic vs plastic deformation | Reversible below yield point vs permanent above it |
| Flocculated vs deflocculated | Fast settling, loose sediment, no cake vs slow settling, compact cake |
| Macro- vs microemulsion | Milky, unstable, needs energy vs clear, stable, spontaneous |
| Creaming vs cracking | Reversible vs irreversible |
| Number vs weight distribution | Microscope/Coulter vs sieve/sedimentation |
| BET vs permeability surface area | Total surface including pores vs external surface |
| Bulk vs true density | Includes voids vs solid only |
| Specific vs general catalysis | H⁺/OH⁻ only vs buffer species too |
| Zero vs first order t½ | Depends on A0 vs independent of A0 |

**Practicals in one line each:** sieve analysis → weight distribution and mean size Σ(% × mean size)/100; microscopy → calibrate eyepiece with stage micrometer, count 300+ particles, dln = Σnd/Σn; bulk density, tapped density, true density (pycnometer) → porosity; angle of repose tan θ = h/r, falls then rises as lubricant increases; Ostwald η1 = η2ρ1t1/ρ2t2; sedimentation volume F = Hu/H0 for different agents and concentrations; Brookfield viscosity at rising and falling rpm → rheogram; methyl acetate hydrolysis k = (2.303/t) log[(V∞ − V0)/(V∞ − Vt)]; ethyl acetate saponification k = (1/at)(Vt − V0)/(V∞ − Vt); accelerated stability → k at 50, 60, 70 °C → log k vs 1/T → t90 at 25 °C.
