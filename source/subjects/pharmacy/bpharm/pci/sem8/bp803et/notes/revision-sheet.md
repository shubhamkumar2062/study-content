# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, learn these five answers with a diagram or table each: **marketing environment (micro and macro)**, **product life cycle with strategies at each stage**, **promotional mix and its determinants**, **duties, selection, training and compensation of the PSR**, and **DPCO and NPPA**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**Definitions in one line**

| Term | One-line meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Marketing | Identifying customer needs and satisfying them profitably through product, price, place and promotion |
| Market | All actual and potential buyers of a product |
| Segmentation | Dividing a market into groups of buyers with similar needs |
| Targeting | Choosing which segments to serve |
| Positioning | Giving a product a clear, distinctive place in the customer's mind |
| Product mix | All product lines and items a company sells (width, length, depth, consistency) |
| Brand | Name, sign or symbol that identifies a seller's product |
| Promotion | Seller's communication to inform, persuade and remind |
| Detailing | Planned scientific presentation of a product to a doctor |
| Marketing channel | Set of organisations that make a product available to the user |
| Physical distribution | Planning and controlling the flow of goods to the point of use |
| Ceiling price | Maximum price notified by the government for a scheduled formulation |
| Consumerism | Movement to protect and strengthen buyers' rights |

**Facts examiners can check**

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| DPCO issued under | Section 3, Essential Commodities Act, 1955 |
| Earlier DPCOs | 1970, 1979, 1987, 1995 (cost-based) |
| Current order | DPCO 2013, based on National Pharmaceutical Pricing Policy 2012 |
| Scheduled formulations | Medicines in the National List of Essential Medicines (First Schedule) |
| Ceiling price formula | Simple average PTR of brands with ≥1% market share + 16% retailer margin |
| Annual revision | 1 April, by wholesale price index |
| Non-scheduled drugs | MRP rise limited to 10% a year |
| NPPA set up | 29 August 1997, Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers |
| Advertising law | Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954; Schedule J of Drugs and Cosmetics Rules |
| Marketing code | Uniform Code for Pharmaceutical Marketing Practices (2014, revised 2024) |
| Consumer law | Consumer Protection Act, 2019 |
| Break-even units | Fixed cost ÷ (price − variable cost per unit) |

**One-line contrasts examiners like**

| Pair | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Marketing vs selling | Starts from customer needs vs starts from the product in the factory |
| Consumer vs industrial buying | Individual, small, emotional vs group (buying centre), large, technical, tender-based |
| Push vs pull | Promotion aimed at channel and prescribers vs aimed at final consumers |
| Skimming vs penetration | High launch price vs low launch price |
| Product line vs product mix | Related group of products vs all products of the company |
| Primary vs secondary pack | In contact with the product vs carton around it |
| C&F agent vs stockist | Works on commission, does not own goods vs buys, owns and resells stock |
| Vertical vs horizontal marketing | Different levels act as one system vs same-level firms combine |
| DPCO vs NPPA | The legal pricing order vs the authority that enforces it |
| Salary vs commission plan | Security and control vs incentive with insecurity |
