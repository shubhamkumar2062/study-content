## Unit 1 — Definitions, public health, seven products

- **Nutraceutical** (DeFelice, 1989): food or food part with medical/health benefit incl. prevention or treatment of disease. **Functional food**: ordinary food with benefit beyond nutrition (idea from Japan; FOSHU). **Dietary supplement** (US DSHEA 1994): oral product adding a dietary ingredient; India says **health supplement**.
- **Classify** by source (plant, animal, microbe, mineral), chemistry (isoprenoids, phenolics, carbohydrates, fatty acids, amino-acid based, microbes, minerals), mechanism (antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, lipid lowering…), product type, traditional vs non-traditional.
- **Diseases**: obesity — fibre, catechins; diabetes — fibre, fenugreek, chromium, lipoic acid; cancer — lycopene, sulforaphane, EGCG; heart — omega-3, sterols, beta-glucan; hypertension — potassium, garlic, milk peptides; stress — adaptogens; osteoarthritis — glucosamine, chondroitin.
- **Public health nutrition**: double burden (undernutrition + obesity), hidden hunger (iron, iodine, vitamin A); fortification, supplementation, diversification, education; ICDS, PM POSHAN, POSHAN Abhiyaan, Anaemia Mukt Bharat.
- **Mother and child**: folic acid (neural tube defects), iron, iodine, calcium, DHA; exclusive breastfeeding 6 months, colostrum; marasmus vs kwashiorkor. **Elderly**: protein (sarcopenia), B12, calcium + vitamin D, fibre, water.

## Unit 2 — Phytochemicals

- **Carotenoids** = C40 tetraterpenoids. Carotenes (alpha, beta, lycopene) — no O; xanthophylls (lutein, zeaxanthin) — contain O. Beta-carotene → 2 retinal; lycopene acyclic, no vitamin A, best singlet O2 quencher, better from cooked tomato in oil; lutein/zeaxanthin = macular pigment.
- **Sulphides**: alliin → allicin → diallyl sulphide, disulphide, **trisulphide (allitridin)**, ajoene.
- **Resveratrol**: 3,5,4′-trihydroxystilbene, red grape skin, phytoalexin, SIRT1, French paradox.
- **Flavonoids** (C6–C3–C6): rutin (quercetin-3-rutinoside, capillaries), naringin (grapefruit flavanone glycoside), quercetin (onion flavonol), anthocyanidins (cyanidin; flavylium; pH colour), catechins (green tea EGCG), flavones (apigenin, luteolin).
- **Prebiotics**: FOS/inulin, β(2→1) fructans, bifidogenic → SCFAs. **Probiotics**: *Lactobacillus* (lactic acid bacteria), strain-specific, survive acid and bile.
- **Phytoestrogens**: isoflavones (genistein, daidzein → equol), lignans (flax → enterolactone), coumestans; weak ERβ agonists.
- **Tocopherols**: 4 tocopherols + 4 tocotrienols; chromanol ring; α most active; chain-breaking, recycled by vitamin C.
- **Functional foods**: oats (beta-glucan), wheat bran (insoluble fibre), rice bran (oryzanol, tocotrienols), fish (EPA, DHA), tea (catechins, theanine), coffee (chlorogenic acid).

## Unit 3 — Free radicals and fibre

- Free radical = unpaired electron. ROS: O2•⁻, •OH (**most reactive**), ROO•; non-radicals H2O2, ¹O2, HOCl. RNS: NO•, ONOO⁻.
- O2 → O2•⁻ → H2O2 → •OH → H2O. **Fenton**: Fe²⁺ + H2O2 → Fe³⁺ + •OH + OH⁻.
- Sources: mitochondria (largest), NADPH oxidase, xanthine oxidase, CYP450, peroxisomes; radiation, smoke, pollution, drugs, metals.
- **Lipid peroxidation**: LH + •OH → L•; L• + O2 → LOO•; LOO• + LH → LOOH + L•; ended by vitamin E. Markers MDA (TBARS), F2-isoprostanes, 4-HNE.
- Proteins: carbonyls, –SH oxidation, 3-nitrotyrosine. Carbohydrates: AGEs, hyaluronic acid breakdown. DNA: **8-oxo-dG** (G→T), strand breaks.
- **Fibre**: soluble (pectin, beta-glucan, gums, psyllium, inulin) → gel, lowers LDL (bile acids) and glucose; insoluble (cellulose, hemicellulose, lignin) → bulk, transit. SCFAs: acetate, propionate, **butyrate** (colon fuel).

## Unit 4 — Disease, ageing, antioxidants

- Diabetes: glucose auto-oxidation, AGE–RAGE, polyol pathway (NADPH), PKC; β-cells low in catalase/GPx.
- Inflammation: respiratory burst, HOCl, peroxynitrite, NF-κB.
- **Ischaemia–reperfusion**: hypoxanthine accumulates, xanthine dehydrogenase → **xanthine oxidase**; on reperfusion → O2•⁻; allopurinol.
- Atherosclerosis: ox-LDL → scavenger receptor → foam cells → fatty streak → plaque.
- Brain: high O2 use, PUFAs, iron, low catalase, dopamine; Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, ALS (SOD1).
- **Harman 1956** free radical theory of ageing; lipofuscin; mitochondrial theory.
- **SOD**: 2 O2•⁻ + 2H⁺ → H2O2 + O2 (Cu/Zn cytosol, Mn mitochondria). **Catalase**: 2 H2O2 → 2 H2O + O2 (haem, peroxisomes). **GPx**: H2O2 + 2GSH → GSSG + 2H2O (**selenium**); glutathione reductase + NADPH.
- **Glutathione** γ-Glu-Cys-Gly; **lipoic acid** — DHLA, regenerates C, E, GSH, PDH cofactor; **melatonin** — pineal indoleamine from tryptophan, crosses BBB.
- **BHT** (2,6-di-tert-butyl-4-methylphenol) and **BHA** — hindered phenols, H-donors to LOO•; synergy with citric acid.

## Unit 5 — Stability and regulation

- Heat, O2, light, moisture, pH, metals, enzymes, time. Protect: antioxidants, chelators, stable esters, encapsulation, opaque/O2-barrier packs, desiccants, cold storage, **overages**, stability testing.
- **FSSAI** — FSS Act 2006 (repealed PFA 1954, FPO, MPO and other orders). Nutraceutical regulations 2016 (notified 23 Dec 2016; enforced 1 Jan 2018) → replaced by **2022** regulations. Positive lists; vitamins/minerals **not above ICMR RDA**; no disease claims; "**NOT FOR MEDICINAL USE**"; warnings, side effects, drug interactions on label; no hormones, steroids, psychotropics.
- **US FDA**: DSHEA 1994, supplements = food, structure/function claim disclaimer, 21 CFR 111.
- **FPO 1955** fruit/vegetable products; **MPO 1973** meat products (both under Essential Commodities Act, now in FSS Act). **AGMARK**: Agricultural Produce (Grading and Marking) Act 1937, DMI.
- **HACCP 7**: hazard analysis → CCPs → critical limits → monitoring → corrective action → verification → records.
- **GMP/GHP**: premises, equipment, water, personnel hygiene, cleaning, pest control, process control, records, recall (FSSAI Schedule 4).
- **Adulteration tests**: starch → iodine blue; metanil yellow → HCl pink; argemone oil → HNO3 red-brown; papaya seeds in pepper float. Supplements spiked with sildenafil, sibutramine, steroids.
- **Pharmacopoeial specs**: identity (HPTLC/HPLC), assay, uniformity, disintegration/dissolution, moisture, microbial limits, heavy metals, pesticides, aflatoxins; USP supplement chapters <2040>, <2091>, <2021>–<2023>, <2750>.
