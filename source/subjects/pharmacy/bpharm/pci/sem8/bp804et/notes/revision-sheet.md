# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, prepare these with a chart or table each: **drug discovery and development stages**, **IND (types, contents, 30-day rule)**, **NDA vs ANDA with patent certifications**, **changes to an approved NDA**, **CTD modules and eCTD**, **DMF types**, **ethics committee and informed consent**, and **Orange Book / CFR / Federal Register**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**Unit 1 — Discovery and development**

- Discovery: target identification → validation → assay and hits (HTS, natural products, structure-based design, computer methods) → lead → lead optimisation (SAR, ADME, early safety) → candidate.
- Pre-clinical under GLP: pharmacology (safety pharmacology core battery: CVS incl. hERG/QT, CNS, respiratory), ADME + toxicokinetics, toxicology (acute, repeated dose → NOAEL, genotoxicity, carcinogenicity, reproductive, local tolerance).
- Non-clinical activities: API, pre-formulation, formulation, analytical, stability, clinical supplies, packaging, IP.
- Phases: 0 micro-dose; I safety/PK (healthy volunteers); II efficacy/dose; III confirmatory; IV post-marketing.
- Generic = same API, dosage form, strength, route, use + BE. Hatch–Waxman 1984: ANDA, patent term restoration, Paragraph IV, 180 days.
- Generic development: reference characterisation → API with DMF → formulation → f2 50–100 → exhibit batch → BE (90% CI 80.00–125.00%) → stability → dossier.

**Unit 2 — Approval process and authorities**

| Item | Key facts |
| --- | --- |
| IND | 21 CFR 312; Form 1571, 1572; investigator, emergency, treatment, commercial; effective 30 days after receipt unless clinical hold |
| NDA | 21 CFR 314; Form 356h; 505(b)(1), 505(b)(2); filing in 60 days; goals 10 months standard, 6 priority; approval or complete response letter |
| ANDA | 505(j); same as RLD + BE; Paragraph I (no patent), II (expired), III (will expire), IV (invalid / not infringed → suit → 30-month stay; 180-day exclusivity); tentative approval |

| India | CDSCO/DCGI (CLA); CT-04 → CT-06; CT-18 import, CT-21 manufacture; 90 working days (30 for India-developed, deemed approval); SUGAM |
| USA | FDA under HHS; CDER, CBER, CDRH, CVM |
| EU | EMA (CHMP, PRAC, CAT, COMP, PDCO, HMPC); Commission grants; centralised (210 active days + 67 days), decentralised, mutual recognition, national; valid 5 years |
| UK | MHRA; Commission on Human Medicines; Yellow Card |
| Australia | TGA; Therapeutic Goods Act 1989; ARTG; AUST R (registered), AUST L (listed) |
| Japan | MHLW approves, PMDA reviews; PMD Act |
| Canada | Health Canada; NDS, ANDS, CTA; NOC + DIN |

**Unit 3 — Overseas registration**

- Export: IEC (DGFT), RCMC (Pharmexcil), manufacturing licence, WHO-GMP, COPP (CDSCO), free sale certificate, export NOC where needed → importing country dossier → registration → import permit → shipment (invoice, packing list, shipping bill, bill of lading, certificate of origin, CoA).
- DMF: confidential; neither approved nor disapproved; LoA; Type II API, III packaging, IV excipients, V reference information; Type I no longer used. EU: ASMF, CEP.
- CTD (ICH M4): M1 regional admin; M2 summaries (2.3 QOS, 2.4/2.5 overviews, 2.6/2.7 summaries); M3 quality (3.2.S, 3.2.P); M4 non-clinical; M5 clinical.
- eCTD: m1–m5 folders, XML backbone, sequences 0000…, new/replace/append/delete, checksums; v3.2.2 and v4.0.
- ACTD (ASEAN): Part I admin + product information, II quality, III non-clinical, IV clinical; with ACTR.

**Unit 4 — Clinical trials**

- Protocol: general information, background, objectives, design (endpoints, randomisation, blinding, control), subjects, treatment, efficacy, safety, statistics, ethics, data, finance.
- Ethics committee (NDCT rule 7): ≥7 members, lay person, woman, legal expert, independent member; ≥50% non-affiliated; non-affiliated chairperson; CT-01 → CT-02. US IRB ≥5.
- Consent: information, comprehension, voluntariness, signature, copy, re-consent; assent for children; impartial witness; AV recording for vulnerable subjects in NCE/NME trials (audio only for anti-HIV, anti-leprosy).
- Investigator: protocol, consent, care, SAE report, drug accountability, records. Sponsor: QMS, IB, GMP supplies, monitoring, audit, safety reporting, compensation, CTRI. Monitor: source data verification, reports.
- Serious = death, life-threatening, hospitalisation, disability, congenital anomaly. ICH E2A: 7 days fatal/life-threatening unexpected, 15 days others. India: SAE within 24 h; analysed report within 14 days. DSUR yearly; PSUR 6-monthly × 2 years then yearly × 2.

**Unit 5 — Regulatory concepts**

| Item | Key facts |
| --- | --- |
| Hierarchy | Act (binding, legislature) → rules/regulations (binding, agency) → guidance/guidelines ("should", not binding) |
| Federal Register | Daily US journal; final rules, proposed rules, notices, presidential documents; India = Gazette of India |
| CFR | 50 titles; Title 21; 11, 50, 54, 56, 58, 201, 210/211, 312, 314, 320, 600s; revised yearly |
| Orange Book | Approved Drug Products with Therapeutic Equivalence Evaluations; A codes (AB) vs B codes; patents, exclusivity |
| Exclusivity | NCE 5 y; new clinical investigation 3 y; orphan 7 y; paediatric +6 months; first generic 180 days |
| Purple Book | Database of Licensed Biological Products; PHS Act 351(a), 351(k); biosimilar, interchangeable; reference product exclusivity |
