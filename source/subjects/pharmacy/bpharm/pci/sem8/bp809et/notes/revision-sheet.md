# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, learn these five answers with a table or diagram each: **cold cream vs vanishing cream (with building blocks)**, **sunscreens: classification and SPF**, **PPD hair dye chemistry and formulation**, **antiperspirants and deodorants**, and **sebumeter, corneometer and TEWL**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**Definitions in one line**

| Term | One-line meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Cosmetic (India) | Article rubbed, poured, sprinkled, sprayed, introduced or applied to the body to cleanse, beautify, promote attractiveness or alter appearance, including components (D&C Act, 1940) |
| Cosmetic (EU) | Substance or mixture placed on external body parts, teeth or oral mucosa mainly to clean, perfume, change appearance, protect, keep in good condition or correct body odour (Regulation 1223/2009) |
| Cosmeceutical | Cosmetic with an active claimed to give a biological benefit; not a legal category |
| Quasi drug | Japanese category of medicated cosmetics (anti-dandruff, whitening, deodorant) |
| Humectant | Hygroscopic agent that attracts and holds water (glycerin, sorbitol, sodium PCA) |
| Emollient | Softens and smooths skin (esters, oils, silicones) |
| SPF | MED of protected skin ÷ MED of unprotected skin |
| MED | Smallest UV dose giving just-visible redness after 16–24 h |
| TEWL | Water passing through the epidermis and evaporating; marker of barrier function |
| Comedogenic | Tends to block pores and form comedones |
| Syndet bar | Cleansing bar of synthetic surfactants (SCI), pH about 5.5–7 |
| TFM | Total fatty matter of soap; BIS grades 1/2/3 = at least 76/70/60% |

**Key comparisons**

| Cold cream | Vanishing cream |
| --- | --- |
| Mainly w/o | o/w |
| Beeswax + liquid paraffin + borax | Stearic acid + KOH/TEA + glycerin |
| Sodium soap formed in situ | Potassium/TEA stearate |
| Greasy, cooling, cleansing | Matt, non-greasy, pearly, foundation base |

| Antiperspirant | Deodorant |
| --- | --- |
| Reduces sweat | Reduces odour |
| Aluminium chlorohydrate, Al–Zr glycine complexes, AlCl3 | Ethanol, antimicrobials, zinc ricinoleate, perfume |
| Al(OH)3 gel plug in sweat duct | Kills bacteria, absorbs or masks odour |

| Soap | Syndet bar |
| --- | --- |
| Fatty acid salts; pH 9–10 | SCI etc.; pH 5.5–7 |
| Scum in hard water | No scum |
| More lipid loss, higher TEWL | Milder |

**Numbers to remember**

- UVB 290–320 nm; UVA 320–400 nm; SPF test at 2 mg/cm²; SPF 15/30/50 ≈ 93/97/98% UVB blocked.
- Skin surface pH about 4.5–5.5; epidermal turnover about 4 weeks.
- Scalp hair grows about 1 cm per month; up to about 100 hairs shed daily is normal.
- Dielectric constant of water about 80 vs below 7 for other skin substances.
- Instrument tests: rest 15–30 min, about 20–22 °C and 40–60% RH.

**Last tips:** write the building-blocks table for every product, add method and evaluation, draw at least one diagram per long answer, and never invent law numbers or limits.
