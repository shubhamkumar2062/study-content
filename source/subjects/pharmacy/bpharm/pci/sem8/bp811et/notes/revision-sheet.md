# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, learn these with full diagrams: **NMR principle with chemical shift, factors and spin–spin coupling**, **mass spectrometer with EI/CI and quadrupole/TOF**, **DSC (with TGA and DTA)**, **Bragg's law with powder and single crystal methods**, **ICH validation parameters**, **calibration of UV-visible spectrophotometer and HPLC**, **RIA principle**, **SPE steps** and **LC-MS/MS with ESI/APCI**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**Comparisons in one line each**

- **¹H vs ¹³C NMR:** 99.98% vs 1.1% abundance; 0–12 vs 0–220 ppm; integration reliable vs not; splitting shown vs decoupled singlets.
- **EI vs CI:** high energy, many fragments, M⁺• may be absent vs proton transfer from reagent gas, [M + H]⁺, few fragments.
- **Quadrupole vs TOF:** DC + RF mass filter, unit resolution vs flight time, unlimited range, pairs with MALDI.
- **TGA vs DSC:** mass change vs heat flow; dehydration and decomposition vs melting, Tg, polymorphs, purity.
- **DTA vs DSC:** ΔT, qualitative vs heat flow, quantitative enthalpy.
- **Powder vs single crystal XRD:** fingerprint of crystal form vs complete 3D structure.
- **Calibration vs validation:** instrument accuracy vs fitness of a method or process.
- **RIA vs IRMA:** labelled antigen, limited antibody, competitive, signal falls vs labelled antibody in excess, non-competitive, signal rises.
- **LLE vs SPE:** two liquids, large solvent use, emulsions vs solid sorbent, little solvent, easy automation.
- **GC-MS vs LC-MS:** volatile compounds, EI, libraries vs polar non-volatile compounds, ESI/APCI, bioanalysis.

**Fifteen-minute self-test**

1. A signal is 1260 Hz from TMS on a 300 MHz instrument. What is δ? (4.20 ppm)
2. Why does an alkyne proton appear upfield of an alkene proton? (Anisotropy: the π cylinder shields along the axis.)
3. Give the splitting of the CH2 in ethyl acetate. (Quartet; 3 neighbours.)
4. A compound shows m/z 156 and 158 in 1 : 1 ratio. What does it contain? (One bromine.)
5. Calculate the % mass loss when CuSO4·5H2O loses all its water. (90.1/249.7 × 100 ≈ 36.1%.)
6. Cu Kα (1.5406 Å) gives a peak at 2θ = 20°. What is d? (4.44 Å)
7. Name the standard used to check UV absorbance accuracy. (Potassium dichromate.)
8. Which RIA separation uses an anti-IgG? (Double antibody method.)
9. Which ESI ion would a 15,000 Da protein carrying 10 protons give? (m/z about 1501.)
10. Name the two scans of a triple quadrupole used for quantitation. (SRM/MRM: Q1 and Q3 both fixed.)
