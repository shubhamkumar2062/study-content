# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, prepare these five answers in full: **levels of prevention with examples**, **prevention and control of malaria (or dengue)**, **NTEP or NACP (objectives, components, pharmacist's role)**, **RMNCH+A / family welfare**, and **functions of the PHC with primary health care principles**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**Unit 1 — health, nutrition, society**

| Topic | Key points |
| --- | --- |
| WHO definition (1948) | Complete physical, mental and social well-being, not merely absence of disease |
| Triad | Agent – host – environment; NCDs are multifactorial |
| Levels of prevention | Primordial (stop risk factors) → primary (vaccines, safe water) → secondary (screening) → tertiary (rehabilitation) |
| Leavell and Clark | Health promotion, specific protection, early diagnosis and treatment, disability limitation, rehabilitation |
| Eradication | Worldwide end (smallpox); elimination = regional or below a set level |
| Vitamin A | Night blindness, Bitot's spots, keratomalacia |
| Niacin | Pellagra: dermatitis, diarrhoea, dementia |
| Folate | Megaloblastic anaemia, neural tube defects |
| Kwashiorkor vs marasmus | Oedema present vs absent |
| SAM (6–59 months) | WHZ < −3 SD, or MUAC < 115 mm, or bilateral pitting oedema |
| Poverty | Vicious cycle: poverty → disease → loss of income → poverty |

**Unit 2 — diseases**

| Disease | Agent / vector | Prevention in one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cholera | *V. cholerae* O1, O139; faecal–oral | ORS, safe water, sanitation, hand washing, oral vaccine in high-risk areas |
| SARS | SARS-CoV; droplets | Isolation, quarantine, PPE, hand hygiene |
| Ebola | Filovirus; body fluids | Isolation, PPE, safe burial, 21-day contact follow-up, vaccine for contacts |
| Influenza | Orthomyxovirus; droplets | Yearly vaccine (drift), cough etiquette, antivirals for high-risk |
| Child pneumonia | *S. pneumoniae*, Hib, RSV | Vaccines, breastfeeding, clean fuel; fast breathing 60/50/40 |
| Malaria | *Plasmodium*; *Anopheles* (night) | Test and treat, IRS, LLIN, fish, source reduction |
| Dengue | 4 serotypes; *Aedes* (day, clean water) | Weekly emptying of containers; paracetamol, no NSAIDs |
| Chikungunya | Alphavirus; *Aedes* | As dengue; severe joint pain |
| Filariasis | *W. bancrofti*; *Culex* | Annual MDA + morbidity management |
| Hypertension | ≥ 140/90 | Salt < 5 g/day, weight, exercise, screening, adherence |
| Diabetes | FPG ≥ 126; HbA1c ≥ 6.5% | Diet, activity, screening, foot and eye care |
| Cancer | Tobacco, HPV, alcohol | Tobacco control, HPV vaccine, screen oral/breast/cervix |
| Substance abuse | Dependence, tolerance, withdrawal | NDPS Act 1985; supply, demand, harm reduction |

**Pharmacist's role — one list for every programme answer**

Logistics and cold chain → dispensing and adherence counselling → health education → screening and referral → surveillance and reporting (TB notification, ADR, AEFI) → rational drug use → stigma reduction and confidentiality.
