# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, prepare these with a flow chart or table each: **pilot plant general considerations and SUPAC**, **technology transfer protocol and steps**, **IND, IB and NDA**, **QbD**, **OOS investigation and change control**, and **CDSCO / State Licensing Authority with the new drug approval procedure**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**Unit 1 — Pilot plant scale-up**

- Pilot plant = lab formula → reliable, economical process on production-like equipment. Pilot batch (solid orals) ≥ 10% of production or 100,000 units, whichever is larger.
- General considerations: reporting responsibility, personnel, space (administration, physical testing, equipment floor, storage), formula review, raw materials, equipment, production rate, process evaluation, master manufacturing procedures, stability, GMP.
- Solids: blending, granulation end point, drying, milling, lubrication time, compression speed. Liquids: agitation, heating/cooling, filtration, homogenisation. Semisolids: heating/cooling rates, shear, vacuum mixing.
- SUPAC (US FDA): IR, MR, SS; changes in components, site, batch size, equipment, process. Level 1 annual report; Level 2 supplement; Level 3 prior approval. Batch size up to 10× = Level 1. f2 50–100 = similar.
- Platform technology: reusable system (OROS, HPMC matrix, liposomes, hot-melt extrusion, mAb and mRNA platforms).

**Unit 3 — Regulatory affairs and drug approval**

- RA roles: strategy, dossier, submission, labelling, life cycle, compliance, intelligence.
- History: 1906 Pure Food and Drugs Act; 1938 FD&C Act (sulfanilamide elixir); 1962 Kefauver–Harris (thalidomide; efficacy); 1984 Hatch–Waxman (ANDA); 1990 ICH.
- Non-clinical: pharmacology + safety pharmacology core battery; ADME + toxicokinetics; toxicity (acute, repeated dose, genotoxicity, carcinogenicity, reproductive, local).
- IND: 21 CFR 312, Form 1571 (sponsor), 1572 (investigator); types investigator, emergency, treatment, commercial; start after 30 days if no clinical hold.
- IB: clinical + non-clinical data for investigators (ICH E6); updated yearly.
- NDA: 21 CFR 314, Form 356h; 505(b)(1), 505(b)(2), 505(j) ANDA; BLA for biologics.
- Phases 0–IV. BE: crossover, washout, Cmax, AUC; 90% CI 80–125%.
- Biostatistics: α 0.05, power 80–90%, p-value, CI, t-test, ANOVA. Submissions: eCTD, ICH E3, CDISC (SDTM, ADaM).

**Unit 4 — Quality management systems**

- QM ⊃ QA ⊃ GMP ⊃ QC. Juran: fitness for use; Crosby: conformance to requirements.
- TQM: customer focus, all employees, process approach, continual improvement, PDCA; seven tools (fishbone, Pareto, control chart …).
- QbD (ICH Q8(R2)): QTPP → CQA → risk assessment (Q9) → DoE → design space → control strategy → life cycle; Q10 quality system; PAT.
- Six Sigma: 3.4 DPMO; DMAIC; DMADV; belts; Cpk ≥ 1.33.
- OOS: Phase I lab → Phase II full investigation → CAPA; no testing into compliance.
- Change control: request → classify → impact → approve → implement → notify → verify → close.
- ISO 9000 vocabulary; 9001 requirements (certifiable); 9004; 19011 auditing; 7 principles. ISO 14001 = EMS.
- NABL (QCI): ISO/IEC 17025 labs, ISO 15189 medical labs. GLP: OECD principles, 21 CFR 58 (1978), NGCMA (DST); study director + QA unit.
