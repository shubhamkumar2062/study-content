# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, prepare these with a diagram or table each: **approaches to controlled release with release kinetics**, **methods of microencapsulation**, **mucoadhesion theories and buccal patch**, **elementary osmotic pump**, **transdermal patch components and types**, **approaches to GRDDS**, **pMDI and DPI**, **liposomes**, and **ocular barriers with Ocusert**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**Unit 1 — Controlled release and polymers**

| Item | Key facts |
| --- | --- |
| Terms | SR (slow, not constant), CR (predictable, zero order), delayed (enteric), repeat action, targeted |
| Good candidate | t1/2 about 2–8 h, small dose, wide therapeutic index, absorbed throughout gut, stable |
| Reservoir | dM/dt = ADKΔC/L → zero order; risk of dose dumping |
| Matrix | Higuchi Q = kH√t; Q = [D(2A − Cs)Cs t]^½ |
| Dissolution | Noyes–Whitney dC/dt = DA(Cs − C)/h; coated beads of different thickness |
| Ion exchange | Cation resin + basic drug; anion resin + acidic drug; coated complex for SR suspension |
| Kinetics | Zero Q = Q0 + k0t, t1/2 = Q0/2k0; first ln Q = ln Q0 − kt, t1/2 = 0.693/k; Peppas n 0.5 Fickian, 1 case II |
| Polymers | Natural (alginate, chitosan, gelatin), semisynthetic (HPMC, EC, CAP), synthetic (Eudragit, PLGA, EVA, silicone); bulk (PLGA) vs surface (polyanhydride) erosion |
| Eudragit | L >pH 6, S >pH 7, RL/RS insoluble permeable, E dissolves in stomach |

Worked check: 25% released at 1 h by Higuchi → 50% at 4 h, 75% at 9 h; first order k = 0.1 h⁻¹ → t1/2 6.93 h, 70% released in 12 h.

**Unit 2 — Microencapsulation, mucosal, implants**

- Microcapsule = core + wall; microsphere = matrix; 1–1000 µm.
- Coacervation: 3 phases → coacervate deposits → hardening; complex = gelatin + acacia at pH about 4–4.5.
- Others: solvent evaporation (o/w, w/o/w), ionic gelation (alginate + Ca²⁺), interfacial polymerisation, Wurster, pan, spray drying/congealing, multi-orifice centrifugal.
- Mucoadhesion: contact → consolidation; theories: electronic, adsorption, wetting, diffusion, fracture, mechanical. Polymers: carbomer, polycarbophil, CMC, HPMC, chitosan, thiomers.
- Buccal patch: backing (EC) + drug/polymer layer + adhesive; tests: mucoadhesive strength, surface pH, residence time.
- Implants: reservoir (silicone), matrix (EVA rod), biodegradable (PLGA, polyanhydride wafer), pumps, in situ forming.
- EOP: cellulose acetate membrane + drug + osmogen (NaCl) + laser orifice → zero order; push–pull with PEO push layer.

**Unit 3 — Transdermal, GRDDS, nasopulmonary**

- Stratum corneum about 10–20 µm, brick-and-mortar; routes: intercellular (main), transcellular, appendageal.
- J = (DK/h)ΔC = Kp Cv; example Kp 1 × 10⁻³ cm/h, Cv 10 mg/ml → 10 µg/cm²/h.
- Ideal drug: MW below about 500, log P 1–3, low melting point, potent.
- Enhancers: DMSO, azone, PG, ethanol, oleic acid, terpenes, SLS; iontophoresis, electroporation, sonophoresis, microneedles.
- Patch: backing → reservoir/matrix → membrane → PSA → liner. Types: reservoir, drug-in-adhesive, matrix, microreservoir.
- GRDDS: MMC phase III housekeeper wave; floating (HBS, NaHCO3 effervescent, microballoons, rafts), high density >2.5 g/cm³, expandable, inflatable, bioadhesive, magnetic; tests: floating lag time, total floating time.
- Nasal about 150 cm², clearance about 15–20 min; pulmonary 1–5 µm deposit deep; pMDI (HFA-134a/227, oleic acid, ethanol), DPI (lactose carrier), nebulisers (jet, ultrasonic, mesh); cascade impactor.

**Unit 4 — Targeted delivery**

- Levels: organ, cell, intracellular. Passive (RES, EPR), active (ligands), physical, inverse.
- Liposomes: PC + cholesterol; MLV, SUV, LUV; thin-film hydration, sonication, ethanol/ether injection, reverse-phase evaporation, detergent removal; PEGylated doxorubicin, liposomal amphotericin B.
- Niosomes: Span/Tween + cholesterol; cheaper, more stable.
- Nanoparticles: nanospheres, nanocapsules, SLN, NLC; nanoprecipitation, solvent evaporation, ionic gelation; DLS and zeta potential.
- mAbs: hybridoma (myeloma + B cell, PEG fusion, HAT selection); -omab, -ximab, -zumab, -umab; ADCs, immunoliposomes.

**Unit 5 — Ocular and intrauterine**

- Drop 30–50 µl vs cul-de-sac 7–10 µl; tear turnover about 1 µl/min; usually less than about 5% absorbed.
- Cornea: lipophilic epithelium, hydrophilic stroma; blood–aqueous and blood–retinal barriers.
- Fixes: viscosity, gels, in situ gels (CAP latex, poloxamer 407, gellan), mucoadhesives, prodrugs (dipivefrin), cyclodextrins, inserts, intravitreal implants.
- Ocusert: pilocarpine + alginic acid between EVA membranes, TiO2 ring, about a week. Lacrisert: HPC rod for dry eye.
- IUDs: inert (Lippes loop) → copper (Cu-T 380A, Multiload 375) → hormonal (progesterone, levonorgestrel). Action: foreign-body reaction, copper spermicidal, progestogen thickens cervical mucus.
