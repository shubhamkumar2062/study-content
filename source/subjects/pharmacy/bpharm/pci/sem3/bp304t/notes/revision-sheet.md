**Definitions that earn 2 marks**

- **Unit operation:** a basic physical step common to many processes (size reduction, drying, filtration).
- **EMC:** moisture content of a solid in equilibrium with air of given humidity and temperature.
- **Critical moisture content:** moisture at which the constant rate period ends.
- **Filter aid:** inert porous powder that keeps the cake porous (kieselguhr, perlite).
- **Corrosion:** destruction of a metal by chemical or electrochemical reaction with its environment.
- **Passivation:** strengthening the oxide film of stainless steel with nitric (or citric) acid.

**Corrosion in five lines**

1. Electrochemical cells form on the metal: anode dissolves (Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻).
2. Cathode: O2 + 2H2O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻, or 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → H2 in acid.
3. Types: uniform, galvanic, pitting, crevice, intergranular, stress cracking, erosion, dezincification.
4. Prevent by material choice, design, coatings, cathodic protection, inhibitors, environment control, passivation.
5. In a galvanic couple the more active metal (zinc, magnesium) corrodes and protects steel.
