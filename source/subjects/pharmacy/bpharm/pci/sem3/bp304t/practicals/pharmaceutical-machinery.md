# Rotary tablet machine, fluidised bed coater, fluid energy mill and dehumidifier

**Aim:** To study and describe the construction, working and applications of pharmaceutical machinery: rotary tablet machine, fluidised bed coater, fluid energy mill and dehumidifier.

> This is a study and description experiment. Do not operate machines without your teacher. Keep hands, hair and loose clothing away from moving parts.

## Principle

Each machine carries out one unit operation used in production: compression (tablet machine), coating (fluidised bed coater), size reduction (fluid energy mill) and air conditioning (dehumidifier). The record should give principle, construction (sketch), working and uses of each.

## Requirements

The machines (or models and charts), record book, pencil and scale.

## Procedure

1. Observe each machine with the teacher; identify every part.
2. Draw a labelled sketch.
3. Note the working steps and the products it is used for.

## Observations

### 1. Rotary tablet machine

**Principle:** powder or granules are compressed between an upper and a lower punch in a die. Many punch–die sets on a rotating turret compress tablets continuously.

**Construction:**

- **Hopper** and **feed frame** (gravity or forced/paddle feeder).
- **Turret (die table)** carrying many dies, with upper and lower punches in tracks.
- **Cams** that raise and lower the punches.
- **Weight-control (fill) cam** and **ejection cam**.
- **Pre-compression and main compression rollers**.
- Take-off blade (scraper) and discharge chute.

```text
     upper punches ↓  ↓  ↓  (moved by upper cam track)
   ════╤══╤══╤══╤══╤══════  turret (die table) rotates
       die die die die
     lower punches ↑  ↑  ↑
   fill → weight adjust → pre-compression → compression → ejection → take-off
```

**Working:** (1) **filling**: lower punch drops and granules fill the die from the feed frame; (2) **weight adjustment**: lower punch rises slightly to push out excess, scraped off; (3) **compression**: punches pass between pressure rollers; (4) **ejection**: lower punch rises and pushes the tablet out; (5) take-off blade guides it to the chute.

**Uses:** large-scale production of tablets, from tens of thousands to several hundred thousand per hour depending on stations and speed. Double-sided machines give two tablets per station per turn.

### 2. Fluidised bed coater

**Principle:** particles or tablets are suspended in an upward stream of hot air while a coating solution is sprayed onto them; the solvent evaporates quickly, leaving a film.

**Construction:** product container with perforated base; air handling unit (fan, filter, heater); **spray nozzle** (top spray, bottom spray with a **Wurster column**, or tangential spray); peristaltic pump for coating liquid; expansion chamber and bag filters.

**Working:** particles are fluidised; coating liquid is atomised onto them; in the Wurster (bottom-spray) type, particles rise through a central partition, are coated, dry in the upper zone and fall back to be coated again, giving a uniform film.

**Uses:** coating of granules, pellets and powders (taste masking, enteric and sustained-release coats); granulation and drying in the same unit.

### 3. Fluid energy mill (jet mill)

**Principle:** attrition and impact between particles moving at very high speed in jets of compressed air or inert gas.

**Construction:** oval loop of pipe with gas nozzles at the bottom, venturi feed inlet, classifier at the outlet leading to a cyclone and bag filter.

**Working:** particles race round the loop, collide and break; fine particles leave through the classifier; coarse ones stay for further grinding. Expanding gas cools the material.

**Uses:** micronising to 1–20 µm: antibiotics, steroids, inhalation drugs, vitamins; thermolabile drugs.

### 4. Dehumidifier

**Principle:** water vapour is removed from air either by **cooling it below its dew point** (refrigerant type) or by **adsorption on a desiccant** (desiccant type).

**Construction:**

- *Refrigerant type:* fan, cooling coil (evaporator), condensate tray and drain, reheating coil, compressor.
- *Desiccant type:* slowly rotating wheel coated with silica gel or lithium chloride; process air passes through one part of the wheel and is dried; a hot reactivation air stream dries the other part.

**Working:** moist room air is drawn in; moisture condenses on the cold coil (or is adsorbed on the desiccant); dry air is returned to the room; RH is set by a humidistat.

**Uses:** maintaining low RH in areas for effervescent granules, hygroscopic powders, gelatin capsule filling, tablet compression and storage; preventing sticking and degradation.

## Result

The construction, working and applications of the rotary tablet machine, fluidised bed coater, fluid energy mill and dehumidifier were studied and recorded with labelled sketches.

## Precautions

1. Machines must be switched off and isolated before parts are examined.
2. Do not insert fingers into dies, turrets or fans.
3. Use guards and follow the standard operating procedure.

## Viva questions

1. **What are the stages of tablet compression in a rotary press?** Filling, weight adjustment, pre-compression and compression, ejection.
2. **What is pre-compression for?** It removes air and gives a first light compression, reducing capping.
3. **What is a Wurster column?** A partition in a bottom-spray fluid bed coater that guides particles past the spray in a regular cycle for uniform coating.
4. **Why is a fluid energy mill suitable for heat-sensitive drugs?** Expansion of compressed gas cools the material, so no heat build-up.
5. **How does a desiccant dehumidifier differ from a refrigerant one?** It adsorbs moisture on silica gel or lithium chloride, and works well at low temperatures and very low RH.
