# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, learn these five topics with full diagrams and one worked numerical each: **factors affecting solubility with the pH–solubility equation**, **distribution law (limitations, applications, extraction)**, **surfactants and HLB**, **classification and analysis of complexes with protein binding**, and **buffer capacity plus isotonicity calculations**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**One-line contrasts examiners like**

| Pair | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Ideal vs real solution | Ideal obeys Raoult's law with no heat or volume change; real deviates (+ or −) |
| Positive vs negative deviation | Weaker vs stronger A–B attraction; minimum vs maximum boiling azeotrope |
| Upper vs lower CST | Miscible above (phenol–water) vs miscible below (triethylamine–water) |
| Crystalline vs amorphous | Sharp mp, ordered, less soluble vs no sharp mp, disordered, more soluble |
| Enantiotropic vs monotropic | Reversible transition vs one form always stable |
| Smectic vs nematic vs cholesteric | Layers vs parallel threads vs twisted layers |
| Physical vs chemical adsorption | Van der Waals, reversible, multilayer vs chemical bonds, irreversible, monolayer |
| Freundlich vs Langmuir | Empirical vs monolayer model with saturation |
| w/o vs o/w emulsifier | HLB 3–6 vs 8–18 |
| Albumin vs α1-acid glycoprotein | Binds acidic drugs vs basic drugs |
| Class I vs Class II isotonic methods | Add NaCl (cryoscopic, E) vs add water then isotonic vehicle (White–Vincent, Sprowls) |

**Practicals in one line each:** solubility by saturation and titration; pKa = pH at half-neutralisation; partition of benzoic acid (Cw/√Cb constant) and iodine (Cc/Cw constant); % NaCl from the rise in phenol–water CST; surface tension by stalagmometer; HLB = 20(1 − S/A); Freundlich and Langmuir plots for acetic acid on charcoal; CMC from the break in surface tension vs log c; PABA–caffeine K from the phase solubility slope; Cu–glycine K from the pH titration formation curve.
