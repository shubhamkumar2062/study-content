# Quality control tests of marketed tablets and capsules (as per IP)

**Aim:** To perform IP quality control tests on a marketed brand of tablets and of capsules and to report whether they comply.

> Quantities are typical for a student batch. Follow your lab manual and your teacher where they differ. Wear goggles and a lab coat. The worked results below are specimen readings for practice.

## Principle

Official tests check that every unit contains the right amount of drug and will release it in the body: uniformity of weight, friability, disintegration and dissolution (with hardness and dimensions as useful non-official checks).

## Requirements

Marketed tablets (e.g. a 500 mg brand) and capsules, analytical balance, vernier calliper, hardness tester, Roche friabilator, IP disintegration apparatus (with discs), dissolution apparatus (basket/paddle), UV spectrophotometer, media.

## Procedure

**Tablets**

1. **Appearance:** note shape, colour, markings, defects.
2. **Thickness and diameter:** measure 10 tablets with a vernier calliper.
3. **Uniformity of weight:** weigh 20 tablets individually; find the average and the % deviation of each.
4. **Hardness:** test 5–10 tablets; note mean and range.
5. **Friability:** take tablets weighing about 6.5 g (if each weighs 650 mg or less; else 10 tablets), de-dust, weigh, rotate 100 times (25 rpm, 4 min), de-dust, reweigh.
6. **Disintegration:** 6 tablets in the basket-rack, water at 37 ± 2 °C; note the time for all to disintegrate (uncoated 15 min, film-coated 30 min, sugar-coated 60 min).
7. **Dissolution:** as per the monograph (apparatus, medium, rpm, time); measure absorbance and calculate % released.

**Capsules**

8. **Uniformity of weight:** weigh an intact capsule, open it, empty fully (wipe shell), weigh the shell; content = intact − shell. Repeat for 20 capsules.
9. **Disintegration:** 6 capsules with discs, water at 37 °C; hard capsules usually within 30 minutes.

## Precautions

1. Use a calibrated balance; handle units with forceps.
2. De-dust tablets before and after friability.
3. Maintain medium temperature at 37 °C.
4. Empty capsules completely without damaging shells.

## Viva questions

1. **IP weight variation for tablets of 80 mg or less?** 10%.
2. **Why is hardness non-official?** It depends on size, shape and purpose of the tablet.
3. **What is Q in dissolution?** Amount of drug (as % of label) that must dissolve in the stated time.
4. **Why are discs used in disintegration?** To keep units submerged and add gentle mechanical action.
5. **What if a friability test shows broken tablets?** The batch fails regardless of % loss.
6. **Content uniformity is needed for which products?** Low-dose units where weight does not reflect drug content.
