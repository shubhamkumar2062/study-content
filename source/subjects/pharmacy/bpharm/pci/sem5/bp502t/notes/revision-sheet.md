# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, prepare these with a flow chart or table each: **granulation methods**, **tablet processing problems and coating defects**, **IP quality control tests of tablets**, **hard and soft gelatin capsule manufacture**, **parenteral production area and QC tests**, and **aerosol propellants and filling**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**Unit 1 — Preformulation**

- Goals: profile the drug, spot problems, choose salt and form, guide dosage form and process.
- Amorphous: faster dissolving, less stable. Stable polymorph: highest melting point, lowest solubility. Detect by XRD, DSC, TGA, hot-stage microscopy, IR.
- Flow: tan θ = h/r; Carr's index = 100(ρt − ρb)/ρt; Hausner = ρt/ρb. Good flow: θ 31–35°, CI 11–15%, HR 1.12–1.18.
- Weak acid S = S0[1 + 10^(pH − pKa)]; log P by octanol–water shake flask.
- Degradation: hydrolysis (aspirin, procaine, penicillins), oxidation (adrenaline, ascorbic acid, oils), racemisation (hyoscyamine → atropine), polymerisation (ampicillin).
- BCS I high/high; II low sol; III low perm; IV both low. High solubility: highest dose in ≤250 ml, pH 1–6.8.

**Unit 2 — Tablets and liquid orals**

| Item | Key facts |
| --- | --- |
| Excipients | Diluent, binder, disintegrant, glidant, lubricant, antiadherent, colour, flavour, sweetener |
| Granulation | Wet (mix → wet mass → screen → dry → size → lubricate); dry (slugging, roller compaction); direct compression |

| Coating defects | Blistering, chipping, cratering, picking, pitting, blooming, blushing, orange peel, cracking, twinning, infilling |
| IP weight variation | ≤80 mg 10%; 80–250 mg 7.5%; ≥250 mg 5% (20 tablets) |
| Friability | 25 rpm × 4 min = 100 revolutions; loss ≤1% |
| Disintegration | Uncoated 15, film 30, sugar 60, dispersible 3, effervescent 5 min; enteric 2 h acid then buffer pH 6.8 |
| Dissolution | Basket/paddle, 900 ml, 37 °C; S1 6 units ≥ Q + 5% |
| Liquids | Simple Syrup IP ≈ 66.7% w/w; Stokes' law; F = Vu/Vo; dry gum 4:2:1 |

**Unit 3 — Capsules and pellets**

- Gelatin A (acid, pork, IEP 7–9), B (alkali, bone, IEP 4.7–5). Hard shell moisture 13–16%.
- Shell making: dip → spin → dry → strip → trim → join.
- Sizes: 000 1.37, 00 0.95, 0 0.68, 1 0.50, 2 0.37, 3 0.30, 4 0.21, 5 0.13 ml.
- Filling: hand plate, auger, dosator, dosing disc. Capsule weight variation: <300 mg 10%; ≥300 mg 7.5%.
- Soft capsule: gelatin + glycerin/sorbitol + water; rotary die (Scherer); BA = g base/g solid; M/g = minims of solid + its base per g.
- Pellets 0.5–1.5 mm: extrusion–spheronisation (MCC), layering, spray drying/congealing, cryopelletisation.

**Unit 4 — Parenterals and ophthalmics**

- Sterile, pyrogen-free, particle-free, stable, isotonic (0.9% NaCl, −0.52 °C).
- WFI by distillation; oily vehicles IM only; no preservative in LVP (≥100 ml).
- Production: wash → depyrogenate (dry heat ~250 °C) → compound → filter (0.22 µm) → fill → seal → sterilise (121 °C, 15 min) → test → pack.
- Clean rooms: Grade A (ISO 5, laminar flow), B, C, D; HEPA ≥ 99.97% at 0.3 µm.
- Lyophilisation: freeze → primary drying (sublimation) → secondary drying.
- Glass Type I borosilicate, II treated soda-lime, III soda-lime. Pull sealing of ampoules.
- QC: sterility (FTM 30–35 °C, SCDM 20–25 °C, 14 days), BET (LAL), rabbit pyrogen, particulate (LVP ≤25/ml ≥10 µm, ≤3/ml ≥25 µm), leaker test (methylene blue).
- Eye: sterile, preserved (benzalkonium chloride + edetate), viscosity agents; ointment base soft paraffin + liquid paraffin + wool fat; metal particle test.

- Aerosol parts: concentrate, propellant, container, valve, actuator. Propellants: CFC (banned), HFA-134a/227ea, hydrocarbons, compressed gases. Systems: two-phase, three-phase, suspension, foam, barrier. Filling: cold, pressure, compressed gas. Tests: flame projection, vapour pressure, spray pattern, dose uniformity, particle size, hot water bath leak test.
- Packaging: primary/secondary/tertiary; glass, plastics, metals, rubber, paper. Interactions: permeation, leaching, sorption, chemical reaction, modification. Container terms: well-closed, tightly closed, hermetic, light-resistant, tamper-evident.
