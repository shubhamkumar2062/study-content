# Last-night revision sheet

If you have only an hour, learn these five answers well: **definitions of misbranded, adulterated and spurious drugs with section 27 penalties**, **schedules G, H, H1, M, N, X, Y**, **constitution and functions of the PCI**, **NDPS definitions, Consultative Committee and National Fund**, and **DPCO 2013 ceiling price with NLEM**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

## NDPS Act — must-know

- s.4 Central Govt (NCB 1986) · s.5 Narcotics Commissioner · **s.6 Consultative Committee: Chairman + up to 20 members, advisory** · **s.7A National Fund for Control of Drug Abuse (1989)** · s.8 prohibition except medical/scientific use · s.9 Central powers · s.10 State powers.
- Narcotic drug = coca leaf, cannabis, opium, poppy straw, manufactured drugs; psychotropic = substances in the Schedule.
- Opium: licensed cultivators (MP, Rajasthan, UP) tender opium to CBN → Government Opium and Alkaloid Works (Ghazipur, Neemuch) → export only by Government.

## Other Acts

- **DMR Act:** s.3 adverts for miscarriage, contraception, sexual pleasure, menstrual disorder, **54 Schedule diseases**; s.4 misleading; s.5 magic remedy (talisman, mantra, kavacha, charm); s.14 exemptions (RMP signboard, scientific book, confidential to doctors, Government); s.7 penalty 6 months (first) / 1 year (subsequent).
- **PCA Act:** s.14–20 experiments; s.15 CPCSEA (now CCSEA); IAEC; 3 Rs (Replacement, Reduction, Refinement) + Rehabilitation; registration; records; s.19 suspend/revoke; s.20 penalty.
- **DPCO 2013:** ceiling price = simple average PTR of brands with ≥1% market share + **16%** retailer margin; revised every **1 April** by **WPI**; non-scheduled MRP rise ≤ **10%/year**; para 19 extraordinary circumstances; NPPA (1997) enforces; **NLEM 2022 = 384 medicines, 27 categories**.
- **MTP Act (2021):** ≤20 weeks 1 RMP; 20–24 weeks 2 RMPs (special categories); >24 weeks foetal abnormality via Medical Board; written consent (guardian for minor); s.5A confidentiality.
- **RTI Act:** ₹10; 30 days; 48 hours for life/liberty; first appeal 30 days; second appeal 90 days to CIC/SIC; penalty ₹250/day up to ₹25,000; s.8 exemptions.
- **IPR:** patent 20 years (novelty, inventive step, industrial application); s.3(d) (Novartis 2013); s.84 compulsory licence (Natco–Bayer 2012); s.107A Bolar; trademark 10 years renewable; copyright life + 60; design 10 + 5; GI 10 renewable.
- **Committees:** Drugs Enquiry (Chopra, 1930–31) → D&C Act; Bhore (1943–46); Mudaliar (1959–61); Hathi (1974–75: 117 essential drugs, generic names, National Drug Authority).
- **Code of ethics:** pharmacist in relation to job, trade, medical profession, profession; pharmacist's oath.
