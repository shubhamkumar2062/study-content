# What did you do? Talking about past events

**Aim:** To describe past events correctly using the simple past and related tenses, and to ask and answer "What did you do?" questions, for example when taking a patient's medication history.

> Language lab activities vary between colleges. Follow your lab teacher and your lab manual where they differ from this guide.

## Principle

When we report what happened (yesterday's lab, last weekend, how a patient took a medicine) we use past tenses. Many errors in Indian English come from mixing present and past forms ("Yesterday I go to the market").

**Tenses for the past**

| Tense | Form | Use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Simple past | verb + -ed or irregular past | Finished action at a stated time | I visited the hospital yesterday. |
| Past continuous | was/were + verb-ing | Action in progress at a past moment | I was reading when the light went off. |
| Present perfect | has/have + past participle | Past action linked to now, no fixed time | I have finished the record. |
| Past perfect | had + past participle | Earlier of two past actions | He had taken the tablet before he ate. |

**Questions and negatives use "did" + base form**

```text
Statement:  She took the tablet.
Question:   Did she take the tablet?        (not "Did she took")
Negative:   She did not take the tablet.
Wh-:        What did you do yesterday?
```

**Common irregular verbs**

| Base | Past | Past participle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| take | took | taken |
| give | gave | given |
| go | went | gone |
| buy | bought | bought |
| write | wrote | written |
| feel | felt | felt |
| forget | forgot | forgotten |
| begin | began | begun |

Use time words to anchor the story: yesterday, last week, two days ago, in 2023, then, after that, finally.

## Requirements

Language lab with headsets and the software module on past events, worksheet of verbs, picture-story cards, voice recorder, timer.

## Procedure

1. Listen to the module's sample narration and underline every past-tense verb in the transcript.
2. Complete the verb worksheet (base → past → past participle).
3. Correct ten given sentences with tense errors.
4. **Speaking:** speak for one minute on "What did you do last weekend?" using at least five past verbs and three time words. Record it.
5. **Pair activity (pharmacy):** a pharmacist takes a medication history. The pharmacist asks: "When did you start this medicine? Did you miss any dose? What did you do when you felt dizzy?" The patient answers in the past tense.
6. Play back recordings and list the tense errors; re-record once.

**Sample medication history**

| Pharmacist | Patient |
| --- | --- |
| When did you start the antihypertensive tablet? | I started it three months ago. |
| Did you miss any doses last week? | Yes, I missed two because I travelled to Delhi. |
| What did you do when you felt dizzy? | I sat down and drank some water. |

**Score (10 marks):** correct tense forms 4, time words and sequence 2, fluency 2, pronunciation of -ed endings 2.

## Result

The student narrated a past event using ___ correct past-tense verbs, corrected ___ of 10 sentences and scored ___ / 10.

## Precautions

1. After "did", always use the base form of the verb.
2. Do not use present perfect with a fixed past time ("I have seen him yesterday" is wrong).
3. Pronounce -ed endings correctly: /t/ after voiceless sounds (helped), /d/ after voiced sounds (played), /ɪd/ after t or d (wanted).
4. Keep events in the order they happened.
5. In medication history, record exactly what the patient said.

## Viva questions

1. **Which tense describes a finished action at a known past time?** Simple past.
2. **Correct: "Did she went home?"** "Did she go home?"
3. **When do we use past perfect?** For the earlier of two past actions.
4. **Past and past participle of "take"?** Took, taken.
5. **How is -ed pronounced in "wanted"?** /ɪd/.
6. **Why is past tense important in a medication history?** It shows clearly when the patient started, stopped or missed medicines.
