# Effective writing: letters, notices and reports

**Aim:** To write a formal letter, a notice and a short report in the correct format, using clear, purposeful language suitable for a pharmacy workplace.

> Language lab activities vary between colleges. Follow your lab teacher and your lab manual where they differ from this guide.

## Principle

Effective writing reaches the right reader, gives the main point quickly, and is organised so the reader can act on it. Before writing, answer four questions: **Who is the reader? What is the purpose? What must they know or do? What is the right format?**

**Planning steps**

```text
Purpose → Audience → Collect points → Order them → Draft → Revise → Proofread
```

**Formats**

| Document | Use | Parts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formal letter | Applications, complaints, orders, requests to outside persons | Sender's address, date, receiver's address, subject line, salutation, body (purpose, details, request), complimentary close, signature, name |
| Notice | Informing a group about an event or rule | Organisation name, "NOTICE", date, heading, body (what, when, where, who), name and designation of issuer |
| Report | Recording what happened or was found | Title, to/from/date, introduction (purpose), findings, conclusion/recommendations, signature |
| Memo | Short internal message within an organisation | To, from, date, subject, message |

**Body of a formal letter in three parts:** (1) why you are writing; (2) the details; (3) what you want the reader to do, with a polite close ("Yours faithfully" when the salutation is "Dear Sir/Madam"; "Yours sincerely" when you use the person's name).

**Style:** formal tone, no contractions, short paragraphs, active voice, specific facts (dates, quantities, batch numbers).

## Requirements

Language lab with the effective writing module (or worksheets), format samples, A4 sheets, timer.

## Procedure

1. Study the sample letter, notice and report in the module and label their parts.
2. **Task 1 (15 min):** write a formal letter from a hospital pharmacy to a distributor about a short supply of an ordered antibiotic.
3. **Task 2 (10 min):** write a notice (in a box, 50–70 words) for students announcing a blood-donation camp in the college.
4. **Task 3 (15 min):** write a 150-word report on a visit to a local pharmaceutical store or hospital pharmacy.
5. Exchange work with a partner and check format, clarity and errors using the rubric.
6. Revise and submit the final copies.

## Observations

**Sample letter (body shown)**

| Part | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | Short supply against order no. HP/245 dated 10 March |
| Salutation | Dear Sir/Madam, |
| Paragraph 1 | We received your consignment against the above order on 14 March. |
| Paragraph 2 | The invoice shows 100 boxes of Amoxicillin 500 mg capsules, but only 80 boxes were delivered. The remaining items were received in full and in good condition. |
| Paragraph 3 | Kindly send the remaining 20 boxes within three working days or issue a credit note. |
| Close | Yours faithfully, (signature) Chief Pharmacist |

**Sample notice**

```text
                    ABC COLLEGE OF PHARMACY
                           NOTICE
 Date: 5 August                         Blood Donation Camp
 A blood donation camp will be held in the college auditorium on 12 August
 from 10 am to 2 pm. Healthy students above 18 years may donate.
 Register with the NSS office by 10 August.
                                        Secretary, NSS Unit
```

**Rubric (10 marks):** correct format 3, clarity and order 3, language accuracy 2, tone 2.

## Result

## Precautions

1. State the purpose in the first lines.
2. Use the correct salutation and matching complimentary close.
3. Keep notices short and inside a box; include date, time and venue.
4. Use facts, not opinions, in reports.
5. Proofread names, dates, quantities and drug strengths.

## Viva questions

1. **What is a subject line in a letter?** A one-line summary of the letter's purpose placed before the salutation.
2. **"Yours faithfully" or "Yours sincerely" after "Dear Sir"?** Yours faithfully.
3. **What must a notice contain?** Issuer's name, date, heading, what, when, where, and who signs it.
4. **What are the main parts of a report?** Title, introduction, findings, conclusion/recommendations.
5. **What is a memo?** A short internal written message within an organisation.
6. **Why plan before writing?** To decide purpose, reader and order of points, so the message is clear.
