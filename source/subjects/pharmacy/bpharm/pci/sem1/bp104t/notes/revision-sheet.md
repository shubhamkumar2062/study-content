# Last-night revision sheet

## Unit 1: Impurities

- IP editions: 1955 (first), 1966, 1985, 1996, 2007, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022. Prepared now by the Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission, Ghaziabad. Bengal Pharmacopoeia 1844.
- Sources: raw materials, manufacturing (reagents, solvents, vessels, intermediates, side reactions), atmosphere, manufacturing defects, storage (container, instability, efflorescence, deliquescence), adulteration.
- Limit test = semi-quantitative; test vs standard, same conditions, Nessler cylinders.

| Test | Key reagent | Result |

| Sulphate | Barium sulphate reagent (BaCl2 + K2SO4 seed + alcohol) + dil. HCl | White turbidity |

- Older standards (check current IP): chloride 1 ml 0.05845% NaCl; sulphate 1 ml 0.1089% K2SO4; iron 2 ml standard iron solution (20 µg Fe/ml).
- Modified tests: remove interference first (bicarbonate: extra acid; KMnO4: decolourise and filter).

## Unit 2: Buffers, electrolytes, dental

- pH = pKa + log(salt/acid); βmax = 0.576 C at pH = pKa; β = ΔB/ΔpH.
- Isotonic = 0.9% NaCl, freezing point −0.52 °C.
- W = (0.52 − a)/b (b = 0.576 for NaCl). E = 17 Liso/M. V = w × E × 111.1.
- 1% boric acid (E 0.50): add 0.40 g NaCl per 100 ml.
- Na⁺ main ECF cation; K⁺ main ICF cation; Ca²⁺ clotting, muscle, bone.
- NaCl assay: AgNO3 (1 ml 0.1 M ≡ 5.844 mg). Calcium gluconate: glucose → gluconic acid + CaCO3; EDTA with mordant black II (1 ml 0.05 M ≡ 22.42 mg).
- ORS (WHO low-osmolarity, g/L): NaCl 2.6, glucose 13.5, KCl 1.5, trisodium citrate 2.9; 245 mOsm/L.
- Blood pH 7.35–7.45; HCO3⁻ : H2CO3 = 20 : 1.
- Fluoride → fluorapatite (acid resistant); excess → fluorosis. Desensitisers: KNO3, SrCl2, fluorides.
- ZnO + eugenol → zinc eugenolate (temporary filling).

## Unit 3: GI agents and antimicrobials

- NH4Cl: NH3 + HCl; formol titration (1 ml 0.1 M NaOH ≡ 5.349 mg).
- Dil. HCl about 10% w/v for achlorhydria, through a straw.
- Antacid ideal: not absorbed, no CO2, no rebound, fast and long, no bowel upset. Al(OH)3 (constipating) + Mg(OH)2 (laxative).
- NaHCO3: Solvay process; assay with HCl, methyl orange (1 ml 1 M ≡ 84.01 mg); risks: alkalosis, CO2, Na load.
- Saline cathartics: MgSO4 (also eclampsia), Na2HPO4 (enemas). Kaolin = adsorbent; bentonite swells.
- KMnO4: MnO2 + KOH + KClO3 → K2MnO4 → KMnO4; 1 in 5,000–10,000.
- Boric acid: borax + acid; titrate with NaOH after glycerin/mannitol (1 ml 1 M ≡ 61.83 mg).
- H2O2: BaO2 + H2SO4; KMnO4 assay (1 ml 0.02 M ≡ 1.701 mg); 20 vol ≈ 6% w/v.
- Chlorinated lime: Cl2 + slaked lime; available chlorine by iodometry in acetic acid (1 ml 0.1 M thiosulphate ≡ 3.545 mg).
- Iodine: Lugol's = 5% I2 + 10% KI; povidone-iodine = iodophor.

## Unit 4: Miscellaneous

- KI: I2 + KOH → KI + KIO3; reduce with charcoal; assay KIO3 in strong HCl, CHCl3 end point (ICl).
- CuSO4: CuO + H2SO4; iodometry (2Cu²⁺ + 4I⁻ → 2CuI + I2); 1 ml 0.1 M ≡ 24.97 mg.
- Rochelle salt = cathartic (tartar emetic is antimony potassium tartrate).
- FeSO4: Fe + dil. H2SO4 (iron in excess); ceric ammonium sulphate + ferroin (1 ml 0.1 M ≡ 27.80 mg); ~20% Fe. Ferrous gluconate ~12% Fe, gentler.
- Na2S2O3: Na2SO3 + S; iodimetry (1 ml 0.05 M I2 ≡ 24.82 mg).
- Cyanide: NaNO2 → methaemoglobin → cyanmethaemoglobin; Na2S2O3 + CN⁻ → SCN⁻ (rhodanese).
- Activated charcoal: not for acids, alkalis, iron, lithium, alcohols.
- Astringents: ZnSO4 (eye drops, zinc supplement), potash alum (styptic, water purification, vaccine adjuvant).

## Unit 5: Radiopharmaceuticals

| | α | β | γ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nature | He nucleus | Electron/positron | Photon |
| Charge | +2 | ∓1 | 0 |
| Ionising | Highest | Medium | Lowest |
| Penetration | Paper | Aluminium | Lead/concrete |

- N = N0 e^(−λt); t½ = 0.693/λ; mean life = 1.44 t½; 1/Te = 1/Tp + 1/Tb.
- 1 Ci = 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq; 1 Gy = 100 rad; 1 Sv = 100 rem.
- Detectors: ionisation chamber, proportional, GM counter (dead time), scintillation NaI(Tl), liquid scintillation, film badge, TLD.
- t½: Tc-99m 6 h; I-131 8 days; I-125 ~60 days; P-32 14.3 days; Co-60 5.3 years; F-18 110 min.
- I-131: Te-130 + neutron; β for therapy, γ for imaging; thyroid uptake, hyperthyroidism, thyroid cancer; not in pregnancy.
- Safety: time, distance (inverse square), shielding; ALARA; no mouth pipetting; decay-in-storage.
