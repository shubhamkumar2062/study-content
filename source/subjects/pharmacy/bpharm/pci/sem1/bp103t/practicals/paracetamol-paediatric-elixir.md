# Paracetamol Paediatric Elixir

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 60 ml of Paracetamol Paediatric Elixir (120 mg in 5 ml).

> The amount of paracetamol follows from the strength. Take the quantities of the cosolvents, syrup and flavour from your laboratory manual.

## Principle

Paracetamol is an **analgesic and antipyretic**. It is only sparingly soluble in water, so a strength of 120 mg in 5 ml (2.4% w/v) cannot be dissolved in water alone. The elixir uses **cosolvents** (alcohol, propylene glycol, glycerin) to keep the drug in solution, and syrup and flavour to mask its bitter taste. This is an example of solubility enhancement by cosolvency.

## Typical ingredients and roles

| Ingredient | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Paracetamol | Analgesic, antipyretic |
| Alcohol (ethanol) | Cosolvent, preservative |
| Propylene glycol | Cosolvent |
| Glycerin | Cosolvent, sweetener |
| Syrup / sorbitol | Sweetener, vehicle |
| Flavour and permitted colour | Acceptability |
| Purified water | Vehicle (to volume) |

## Requirements

Paracetamol, ethanol, propylene glycol, glycerin, syrup, flavour, colour, purified water, beakers, glass rod, measuring cylinder, filter paper, amber bottle.

## Procedure

1. Weigh 1.44 g of paracetamol (see calculation).
2. Dissolve it in the alcohol and propylene glycol with stirring (warm gently if needed).
3. Add the glycerin and syrup and mix well.
4. Add flavour and colour.
5. Make up to 60 ml with purified water, adding it slowly with stirring.
6. Filter if needed and pack in an amber bottle.

## Observations and calculations

Clear, coloured, sweet, flavoured liquid.

Paracetamol needed = 120 mg × 60 ml ÷ 5 ml = 1440 mg = **1.44 g**.
Strength = 1.44 × 100 ÷ 60 = **2.4% w/v**.

## Result

60 ml of Paracetamol Paediatric Elixir (120 mg/5 ml) was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Paracetamol Paediatric Elixir, 120 mg in 5 ml, 60 ml. Dose: as directed by the physician (based on the child's weight). Do not exceed the stated dose. Keep out of reach of children.

## Precautions

1. Dissolve paracetamol in the cosolvents before adding water, or it may precipitate.
2. Add water slowly with stirring.
3. Use a measuring cup or oral syringe for dosing.

## Viva questions

1. **Why are cosolvents needed?** The required strength is above the water solubility of paracetamol.
2. **What is the usual paediatric strength?** 120 mg in 5 ml.
3. **Why is overdose dangerous?** Large doses damage the liver.
4. **Why is an elixir used for children?** Easy to swallow and dose by volume, with taste masked.
5. **Name another way to enhance solubility.** Complexation, surfactant solubilisation or salt formation.
