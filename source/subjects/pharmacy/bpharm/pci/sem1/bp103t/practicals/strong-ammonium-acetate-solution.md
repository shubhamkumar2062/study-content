# Strong Solution of Ammonium Acetate

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense Strong Solution of Ammonium Acetate.

> Take the official quantities from your laboratory manual. The calculation below shows how the reacting amounts are related.

## Principle

Ammonium acetate is made **in situ by chemical reaction** (neutralisation) of ammonium bicarbonate with acetic acid:

NH4HCO3 + CH3COOH → CH3COONH4 + H2O + CO2↑

Carbon dioxide is given off as effervescence. Ammonium bicarbonate partly loses ammonia on storage, so its content is uncertain; the final solution is therefore adjusted to **neutrality** with strong ammonia solution (or acetic acid) using litmus or a pH check. The strong solution is diluted to make Dilute Solution of Ammonium Acetate, used as a **diaphoretic** and **expectorant** in mixtures.

## Ingredients and roles

| Ingredient | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Ammonium bicarbonate | Reactant (source of ammonium) |
| Glacial acetic acid | Reactant (source of acetate) |
| Strong ammonia solution | Final neutralisation |
| Purified water | Solvent |

## Requirements

Ammonium bicarbonate, glacial acetic acid, strong ammonia solution, purified water, large beaker (to allow for frothing), glass rod, measuring cylinder, litmus paper, bottle.

## Procedure

1. Dilute the glacial acetic acid with part of the purified water in a large beaker.
2. Add the ammonium bicarbonate **in small portions** with stirring, waiting for effervescence to subside each time.
3. When all is added and effervescence has stopped, test with litmus. Add strong ammonia solution drop by drop until the solution is just neutral (or very slightly alkaline as directed).
4. Make up to volume with purified water.
5. Transfer to a well-closed bottle.

## Observations and calculations

Clear, colourless liquid with a faint odour of ammonia and acetic acid.

Molecular weights: NH4HCO3 = 79.06; CH3COOH = 60.05 (1 : 1 reaction).
Acetic acid needed for 10 g ammonium bicarbonate = 10 × 60.05 ÷ 79.06 = **7.60 g**. Glacial acetic acid is used in slight excess and the excess is neutralised with ammonia.

## Result

Strong Solution of Ammonium Acetate was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Strong Solution of Ammonium Acetate. For dispensing use only (to be diluted). Store in a well-closed container in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Add ammonium bicarbonate slowly to avoid frothing over.
2. Use a large vessel.
3. Allow all CO2 to escape before closing the bottle.
4. Handle glacial acetic acid and strong ammonia in a fume hood or ventilated area.

## Viva questions

1. **Type of preparation?** Prepared by chemical reaction (neutralisation).
2. **Why is effervescence seen?** Carbon dioxide is released from the bicarbonate.
3. **Why is strong ammonia added at the end?** Ammonium bicarbonate loses ammonia on storage, so the final solution is adjusted to neutrality.
4. **Use?** Diluted to Dilute Solution of Ammonium Acetate, a diaphoretic and expectorant.
5. **Why not close the bottle at once?** Trapped CO2 could build pressure.
