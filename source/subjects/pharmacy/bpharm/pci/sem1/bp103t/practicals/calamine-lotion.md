# Calamine Lotion

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 100 ml of Calamine Lotion.

## Principle

Calamine lotion is a **suspension for external use** that soothes itching, sunburn and mild skin irritation. Calamine and zinc oxide are **indiffusible** powders, so a suspending agent is needed: **bentonite** forms a gel-like structure that slows settling, and **sodium citrate** acts as a flocculating agent (controlled flocculation) so the sediment stays loose and easily redispersed. Glycerin keeps the skin moist and helps the film stick; liquefied phenol is an antipruritic and preservative.

## Formula

| Ingredient | For 100 ml | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calamine | 15 g | Soothing, mild astringent |
| Zinc oxide | 5 g | Astringent, protective |
| Bentonite | 3 g | Suspending agent |
| Sodium citrate | 0.5 g | Flocculating agent |
| Liquefied phenol | 0.5 ml | Antipruritic, preservative |
| Glycerin | 5 ml | Humectant, helps adhesion |
| Rose water (or purified water) | to 100 ml | Vehicle |

## Requirements

Calamine, zinc oxide, bentonite, sodium citrate, liquefied phenol, glycerin, rose water, mortar and pestle, sieve, measuring cylinder, wide-mouthed bottle.

## Procedure

1. Sift calamine, zinc oxide and bentonite and mix them in a mortar.
2. Dissolve sodium citrate in about 70 ml of rose water.
3. Triturate the powders with the glycerin and a little of the citrate solution to form a smooth paste.
4. Add the rest of the citrate solution gradually with trituration.
5. Add the liquefied phenol, transfer to a measuring cylinder and make up to 100 ml with rose water.
6. Pack in a wide-mouthed bottle with space for shaking.

## Observations

Pink, opaque suspension that settles slowly and redisperses easily on shaking.

## Result

100 ml of Calamine Lotion was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Calamine Lotion, 100 ml. **Shake well before use. For external use only.** Apply with cotton wool to the affected area. Store in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Sift the powders to avoid grittiness.
2. Wet the powders with glycerin into a smooth paste before adding the vehicle.
3. Handle liquefied phenol carefully; it is corrosive.

## Viva questions

1. **What gives calamine its pink colour?** A small amount of ferric oxide with the zinc oxide/carbonate.
2. **Role of bentonite?** Suspending agent.
3. **Role of sodium citrate?** Flocculating agent, giving a loose, easily redispersed sediment.
4. **Why is it a lotion and not a liniment?** It is applied without friction.
5. **Are calamine and zinc oxide diffusible?** No, they are indiffusible, so a suspending agent is needed.
