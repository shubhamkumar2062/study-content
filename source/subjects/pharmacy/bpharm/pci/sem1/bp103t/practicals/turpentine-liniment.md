# Turpentine Liniment

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 100 ml of Turpentine Liniment.

## Principle

Turpentine liniment is a **rubefacient and counter-irritant** applied with friction to relieve muscle pain, sprains and stiffness. It is an **O/W emulsion** of turpentine oil in water, emulsified by **soft soap** (a potassium soap). Camphor is dissolved in the turpentine oil and adds counter-irritant action. The oil (with camphor) is added gradually to the soap–water mixture so that a creamy emulsion forms.

## Formula

| Ingredient | For 100 ml | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Turpentine oil | 65 ml | Counter-irritant, rubefacient |
| Camphor | 5 g | Counter-irritant, mild analgesic |
| Soft soap | 7.5 g | Emulsifying agent (O/W) |
| Purified water | 22.5 ml | External phase |

## Requirements

Turpentine oil, camphor, soft soap, purified water, mortar and pestle (or bottle), measuring cylinder, beaker, glass rod, coloured fluted bottle.

## Procedure

1. Dissolve the camphor in the turpentine oil.
2. Triturate the soft soap with the water in a mortar to a smooth mixture.
3. Add the camphor–turpentine solution in small portions with continuous, vigorous trituration (or shake in a bottle) until a thick creamy emulsion forms.
4. Transfer to a coloured, fluted bottle.

## Observations

White to creamy, thick emulsion with the odour of turpentine and camphor; dilutes with water (O/W).

## Result

100 ml of Turpentine Liniment was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Turpentine Liniment, 100 ml. **For external use only. Shake well before use.** Rub into the affected area. Do not apply to broken skin. Keep away from flames.

## Precautions

1. Add the oil to the soap mixture gradually with vigorous mixing.
2. Turpentine oil is flammable: keep away from flames.
3. Dispense in a fluted bottle to distinguish it as an external preparation.

## Viva questions

1. **Type of emulsion?** Oil-in-water.
2. **Emulsifying agent?** Soft soap (potassium soap).
3. **What is a rubefacient?** A substance that reddens the skin by increasing blood flow.
4. **Why not on broken skin?** It causes pain and irritation and may be absorbed.
5. **Why a fluted bottle?** It warns by touch that the contents are for external use.
