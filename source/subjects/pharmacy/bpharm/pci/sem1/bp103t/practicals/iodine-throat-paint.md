# Compound Iodine Paint (Mandl's Throat Paint)

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 50 ml of Compound Iodine Paint (Mandl's paint).

> Iodine 1.25% w/v and potassium iodide 2.5% w/v are the usual strengths. Take the small amounts of water, alcohol and peppermint oil from your laboratory manual.

## Principle

Throat paints are **viscous** liquids applied to the throat with a brush or swab. **Glycerin** is the base: it is viscous, so it clings to the mucosa, and it is sweet and mildly antiseptic. Iodine is an antiseptic but is almost insoluble in water and glycerin. It dissolves readily in a concentrated solution of potassium iodide because it forms the soluble **triiodide** complex:

I2 + KI ⇌ KI3

So iodine is always dissolved in a small volume of concentrated KI solution before the glycerin is added. Peppermint oil gives flavour and alcohol helps dissolve it.

## Formula (per 100 ml)

| Ingredient | For 100 ml | For 50 ml | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Iodine | 1.25 g | 0.625 g | Antiseptic |
| Potassium iodide | 2.5 g | 1.25 g | Solubilises iodine (KI3) |
| Purified water | small volume (manual) | half | Dissolves KI |
| Peppermint oil, alcohol | as in manual | half | Flavour; solvent for oil |
| Glycerin | to 100 ml | to 50 ml | Viscous base |

## Requirements

Iodine, potassium iodide, purified water, peppermint oil, alcohol, glycerin, glass mortar or beaker, glass rod, measuring cylinder, amber glass bottle with glass stopper, brush.

## Procedure

1. Dissolve 1.25 g potassium iodide in the minimum quantity of purified water.
2. Add 0.625 g iodine (weighed on a watch glass, not paper) and stir until completely dissolved.
3. Dissolve the peppermint oil in the alcohol and add to the solution.
4. Add glycerin gradually with stirring and make up to 50 ml with glycerin.
5. Transfer to an amber glass bottle.

## Observations

Dark brown, viscous liquid with odour of iodine and peppermint.

## Result

50 ml of Compound Iodine Paint was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Compound Iodine Paint, 50 ml. **For external use only** (apply to the throat with a brush). Not to be swallowed. Store in a cool place, protected from light.

## Precautions

1. Dissolve iodine in a concentrated KI solution before adding glycerin; iodine does not dissolve in glycerin or dilute KI.
2. Weigh iodine on a glass surface; it corrodes metal and stains paper.
3. Use glass-stoppered amber bottles (iodine attacks rubber and cork).

## Viva questions

1. **Why is KI used?** To dissolve iodine by forming soluble potassium triiodide.
2. **Why is glycerin the base?** It is viscous, adheres to the throat, sweet and mildly antiseptic.
3. **Why are metal spatulas avoided?** Iodine reacts with and corrodes metals.
4. **What is a throat paint?** A viscous liquid applied to the throat or mouth with a brush.
5. **Uses of Mandl's paint?** Sore throat, tonsillitis and pharyngitis as an antiseptic.
