# Compound Syrup of Ferrous Phosphate (Chemical Food)

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense Compound Syrup of Ferrous Phosphate BPC.

> This is an old official formula with several ingredients. Take the exact quantities from your laboratory manual; the notes below explain the role of each ingredient and the method.

## Principle

Compound Syrup of Ferrous Phosphate is a **haematinic and tonic** syrup supplying iron, calcium, potassium, sodium and phosphate. Iron is present in the **ferrous** state, which is better absorbed but easily oxidised to the ferric state. Two features protect it:

- **Phosphoric acid** keeps the phosphates in solution (they are insoluble at neutral pH).
- **Sucrose** in high concentration acts as a reducing and protective medium that slows oxidation of ferrous iron, and also preserves the syrup.

Cochineal gives a red colour and orange flower water gives flavour.

## Typical ingredients and roles

| Ingredient | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Ferrous phosphate (formed from iron and phosphoric acid) | Source of iron (haematinic) |
| Calcium phosphate | Source of calcium and phosphate |
| Potassium and sodium phosphates | Sources of potassium, sodium and phosphate |
| Dilute phosphoric acid | Keeps phosphates dissolved |
| Sucrose | Sweetener, preservative, protects ferrous iron |
| Cochineal | Colour |
| Orange flower water | Flavour |
| Purified water | Vehicle |

## Requirements

The ingredients above, beakers, glass rod, water bath, measuring cylinder, filter paper, amber bottle.

## Procedure

1. Dissolve the phosphate salts in the dilute phosphoric acid and a little purified water, warming gently if needed.
2. Prepare or dissolve the ferrous component as directed and add it to the acid phosphate solution.
3. Add the sucrose and dissolve with gentle warming (avoid long heating).
4. Add the cochineal colour and orange flower water.
5. Cool, filter if needed, make up to volume with purified water.
6. Fill completely into a clean amber bottle and close tightly.

## Observations

Red, clear, sweet syrup with an orange flower odour.

## Result

Compound Syrup of Ferrous Phosphate was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Compound Syrup of Ferrous Phosphate. Dose: as directed by the physician. Store in a cool place, protected from light, in a tightly closed bottle.

## Precautions

1. Minimise contact with air and avoid prolonged heating, which oxidises ferrous iron.
2. Use amber bottles filled to the neck.
3. Dissolve the phosphates in acid before adding the sugar.

## Viva questions

1. **Why is the iron used in the ferrous form?** Ferrous iron is better absorbed than ferric iron.
2. **What is the role of phosphoric acid?** It keeps the calcium and iron phosphates in solution.
3. **How does sugar protect the iron?** The concentrated sugar solution limits dissolved oxygen and slows oxidation of ferrous to ferric iron.
4. **Why is the common name "Chemical Food"?** It was used as a mineral tonic supplying iron, calcium and phosphorus.
5. **What colour change shows deterioration?** Darkening or precipitation as ferrous iron oxidises to ferric compounds.
