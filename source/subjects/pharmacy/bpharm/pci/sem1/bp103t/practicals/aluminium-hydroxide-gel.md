# Aluminium Hydroxide Gel

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense Aluminium Hydroxide Gel.

> Take the official quantities from your laboratory manual. The calculation shows the reacting amounts.

## Principle

Aluminium hydroxide gel is an **antacid**. It is a suspension of hydrated aluminium hydroxide prepared by **double decomposition** of potash alum with sodium carbonate:

2KAl(SO4)2 + 3Na2CO3 + 3H2O → 2Al(OH)3↓ + K2SO4 + 3Na2SO4 + 3CO2↑

The precipitate is a white, gelatinous solid. Soluble by-products (potassium and sodium sulphates) are removed by washing. Precipitation gives very small particles that form a **two-phase gel** (a structured suspension). Carbon dioxide is given off, so mixing is done slowly in a large vessel.

## Ingredients and roles

| Ingredient | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Potash alum, KAl(SO4)2·12H2O | Source of aluminium |
| Sodium carbonate | Precipitant |
| Peppermint oil, saccharin (in some formulas) | Flavour, sweetener |
| Preservative | Microbial protection |
| Purified water | Vehicle |

## Requirements

Potash alum, sodium carbonate, purified water, large beakers, glass rod, water bath, muslin, measuring cylinder, wide-mouthed bottle.

## Procedure

1. Dissolve the alum in hot purified water.
2. Dissolve the sodium carbonate separately in hot water.
3. Add the alum solution slowly to the carbonate solution with constant stirring; allow effervescence to subside.
4. Let the precipitate settle; decant; wash the precipitate repeatedly with hot water until the washings are free from sulphate (barium chloride test).
5. Transfer the washed gel to a measuring cylinder, add flavour and preservative, make up to volume and shake.

## Observations and calculations

White, viscous, translucent to opaque suspension.

Molecular weights: KAl(SO4)2·12H2O 474.4; Na2CO3 (anhydrous) 106.0; Al(OH)3 78.0.
For 10 g alum: Na2CO3 needed = 10 × (3 × 106.0) ÷ (2 × 474.4) = **3.35 g anhydrous** (or 9.05 g of the decahydrate); Al(OH)3 formed = 10 × 78.0 ÷ 474.4 = **1.64 g**.

## Result

Aluminium Hydroxide Gel was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Aluminium Hydroxide Gel. **Shake well before use.** Dose: as directed by the physician. Store in a cool place; do not freeze.

## Precautions

1. Mix slowly in a large vessel because of CO2 evolution.
2. Wash the precipitate free from sulphate.
3. Do not let the gel dry out, which reduces its reactivity.

## Viva questions

1. **Method of preparation?** Double decomposition.
2. **What gas is evolved?** Carbon dioxide.
3. **Common side effect of aluminium antacids?** Constipation (often combined with magnesium salts, which are laxative).
4. **Why is the gel washed?** To remove soluble sulphates.
5. **What is a two-phase gel?** A gel in which small discrete particles form the network, such as aluminium hydroxide gel.
