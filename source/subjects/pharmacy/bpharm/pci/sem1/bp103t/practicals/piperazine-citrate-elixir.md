# Piperazine Citrate Elixir

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 50 ml of Piperazine Citrate Elixir.

> The quantities below are a commonly used laboratory formula. Follow your manual where it differs.

## Principle

An **elixir** is a clear, sweetened, flavoured liquid for oral use. Piperazine citrate is an **anthelmintic** for roundworm (Ascaris) and threadworm (Enterobius) infections. It is freely soluble in water, so the elixir is a simple solution; glycerin and syrup sweeten it, add body and help keep it clear. The usual strength gives about 750 mg of piperazine hydrate in each 5 ml (18.75 g of piperazine citrate per 100 ml).

## Formula

| Ingredient | For 100 ml | For 50 ml | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Piperazine citrate | 18.75 g | 9.375 g | Anthelmintic |
| Glycerin | 5 ml | 2.5 ml | Sweetener, cosolvent, viscosity |
| Syrup | 62.5 ml | 31.25 ml | Sweetener, vehicle |
| Flavour and colour | q.s. | q.s. | Acceptability |
| Purified water | to 100 ml | to 50 ml | Vehicle |

## Requirements

Piperazine citrate, glycerin, syrup, flavour, purified water, beaker, glass rod, measuring cylinder, funnel, filter paper, amber bottle.

## Procedure

1. Weigh the piperazine citrate and dissolve it in about 10 ml of purified water.
2. Add the glycerin and syrup and mix.
3. Add flavour and colour and stir.
4. Make up to 50 ml with purified water.
5. Filter if not clear; transfer to a clean bottle.

## Observations and calculations

Clear, sweet, flavoured liquid.

Strength check: 18.75 g per 100 ml = 18.75 × 5 ÷ 100 = **0.9375 g of piperazine citrate per 5 ml** (equivalent to about 750 mg piperazine hydrate).

## Result

50 ml of Piperazine Citrate Elixir was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Piperazine Citrate Elixir, 50 ml. Dose: as directed by the physician. Store in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Weigh the drug accurately.
2. Dissolve the drug fully before adding syrup.
3. Keep the container well closed.

## Viva questions

1. **What is an elixir?** A clear, sweetened, flavoured (usually hydroalcoholic) liquid for oral use.
2. **What is piperazine used for?** Roundworm and threadworm infections; it paralyses the worms, which are passed out.
3. **Role of glycerin?** Sweetener, cosolvent and viscosity builder.
4. **How does an elixir differ from a syrup?** An elixir is less sweet and less viscous and usually contains alcohol.
5. **Why filter the elixir?** Elixirs must be clear.
