# Terpin Hydrate Linctus

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense Terpin Hydrate Linctus.

> Take the exact quantities from your laboratory manual (IP 1966 formula). The notes explain the role of each ingredient and the order of mixing.

## Principle

A **linctus** is a viscous, sweet liquid used to relieve **cough**. It is sipped slowly and swallowed without water so that it coats the throat. **Terpin hydrate** is an expectorant that loosens bronchial secretions. It is only slightly soluble in water but dissolves in alcohol and is helped by glycerin, so the linctus is a **hydroalcoholic solution**: the drug is dissolved in the alcoholic part first and the viscous sweet base is added afterwards.

## Typical ingredients and roles

| Ingredient | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Terpin hydrate | Expectorant |
| Alcohol | Solvent for terpin hydrate, preservative |
| Glycerin | Cosolvent, demulcent, sweetener |
| Syrup | Viscous, sweet base; soothes the throat |
| Flavour (for example orange or lemon) | Acceptability |
| Purified water | To volume (if in the formula) |

## Requirements

Terpin hydrate, alcohol, glycerin, syrup, flavour, mortar and pestle or beaker, glass rod, measuring cylinder, amber bottle.

## Procedure

1. Weigh the terpin hydrate and dissolve it in the alcohol with stirring (warm gently on a water bath if needed, keeping the beaker covered).
2. Add the glycerin and mix.
3. Add the syrup gradually with constant stirring so the drug does not separate.
4. Add the flavour and make up to volume with syrup (or the vehicle given).
5. Transfer to a clean, dry amber bottle.

## Observations

Clear, viscous, sweet liquid with an odour of the flavour.

## Result

Terpin Hydrate Linctus was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Terpin Hydrate Linctus. Dose: as directed by the physician; sip slowly and do not dilute. Store in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Dissolve terpin hydrate in alcohol before adding the aqueous or syrup part.
2. Avoid loss of alcohol by evaporation (cover while warming).
3. Add the syrup slowly with stirring.

## Viva questions

1. **What is a linctus?** A viscous, sweet oral liquid for cough, sipped slowly to coat the throat.
2. **Why should a linctus not be diluted?** Dilution reduces its soothing (demulcent) effect on the throat.
3. **Action of terpin hydrate?** Expectorant.
4. **Why is alcohol used?** Terpin hydrate is poorly soluble in water but soluble in alcohol.
5. **Name another linctus.** Simple linctus or codeine linctus.
