# Non-staining Iodine Ointment with Methyl Salicylate

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense Non-staining Iodine Ointment with Methyl Salicylate.

> Take the official quantities from your laboratory manual (BPC formula). The notes explain the principle and method.

## Principle

Ordinary iodine preparations stain the skin and clothes brown. In this ointment iodine is **combined chemically with a fixed oil** by gentle heating in a closed vessel. The iodine adds to the unsaturated fatty acid chains of the oil, and the free brown colour of iodine is discharged, so the product does **not stain**. This is the **chemical reaction method** of preparing ointments. The iodised oil is then mixed with a paraffin base, and **methyl salicylate** is added as a counter-irritant and analgesic. The ointment is used for its counter-irritant and mild antiseptic effect in sprains and stiff joints.

## Typical ingredients and roles

| Ingredient | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Iodine | Antiseptic, counter-irritant |
| Fixed oil (for example arachis oil) | Combines with iodine |
| Soft paraffin | Base |
| Methyl salicylate | Counter-irritant, analgesic |

## Requirements

Iodine, fixed oil, soft paraffin, methyl salicylate, wide-mouthed glass-stoppered bottle or closed flask, water bath, thermometer, porcelain dish, glass rod, spatula, ointment jar.

## Procedure

1. Weigh iodine on a watch glass and place it with the oil in a glass-stoppered container.
2. Heat on a water bath at the temperature given in your manual, shaking occasionally, until the iodine has combined with the oil and the brown colour has disappeared.
3. Melt the soft paraffin separately and mix it with the iodised oil.
4. Stir until it begins to set, then add the methyl salicylate (volatile) and stir until cool.
5. Fill into an ointment jar.

## Observations

A smooth ointment without the brown colour of free iodine; odour of methyl salicylate.

## Result

Non-staining Iodine Ointment with Methyl Salicylate was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Non-staining Iodine Ointment with Methyl Salicylate. **For external use only.** Rub gently on the affected area. Do not apply to broken skin. Store in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Heat in a closed vessel to prevent loss of iodine vapour.
2. Do not overheat.
3. Add methyl salicylate only when the base has cooled, to reduce evaporation.
4. Use glass, not metal, equipment for iodine.

## Viva questions

1. **Why is it non-staining?** Iodine is chemically combined with the oil, so no free brown iodine remains.
2. **Method of preparation?** Chemical reaction method (followed by fusion and mixing).
3. **Role of methyl salicylate?** Counter-irritant and analgesic.
4. **Why a closed vessel?** Iodine is volatile.
5. **Name the other ointment preparation methods.** Trituration, fusion and emulsification.
