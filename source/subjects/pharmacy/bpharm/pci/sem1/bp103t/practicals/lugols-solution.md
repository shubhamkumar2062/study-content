# Lugol's Solution (Aqueous Iodine Solution)

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 50 ml of Lugol's Solution (Aqueous Iodine Solution).

## Principle

Iodine is very slightly soluble in water, but readily soluble in a concentrated solution of potassium iodide because it forms the soluble **triiodide** complex: I2 + KI ⇌ KI3. This is an example of solubility enhancement by **complexation**. Iodine must be added to a **concentrated** KI solution (minimum water); in dilute KI it dissolves very slowly. Lugol's solution is used before thyroid surgery and in thyroid crisis, and as a source of iodine.

## Formula

| Ingredient | For 100 ml | For 50 ml | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Iodine | 5 g | 2.5 g | Active ingredient |
| Potassium iodide | 10 g | 5 g | Solubiliser (forms KI3) |
| Purified water | to 100 ml | to 50 ml | Vehicle |

## Requirements

Iodine, potassium iodide, purified water, glass beaker, glass rod, watch glass, measuring cylinder, amber glass-stoppered bottle.

## Procedure

1. Dissolve 5 g potassium iodide in about 5 ml of purified water (minimum quantity).
2. Weigh 2.5 g iodine on a watch glass and add it to the concentrated KI solution; stir until completely dissolved.
3. Add purified water to 50 ml and mix.
4. Transfer to an amber glass-stoppered bottle.

## Observations and calculations

Dark brown, clear liquid with odour of iodine.

Iodine 5% w/v = 50 mg per ml; potassium iodide 10% w/v = 100 mg per ml.

## Result

50 ml of Lugol's Solution was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Aqueous Iodine Solution (Lugol's Solution), 50 ml. Dose: as directed by the physician, well diluted with milk or water. Store in a cool place, protected from light, in a glass-stoppered bottle.

## Precautions

1. Dissolve KI in the minimum amount of water first.
2. Weigh iodine on glass, not paper or metal.
3. Avoid rubber or cork closures, which iodine attacks.

## Viva questions

1. **Why is KI added?** To form soluble triiodide and dissolve the iodine.
2. **Why use minimum water for KI?** Iodine dissolves quickly only in a concentrated KI solution.
3. **Uses?** Preparation of the thyroid before surgery, thyroid crisis, iodine deficiency.
4. **Type of solubility enhancement?** Complexation.
5. **Why an amber bottle?** Iodine solutions are light sensitive and volatile.
