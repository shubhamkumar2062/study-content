# Chlorhexidine Mouthwash

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 100 ml of Chlorhexidine Gluconate Mouthwash 0.2% w/v.

> This is a typical laboratory formula. Follow your manual for the flavour and sweetener.

## Principle

A **mouthwash** is an aqueous solution used to clean, deodorise and disinfect the mouth. **Chlorhexidine** is a cationic antiseptic that binds to tooth surfaces and oral mucosa and is released slowly, so it controls **dental plaque and gingivitis**. It is usually supplied as a 20% w/v chlorhexidine gluconate solution, which is diluted. Because chlorhexidine is **cationic**, it is incompatible with anionic substances such as soaps and sodium lauryl sulphate (found in many toothpastes).

## Formula and calculation

Chlorhexidine gluconate needed for 100 ml of 0.2% w/v = 0.2 g.
Volume of 20% w/v solution = 0.2 × 100 ÷ 20 = **1 ml** (check by C1V1 = C2V2: 20 × V1 = 0.2 × 100 → V1 = 1 ml).

| Ingredient | For 100 ml | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chlorhexidine gluconate solution 20% w/v | 1 ml | Antiseptic (0.2% w/v) |
| Glycerin | 5 ml | Sweetener, demulcent |
| Peppermint (or other) flavour | q.s. | Flavour |
| Permitted colour | q.s. | Appearance |
| Purified water | to 100 ml | Vehicle |

## Requirements

Chlorhexidine gluconate solution 20%, glycerin, flavour, colour, purified water, graduated pipette, measuring cylinder, beaker, glass rod, bottle.

## Procedure

1. Measure about 70 ml of purified water in a beaker.
2. Add 1 ml of 20% chlorhexidine gluconate solution with a pipette and mix.
3. Add the glycerin, flavour and colour and stir.
4. Make up to 100 ml with purified water.
5. Filter if needed and transfer to a bottle.

## Observations

Clear, coloured liquid with a pleasant flavour.

## Result

100 ml of Chlorhexidine Gluconate Mouthwash 0.2% w/v was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Chlorhexidine Mouthwash 0.2%, 100 ml. Rinse the mouth with 10 ml for about 1 minute twice daily (or as directed). **Do not swallow.** Use at a different time from toothpaste. Store in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Measure the concentrate accurately with a pipette.
2. Avoid anionic surfactants and soaps in the formula.
3. Use purified water.

## Viva questions

1. **Use of chlorhexidine mouthwash?** Control of dental plaque and gingivitis; oral hygiene.
2. **Why avoid sodium lauryl sulphate?** Chlorhexidine is cationic and is inactivated by anionic surfactants.
3. **Common side effect with long use?** Brown staining of teeth and altered taste.
4. **Volume of 20% solution for 100 ml of 0.2%?** 1 ml.
5. **Difference between mouthwash and gargle?** A mouthwash cleans the mouth; a gargle treats the throat.
