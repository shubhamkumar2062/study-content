# Liquid Paraffin Emulsion

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 100 ml of Liquid Paraffin Emulsion by the dry gum method.

> The official formula uses methylcellulose as the emulsifier. Many laboratories teach the dry gum method with acacia, shown here. Follow your manual where it differs.

## Principle

Liquid paraffin is a **lubricant laxative**. It is unpleasant to swallow alone, so it is given as an **O/W emulsion**, which is more palatable and mixes with intestinal contents. With acacia as emulsifier, the **dry gum method** uses the ratio oil : water : gum = **3 : 2 : 1 for mineral oils**. The oil is triturated with the dry gum, the whole of the water for the primary emulsion is added at once, and rapid trituration forms a thick, white primary emulsion that makes a clicking sound. It is then diluted with the rest of the vehicle.

## Formula (practice formula)

| Ingredient | For 100 ml | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liquid paraffin | 30 ml | Laxative (oil phase) |
| Acacia (powdered) | 10 g | Emulsifying agent (O/W) |
| Purified water (for primary emulsion) | 20 ml | Aqueous phase |
| Preservative (for example benzoic acid solution) | as in manual | Preservative |
| Flavour (vanillin) and sweetener | q.s. | Taste |
| Purified water | to 100 ml | Vehicle |

Check of the ratio: 30 : 20 : 10 = **3 : 2 : 1**.

## Requirements

Liquid paraffin, acacia, preservative, flavour, purified water, dry porcelain mortar and pestle, measuring cylinders, bottle.

## Procedure

1. Measure 30 ml liquid paraffin into a **dry** mortar.
2. Add 10 g powdered acacia and triturate lightly just to mix.
3. Add 20 ml water **all at once** and triturate rapidly in one direction until a thick white primary emulsion forms with a clicking sound.
4. Add the preservative and flavour dissolved in a little water, and dilute gradually with more water while triturating.
5. Transfer to a measuring cylinder, make up to 100 ml and pack in a bottle.
6. Confirm the type by the dilution test (mixes with water: O/W).

## Observations

White, opaque, smooth emulsion; dilutes readily with water.

## Result

100 ml of Liquid Paraffin Emulsion was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Liquid Paraffin Emulsion, 100 ml. **Shake well before use.** Dose: as directed by the physician; not to be taken at bedtime or with meals. Store in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Use a dry mortar; water added before the oil and gum are mixed makes acacia lumps.
2. Add the water for the primary emulsion all at once and triturate quickly.
3. Do not stop triturating until the primary emulsion forms.

## Viva questions

1. **Ratio for mineral oil in the dry gum method?** 3 : 2 : 1.
2. **What is a primary emulsion?** The thick, white emulsion formed first, before dilution.
3. **Why should liquid paraffin not be taken with meals or at bedtime?** It reduces absorption of fat-soluble vitamins and may be aspirated into the lungs when lying down.
4. **Type of emulsion?** O/W.
5. **Test to confirm the type?** Dilution test or dye test with amaranth.
