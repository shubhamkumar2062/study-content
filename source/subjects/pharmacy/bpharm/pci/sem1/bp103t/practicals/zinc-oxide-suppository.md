# Zinc Oxide Suppositories

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 6 suppositories each containing 300 mg of zinc oxide in theobroma oil, using a 1 g mould.

## Principle

Zinc oxide is an **astringent and protective** agent used in suppositories for haemorrhoids. It is a dense, insoluble powder, so it displaces much less than its own weight of base; its displacement value is taken as **5** (given in most laboratory manuals). Being insoluble and heavy, it tends to **settle** in the molten base, so it is finely sifted, levigated with a little melted base, and stirred continuously until the mass is poured near its setting point.

## Calculation

Prepare 8 suppositories (2 extra) and dispense 6. DV of zinc oxide = 5.

- Zinc oxide = 8 × 0.3 = **2.4 g**
- Base displaced = 2.4 ÷ 5 = **0.48 g**
- Base needed = 8 × 1 − 0.48 = **7.52 g theobroma oil**

(For exactly 6: zinc oxide 1.8 g; base = 6 − 0.36 = 5.64 g.)

**Finding a displacement value (if asked).** 6 plain suppositories weigh 6.0 g; 6 suppositories with 40% drug weigh 8.5 g. Drug = 3.4 g, base = 5.1 g, base displaced = 6.0 − 5.1 = 0.9 g, DV = 3.4 ÷ 0.9 = **3.78**.

## Requirements

Zinc oxide, theobroma oil, fine sieve, mortar, ointment slab, spatula, porcelain dish, water bath, suppository mould, soap spirit, foil.

## Procedure

1. Lubricate the calibrated mould with soap spirit.
2. Sift the zinc oxide; weigh 2.4 g.
3. Grate and weigh 7.52 g theobroma oil; melt it gently on a water bath (do not overheat).
4. Levigate the zinc oxide with a little melted base on a warm slab or in a mortar to a smooth paste, then add the rest of the base gradually with stirring.
5. Keep stirring until the mixture thickens slightly (creamy), then pour into the mould, slightly overfilling.
6. Cool, trim, remove and wrap each suppository in foil.

## Observations

Six white, opaque, uniform suppositories with no settling of powder at the tip (check by cutting one lengthwise).

## Result

Six zinc oxide suppositories, each containing 300 mg zinc oxide, were prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Zinc Oxide Suppositories, 300 mg, 6. **For rectal use only. Not to be swallowed.** Store in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Sift zinc oxide to remove grit and lumps.
2. Stir continuously to prevent sedimentation of the dense powder.
3. Pour near the setting point.
4. Do not overheat the base.

## Viva questions

1. **Displacement value of zinc oxide used here?** 5.
2. **Why does zinc oxide displace little base?** It is dense, so a large weight occupies a small volume.
3. **How is sedimentation prevented?** Fine particle size, levigation and pouring near the setting point with stirring.
4. **Use of zinc oxide suppositories?** Haemorrhoids (astringent, protective).
5. **How do you check drug distribution?** Cut a suppository lengthwise and look for powder at the tip.
