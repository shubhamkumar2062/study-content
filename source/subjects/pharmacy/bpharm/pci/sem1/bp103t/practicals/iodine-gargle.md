# Iodine Gargle

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 100 ml of Iodine Gargle.

> Take the official quantities from your laboratory manual. The method of dissolving iodine is the key step.

## Principle

A **gargle** is an aqueous solution used to treat or soothe the throat; it is held in the throat, gargled and **spat out, not swallowed**. Iodine is an antiseptic. Because iodine is almost insoluble in water, it is first dissolved in a **concentrated potassium iodide solution**, forming soluble potassium triiodide (I2 + KI ⇌ KI3). Glycerin may be included as a demulcent and sweetener, and flavour may be added. The gargle is usually supplied as a concentrated solution to be diluted with warm water before use.

## Typical ingredients and roles

| Ingredient | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Iodine | Antiseptic |
| Potassium iodide | Dissolves iodine (forms KI3) |
| Glycerin | Demulcent, sweetener |
| Flavour (for example peppermint) | Taste |
| Purified water | Vehicle |

## Requirements

Iodine, potassium iodide, glycerin, flavour, purified water, glass beaker, glass rod, watch glass, measuring cylinder, amber glass-stoppered bottle.

## Procedure

1. Dissolve the potassium iodide in the minimum quantity of purified water.
2. Add the iodine (weighed on a watch glass) and stir until completely dissolved.
3. Add the glycerin and flavour and mix.
4. Make up to 100 ml with purified water.
5. Transfer to an amber glass-stoppered bottle.

## Observations

Clear, brown liquid with the odour of iodine.

## Result

100 ml of Iodine Gargle was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Iodine Gargle, 100 ml. **Not to be swallowed.** Dilute with an equal volume of warm water (or as directed) and gargle. Store in a cool place, protected from light.

## Precautions

1. Dissolve iodine in concentrated KI solution before diluting.
2. Weigh iodine on glass, not paper or metal.
3. Use amber glass-stoppered bottles.

## Viva questions

1. **What is a gargle?** An aqueous solution for the throat that is gargled and spat out.
2. **How is iodine made soluble?** By forming potassium triiodide with KI.
3. **Role of glycerin?** Demulcent and sweetener; it also slightly prolongs contact.
4. **Why "not to be swallowed"?** It is meant for local action in the throat; iodine is irritant if swallowed in quantity.
5. **Difference between a gargle and a mouthwash?** A gargle treats the throat; a mouthwash cleans and freshens the mouth.
