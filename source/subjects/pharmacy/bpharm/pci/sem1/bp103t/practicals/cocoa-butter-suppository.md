# Cocoa Butter (Theobroma Oil) Suppositories

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 6 suppositories in theobroma oil, each containing 200 mg of a drug (displacement value 2), using a 1 g mould.

> Use the drug named in your laboratory manual. The method of calibration and the displacement value calculation are the same for any drug.

## Principle

Theobroma oil (cocoa butter) is a fatty base that **melts at about 30–35 °C**, just below body temperature, so the suppository melts in the rectum and releases the drug. It is **polymorphic**: if heated much above 36 °C and cooled quickly, it forms unstable low-melting crystals and the suppositories do not set properly. It is therefore melted **gently** to a creamy consistency. It contracts little on cooling, so the mould is lubricated with **soap spirit**.

Because the drug occupies space in the mould, the amount of base is reduced according to the **displacement value** (DV = parts by weight of drug that displace one part of base).

## Calculation

Prepare 8 suppositories (2 extra for losses) and dispense 6.

- Drug = 8 × 0.2 = **1.6 g**
- Base displaced = 1.6 ÷ 2 = **0.8 g**
- Base needed = 8 × 1 − 0.8 = **7.2 g theobroma oil**

(For exactly 6: drug 1.2 g, base 6 − 0.6 = 5.4 g.)

## Requirements

Theobroma oil, drug, grater, porcelain dish, water bath, thermometer, glass rod, mortar, suppository mould (calibrated), soap spirit, spatula, foil, refrigerator.

## Procedure

1. Calibrate the mould: make plain suppositories and find the average weight (if not already known).
2. Lubricate the mould with soap spirit and drain the excess.
3. Grate the theobroma oil and weigh 7.2 g.
4. Melt about two-thirds of the base on a water bath at the lowest possible temperature (not above about 35 °C), stirring.
5. Levigate the finely powdered drug with a little of the melted base, then add the rest of the base (including the unmelted part) and stir to a creamy, uniform mixture.
6. Pour into the mould near the setting point, filling slightly above the cavities.
7. Cool in a refrigerator, trim the top with a warm spatula, open the mould and remove the suppositories.
8. Wrap each in foil; pack and label.

## Observations

Six smooth, uniform, creamy-white, torpedo-shaped suppositories.

## Result

Six theobroma oil suppositories each containing 200 mg drug were prepared using 5.4 g base per 6 (7.2 g for the batch of 8).

## Label

[Drug] Suppositories, 200 mg, 6. **For rectal use only. Not to be swallowed.** Store in a cool place (below 25 °C).

## Precautions

1. Do not overheat theobroma oil (polymorphism).
2. Stir continuously while pouring to prevent the drug from settling.
3. Lubricate the mould with soap spirit.
4. Keep suppositories cool.

## Viva questions

1. **Melting range of theobroma oil?** About 30–35 °C.
2. **Why melt it gently?** To avoid unstable low-melting polymorphs.
3. **Lubricant for theobroma oil moulds?** Soap spirit.
4. **What is displacement value?** Parts by weight of drug that displace one part by weight of base.
5. **Why make extra suppositories?** To allow for losses in the dish and on pouring.
