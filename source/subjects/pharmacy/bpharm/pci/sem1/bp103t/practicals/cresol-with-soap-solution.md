# Cresol with Soap Solution (Lysol)

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense Cresol with Soap Solution.

> The solution contains 50% v/v cresol. Take the quantities of oil and potassium hydroxide from your laboratory manual; the calculation shows how the alkali amount is worked out.

## Principle

Cresol is a strong **disinfectant** but only slightly soluble in water. It is made miscible with water by **solubilisation in soap micelles**. The soap is made in the same vessel by **saponification** of a vegetable oil (traditionally linseed oil) with potassium hydroxide:

Oil (triglyceride) + 3KOH → 3 potassium soap + glycerol

Potassium soaps are soft and soluble, and cresol dissolves in the soap solution to give a clear liquid that mixes with water for use as a disinfectant. This is an example of **micellar solubilisation**.

## Ingredients and roles

| Ingredient | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Cresol | Disinfectant (50% v/v in the product) |
| Linseed (vegetable) oil | Forms soap on saponification |
| Potassium hydroxide | Saponifying alkali |
| Purified water | Solvent |

## Requirements

Cresol, vegetable oil, potassium hydroxide, purified water, porcelain dish or beaker, water bath, glass rod, measuring cylinder, test tube, bottle.

## Procedure

1. Dissolve the potassium hydroxide in a little water.
2. Add the oil and heat on a water bath with constant stirring until saponification is complete. Test: a drop of the mixture should dissolve clearly in water with no oil globules.
3. Add the cresol and stir until a clear solution forms.
4. Make up to volume with purified water and mix.
5. Transfer to a well-closed bottle.

## Observations and calculations

Clear, reddish-brown, soapy liquid with a phenolic odour; forms a milky emulsion-like dilution with water.

Cresol for 100 ml at 50% v/v = **50 ml**.
KOH needed for an oil = weight of oil × saponification value (mg KOH per g). For example, 18 g of an oil with saponification value 190 needs 18 × 0.190 = **3.42 g KOH**; a small excess is used to complete saponification.

## Result

Cresol with Soap Solution was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Cresol with Soap Solution. **For external use only. Poison.** Dilute before use. Store in a well-closed container.

## Precautions

1. Cresol is corrosive and toxic: avoid skin contact, wear gloves.
2. Complete saponification before adding cresol.
3. Handle potassium hydroxide with care.

## Viva questions

1. **Why is soap needed?** To solubilise cresol, which is only slightly soluble in water, in soap micelles.
2. **What is saponification?** Hydrolysis of a fat or oil by alkali to form soap and glycerol.
3. **Why potassium hydroxide rather than sodium hydroxide?** Potassium soaps are softer and more soluble.
4. **Use?** Disinfection of floors, drains, instruments and excreta (diluted).
5. **Test for complete saponification?** A drop dissolves in water without oil globules.
