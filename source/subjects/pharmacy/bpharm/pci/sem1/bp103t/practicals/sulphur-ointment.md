# Sulphur Ointment

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 50 g of Sulphur Ointment (10% w/w) in simple ointment base.

## Principle

Sulphur ointment is used for **scabies**, acne and some fungal skin infections. Sulphur is converted on the skin to compounds (such as hydrogen sulphide and polythionic acids) with antiparasitic and keratolytic action. The ointment has two stages:

1. **Simple ointment** (the base) is made by the **fusion method**: the hard ingredients are melted in decreasing order of melting point and stirred until cool.
2. **Precipitated sulphur** is incorporated by **trituration (levigation)** on a slab, because it is insoluble in the base.

## Formula

**Simple ointment** (for 100 g): wool fat 5 g, hard paraffin 5 g, cetostearyl alcohol 5 g, white or yellow soft paraffin 85 g.

| Ingredient | For 50 g sulphur ointment | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Precipitated sulphur | 5 g | Antiparasitic, keratolytic |
| Simple ointment | 45 g | Base |

Simple ointment for 45 g: wool fat 2.25 g, hard paraffin 2.25 g, cetostearyl alcohol 2.25 g, soft paraffin 38.25 g (total 45 g).

## Requirements

Precipitated sulphur, wool fat, hard paraffin, cetostearyl alcohol, soft paraffin, porcelain dish, water bath, glass rod, ointment slab, spatula, sieve, ointment jar.

## Procedure

1. **Base:** melt the hard paraffin and cetostearyl alcohol on a water bath, then add the wool fat and soft paraffin and melt; stir continuously until cool (fusion).
2. Sift the precipitated sulphur.
3. Place the sulphur on the ointment slab and levigate it with a small portion of the base to a smooth paste.
4. Add the rest of the base in portions with continuous mixing (geometric dilution) until uniform.
5. Fill into a clean ointment jar without air pockets and smooth the surface.

## Observations

Pale yellow, smooth, homogeneous ointment with a faint sulphur odour.

## Result

50 g of Sulphur Ointment (10% w/w) was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Sulphur Ointment, 50 g. **For external use only.** Apply as directed. Store in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Melt ingredients in decreasing order of melting point; do not overheat.
2. Stir the base until it sets to avoid graininess.
3. Use precipitated (not sublimed) sulphur, which is finer and smoother.
4. Avoid metal contact for long (sulphur tarnishes metals).

## Viva questions

1. **Methods used here?** Fusion for the base and trituration for sulphur.
2. **Why precipitated sulphur?** It is a very fine, smooth powder.
3. **Use?** Scabies, acne and fungal infections.
4. **Composition of simple ointment?** Wool fat, hard paraffin and cetostearyl alcohol (5% each) in soft paraffin.
5. **Why stir until cool?** To prevent separation and a granular texture.
