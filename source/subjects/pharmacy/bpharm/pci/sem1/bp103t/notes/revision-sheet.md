# Last-night revision sheet

## Unit 1: History, pharmacopoeias, dosage forms, prescription, posology

- **Key dates:** Chopra Committee 1930–31 → Drugs Act 1940 (now Drugs and Cosmetics Act); Pharmacy Act 1948 → PCI 1949; first IP 1955, current IP 2022 (IPC, Ghaziabad); BP first 1864; USP first 1820 (now USP–NF). Prof. M. L. Schroff = father of pharmacy education in India (BHU).
- **Martindale** (Extra Pharmacopoeia) = reference book, not an official standard.
- **Dosage form** = drug + excipients in a usable form. Classify by state (solid, semisolid, liquid, gas) and by route.
- **Prescription parts:** date → patient details → superscription (Rx) → inscription (ingredients) → subscription (instructions to pharmacist) → signatura (instructions to patient) → renewal → prescriber's signature and registration.
- **Handling:** receive → read and check → collect and weigh → compound → finish → label → record → counsel. Clear doubts with the prescriber, never guess.
- **Errors:** illegible writing, sound-alike names, decimal errors (write 0.5 not .5; never 5.0), ambiguous abbreviations, missing age/weight.
- **Dose factors:** age, weight, BSA, sex, route, time, disease, tolerance, idiosyncrasy, cumulation, interactions.

| Rule | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Young | Age/(Age + 12) × adult dose |
| Dilling | Age/20 × adult dose |
| Cowling | Age next birthday/24 × adult dose |
| Fried | Age in months/150 × adult dose |
| Clark | Weight lb/150 (or kg/70) × adult dose |
| BSA | BSA m²/1.73 × adult dose; Mosteller BSA = √(cm × kg/3600) |

## Unit 2: Calculations, powders, liquids

- 1 grain = 64.8 mg; 1 fl oz = 28.4 ml; 1 pint = 568 ml; 1 kg = 2.2 lb; teaspoon 5 ml, tablespoon 15 ml.
- % w/v = g/100 ml; 1 in 1000 = 0.1%; C1V1 = C2V2.
- **Alligation medial:** Σ(q × s)/Σq. **Alligation alternate:** subtract diagonally; water = 0%.
- **Proof spirit** = 57.1% v/v ethanol. Proof = % v/v × 1.753; o.p. = proof − 100; u.p. = 100 − proof. 90% v/v = 157.8 proof = 57.8 o.p.
- **Isotonicity:** W = (0.52 − a)/0.576 g NaCl per 100 ml. E = 17 L_iso/M (L_iso 1.9 non-electrolyte, 3.4 for 1:1 salt). NaCl to add = 0.9 − %C × E. Dextrose 5% is isotonic. White–Vincent V = w × E × 111.1 ml.
- **Powders:** bulk (internal, external: dusting powder, snuff, insufflation, dentifrice) and divided; simple and compound.
- **Effervescent:** citric : tartaric : NaHCO3 = 1 : 2 : 3.44; granulate by fusion (water of crystallisation) or wet (alcohol).
- **Efflorescent** (lose water: Glauber's salt, sodium carbonate, alum) → use dried form. **Hygroscopic/deliquescent** (CaCl2, K2CO3) → airtight. **Eutectic** (camphor, menthol, phenol, thymol) → dispense separately or absorb on light MgO/kaolin.
- **Geometric dilution:** equal amounts, then keep doubling.
- **Solubility enhancement:** pH, salt, cosolvent, complexation (iodine + KI, cyclodextrin), micelles, hydrotropy, size reduction, solid dispersion, prodrug.

## Unit 3: Monophasic and biphasic liquids

- **Simple Syrup IP 66.7% w/w** sucrose; self-preserving. Problems: inversion, crystallisation, fermentation.
- **Elixir** = clear, sweet, hydroalcoholic. **Linctus** = viscous cough liquid sipped slowly. **Throat paint** = glycerin base (Mandl's paint: iodine + KI). **Gargle** = not swallowed. **Nasal drops** = aqueous, isotonic, never oily. **Enema** = evacuant or retention.
- **Liniment** = rubbed in, never on broken skin. **Lotion** = dabbed, no friction.
- **Stokes:** v = d²(ρs − ρo)g/18η.
- **Flocculated:** fast settling, loose sediment, easy redispersion, clear supernatant. **Deflocculated:** slow settling, hard cake. Aim: controlled flocculation (electrolytes, surfactants, polymers). F = Vu/Vo; β = F/F∞.
- **Diffusible** solids (light kaolin, MgCO3) vs **indiffusible** (calamine, ZnO) need a suspending agent.
- **Emulsions:** O/W, W/O, multiple, micro. Emulsifiers: natural (acacia O/W, wool fat W/O), surfactants (Tween O/W, Span W/O, sodium soaps O/W, calcium soaps W/O), solids (bentonite).
- **HLB:** 3–6 W/O, 7–9 wetting, 8–18 O/W, 13–16 detergent, 15–18 solubiliser.
- **Type tests:** dilution, dye (amaranth/Sudan III), conductivity, fluorescence, CoCl2 paper.
- **Methods:** dry gum 4:2:1 (fixed), 3:2:1 (mineral), 2:2:1 (volatile); wet gum; Forbes bottle; nascent soap; mechanical.
- **Instability:** creaming (reversible), cracking (irreversible), phase inversion, microbial, rancidity.

## Unit 4: Suppositories and incompatibilities

- Rectal ~1 g (child) / 2 g (adult); pessaries 3–8 g; bougies urethral.
- **Bases:** theobroma oil (30–35 °C, polymorphic, rancid, poor contraction → lubricate mould); synthetic hard fats; glycero-gelatin (hygroscopic, needs preservative); PEG (dissolves, may irritate).
- **Methods:** hand rolling, compression (heat-labile drugs), fusion/pour moulding (calibrate → lubricate → melt gently → add drug → pour slightly overfull → cool → trim).
- **DV** = drug/base displaced. Base = N × mould capacity − total drug/DV. Example: 10 × 300 mg ZnO (DV 5), 1 g mould → 10 − 0.6 = 9.4 g.
- DV from data = c/[a − (b − c)]; 6.0 g plain, 8.5 g with 40% drug → 3.4/0.9 = 3.78.
- **Evaluation:** appearance, weight uniformity, content, melting range, liquefaction time, breaking test, disintegration (~30 min fatty, ~60 min water-soluble), dissolution.
- **Incompatibility:** physical (insolubility, immiscibility, liquefaction), chemical (precipitation, effervescence, oxidation, hydrolysis, explosive: KMnO4 + glycerin, chelation), therapeutic (antagonism, toxic synergism, contraindication, overdose). Therapeutic ones only corrected with the prescriber.

## Unit 5: Semisolids

- Ointment (greasy, single phase), cream (emulsion), paste (high solid), gel (polymer network).
- Barrier = stratum corneum; routes: intercellular (main), transcellular, appendageal. Fick: J = DKΔC/h.
- Penetration ↑ with small MW (< about 500), balanced log P, high concentration, occlusion, hydration, thin or damaged skin, enhancers (PG, DMSO, oleic acid, urea).
- **Bases:** hydrocarbon (soft paraffin) → absorption (wool fat; hydrous wool fat) → water-removable O/W (hydrophilic ointment) → water-soluble (PEG).
- **Methods:** trituration/levigation, fusion (melt highest melting point first, stir till cool), chemical reaction, emulsification (both phases at 70–75 °C).
- **Carbopol gel:** disperse, swell, neutralise with triethanolamine to pH 6–7.
- **Evaluation:** homogeneity, content, pH, viscosity, cone penetrometer, spreadability S = ML/T, extrudability, Franz cell release, irritation, microbial limits, stability.
