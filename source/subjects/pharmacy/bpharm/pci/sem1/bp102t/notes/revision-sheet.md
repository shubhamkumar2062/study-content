# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, learn these five topics fully: **errors with accuracy, precision and RSD**, **limit tests (chloride, sulphate, iron, arsenic)**, **non-aqueous titrations with solvents and the two assays**, **complexometric titrations with indicators, masking and the two assays**, and **reference and indicator electrodes with potentiometric end points**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

| Formula | Use | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |

| C1V1 = C2V2 | Dilution | 1 |
| f = actual M ÷ nominal M = expected V ÷ used V | Molarity factor | 1 |

| x̄ = Σx/n; s = √[Σ(x − x̄)²/(n − 1)]; RSD = s/x̄ × 100 | Precision | 1 |
| Relative error = (X − Xt)/Xt × 100 | Accuracy | 1 |

**One-line contrasts examiners like**

| Pair | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Accuracy vs precision | Closeness to the true value vs closeness of results to each other |
| Determinate vs indeterminate error | Known cause, one direction, correctable vs random, both directions, reduced by averaging |
| Primary vs secondary standard | Weighed to exact strength vs strength found by standardisation |
| Iodimetry vs iodometry | Direct titration with I2 (reductants) vs liberated I2 titrated with thiosulphate (oxidants) |
| Mohr vs Volhard | Direct, neutral, chromate vs back titration, nitric acid, ferric alum |
| Co- vs post-precipitation | During (less on digestion) vs after (more on standing) |
| Protogenic vs protophilic solvent | Acidic, for weak bases (glacial acetic acid) vs basic, for weak acids (DMF, pyridine) |
| Levelling vs differentiating | Makes strengths look equal (water for strong acids) vs shows real differences (glacial acetic acid) |
| Masking vs demasking | Hiding an interfering ion in a complex vs releasing it (formaldehyde frees Zn from cyanide) |
| Reference vs indicator electrode | Constant potential vs potential depends on analyte |
| DME vs RPE | Renewed surface, negative potentials vs rotating Pt, positive potentials, amperometry |

**Practicals in one line each:** limit tests in Nessler cylinders (Cl with AgNO3/HNO3, SO4 with barium sulphate reagent/HCl, Fe with thioglycollic acid/citric acid/NH3, As by Gutzeit on HgCl2 paper); standardise NaOH (KHP), H2SO4 (Na2CO3), thiosulphate (KBrO3), KMnO4 (oxalic acid), ceric (As2O3); assays: NH4Cl by formol titration, FeSO4 by ceric/ferroin, CuSO4 by iodometry, calcium gluconate by EDTA with added Mg, H2O2 by KMnO4, sodium benzoate by HClO4 in acetic acid, NaCl by Mohr; conductometric V curve (HCl/NaOH) and two-break curve (HCl + CH3COOH/NaOH); potentiometric HCl/NaOH with first and second derivatives.
