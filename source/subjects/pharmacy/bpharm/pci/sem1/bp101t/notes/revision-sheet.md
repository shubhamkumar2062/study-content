**One-line facts**

1. Negative feedback restores normal (BP, temperature); positive feedback amplifies (labour, clotting).
2. Forms of signalling: contact-dependent, paracrine, synaptic, endocrine; second messengers cAMP, IP3/DAG, Ca2+.
3. Epithelium is avascular and sits on a basement membrane; transitional epithelium lines the bladder.
4. Epidermis from deep: basale, spinosum, granulosum, lucidum, corneum; skin makes vitamin D3.
5. Atlas (C1) holds the skull; axis (C2) has the dens; patella is the largest sesamoid bone; femur is the longest bone.
6. Sarcomere = Z to Z; Ca2+ binds troponin C; ATP detaches myosin; acetylcholine acts on nicotinic receptors at the NMJ.
7. Synovial joints: plane, hinge, pivot, condyloid, saddle (thumb), ball and socket (shoulder, hip).
8. Erythropoietin comes from the kidney; vitamin B12, folic acid and iron are needed; reticulocyte precedes the mature RBC.
9. Hb = 4 globin chains + 4 haems; breakdown → bilirubin.
10. Clotting factors II, VII, IX, X need vitamin K; factor IV = Ca2+; no factor VI; haemophilia A = factor VIII.
11. O is the universal donor, AB the universal recipient; anti-D immunoglobulin within 72 hours of delivery.
12. Thoracic duct drains into the left subclavian vein; Kupffer cells, microglia and alveolar macrophages belong to the RES.
13. Sympathetic: T1–L2, short pre / long post, noradrenaline. Parasympathetic: III, VII, IX, X and S2–S4, acetylcholine.
14. Phrenic C3–C5; sciatic is the largest nerve; I, II, VIII purely sensory cranial nerves.
15. Rods need vitamin A; myopia → concave lens; glaucoma = raised intraocular pressure; organ of Corti = hearing.
16. SA node → AV node → bundle of His → bundle branches → Purkinje fibres; AV delay about 0.1 s.
17. S1 = closure of AV valves; S2 = closure of semilunar valves.
18. BP = CO × peripheral resistance; baroreceptors in carotid sinus and aortic arch; angiotensin II constricts vessels and releases aldosterone.
