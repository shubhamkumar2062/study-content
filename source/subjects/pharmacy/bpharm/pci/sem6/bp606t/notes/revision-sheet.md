# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, learn these five topics with a flow chart or table each: **QA vs QC vs GMP with GMP principles**, **ICH guidelines and stability storage conditions**, **GLP (study director, QAU, protocol, disqualification)**, **complaints and recall procedure with MFR/BMR contents**, and **types of validation with DQ/IQ/OQ/PQ and method validation parameters**. Together they cover most 10-mark questions.

**Definitions in one line**

| Term | One line |
| --- | --- |
| Quality control | Sampling, specifications and testing; release or reject |
| Quality assurance | All arrangements made so products are fit for intended use |
| GMP | Part of QA: consistent production and control to quality standards (Schedule M in India) |
| TQM | Whole organisation continually improving for customer satisfaction |
| QbD (ICH Q8(R2)) | Systematic development from predefined objectives, with product/process understanding, based on science and risk management |
| GLP | Quality system for planning, doing, monitoring, recording, archiving and reporting non-clinical safety studies |
| Calibration | Comparing instrument readings with traceable standards and adjusting |
| Qualification | Proving equipment is installed and works as expected (DQ, IQ, OQ, PQ) |
| Validation | Documented proof that a process or method consistently gives the expected result |
| Recall | Removing defective batches from the market |

**ICH codes to write correctly (check ich.org for current R numbers)**

| Code | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| Q1A(R2) / Q1B / Q1C / Q1D / Q1E | Stability / photostability / new dosage forms / bracketing and matrixing / evaluation of data |
| Q2(R2) | Validation of analytical procedures |
| Q3A / Q3B / Q3C / Q3D | Impurities in substances / in products / residual solvents / elemental impurities |
| Q6A / Q6B | Specifications (chemical / biotech) |
| Q7 | GMP for APIs |
| Q8(R2) / Q9(R1) / Q10 | Pharmaceutical development / quality risk management / pharmaceutical quality system |
| Q11 / Q12 / Q13 / Q14 | Drug substance development / lifecycle management / continuous manufacturing / analytical procedure development |

ICH steps: 1 consensus → 2 (2a confirmation, 2b draft adopted) → 3 regulatory consultation → 4 adoption → 5 implementation. QSEM = Quality, Safety, Efficacy, Multidisciplinary.

**Stability conditions (Q1A(R2))**

| Study | Condition | Minimum at submission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-term | 25 ± 2 °C / 60 ± 5% RH or 30 ± 2 °C / 65 ± 5% RH | 12 months |
| Intermediate | 30 ± 2 °C / 65 ± 5% RH | 6 months |
| Accelerated | 40 ± 2 °C / 75 ± 5% RH | 6 months |
| Refrigerated product | 5 ± 3 °C (accelerated 25 °C / 60% RH) | — |
| India (zone IVb) | 30 °C / 75% RH long-term | — |

Photostability (Q1B): not less than 1.2 million lux hours and 200 watt hours/m² near-UV. Testing frequency: every 3 months year 1, every 6 months year 2, then yearly.

**QbD chain:** QTPP → CQA → risk assessment → CMA/CPP → design space → control strategy → lifecycle. Tools: DoE, FMEA, fishbone, PAT.

**Quality gurus:** Deming (PDCA, 14 points), Juran (trilogy, fitness for use), Crosby (zero defects, conformance), Ishikawa (fishbone, quality circles), Feigenbaum (total quality control), Taguchi (loss function).

**ISO / NABL:** ISO 9001 = QMS requirements (7 principles, clauses 4–10); ISO 14001 = EMS; NABL (part of QCI) accredits labs to ISO/IEC 17025 (testing/calibration) and ISO 15189 (medical). Certification = system exists; accreditation = technically competent.

**Sterile grades (≥0.5 µm per m³, at rest):** A 3,520 · B 3,520 · C 352,000 · D 3,520,000.

**Glass:** Type I borosilicate · II surface-treated soda-lime · III soda-lime · IV general purpose. Powdered glass test = bulk; water attack (surface) test = surface.

**Rubber closures:** penetrability, fragmentation, self-sealability, extract tests. **Secondary:** GSM, bursting strength, compression, Cobb, drop test.

**GLP (21 CFR 58 subparts):** A General provisions · B Organization and personnel · C Facilities · D Equipment · E Testing facilities operation · F Test and control articles · G Protocol and conduct · J Records and reports · K Disqualification. QAU: master schedule, inspections, final report review, QA statement.

**Recall classes:** I serious harm/death · II temporary/reversible harm · III no harm likely.

**Documents:** MFR = master instructions; BMR = one batch's history; SOP sections: objective, scope, responsibility, procedure, references, revision history. ALCOA = Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate.

**Validation types:** prospective · concurrent · retrospective · revalidation. Lifecycle: design → qualification → continued verification.

**Method validation (Q2(R2)):** specificity, accuracy, precision (repeatability, intermediate, reproducibility), linearity, range (assay 80–120%), DL = 3.3σ/S, QL = 10σ/S, robustness. Identification test needs specificity only.

**Instrument checks:** pH buffers 4.01, 6.86, 9.18 at 25 °C (slope ~59.16 mV/pH). UV: holmium (wavelength), potassium dichromate (absorbance), KCl (stray light), toluene in hexane (resolution).

**Warehousing:** quarantine → sampling → approved/rejected; FEFO; temperature mapping. EOQ = √(2DS/H); reorder level = daily use × lead time + safety stock. ABC (value), VED (criticality), FSN (movement).
