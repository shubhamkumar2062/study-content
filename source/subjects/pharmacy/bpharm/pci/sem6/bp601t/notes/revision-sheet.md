# Last-night revision sheet

## Classifications to reproduce exactly

- **Penicillins:** natural · β-lactamase-resistant · amino · carboxy · ureido · amidino.
- **Cephalosporins:** five generations with two named drugs each.
- **Antimalarials by stage:** tissue schizonticide · hypnozoitocide · blood schizonticide · gametocytocide · sporontocide.
- **Antitubercular:** first-line synthetic (isoniazid, pyrazinamide, ethambutol, PAS) · first-line antibiotics (rifampicin, streptomycin) · second-line (ethionamide, cycloserine, capreomycin, fluoroquinolones).
- **Antivirals:** uncoating inhibitors · anti-herpes nucleosides · NRTIs · NNRTIs · protease inhibitors · neuraminidase inhibitors · others.
- **Antifungals:** polyene antibiotics · griseofulvin · imidazoles · triazoles · allylamines · thiocarbamate · flucytosine · echinocandins.
- **Anthelmintics:** benzimidazoles · piperazine type · salicylanilide · praziquantel · ivermectin.
- **Sulphonamides:** short · intermediate · long acting · topical · gut-acting, plus the sulfone dapsone.
- **Prodrugs:** carrier-linked · bioprecursor · site-specific · mutual · macromolecular.

## Unit 5 in twelve lines

1. Lead compound → lead optimisation → preclinical → Phase I–IV.
2. Approaches: random screening, analogue/bioisosteric, mechanism-based, structure-based, ligand-based, prodrug, combinatorial, computer-aided.
3. Bioisosteres: F for H, OH for NH2, tetrazole for COOH, S for CH2.
4. QSAR correlates **log (1/C)** with lipophilic, electronic and steric terms.
5. **π** = log P(substituted) − log P(parent); positive = more lipophilic.
6. **σ** (Hammett) from benzoic acid ionisation; positive = electron-withdrawing; σm and σp only.
7. **Es** (Taft) steric; more negative = bulkier. **MR** = volume + polarisability. **STERIMOL** L, B1–B5.
8. Hansch: log(1/C) = −k1(log P)² + k2 log P + k3σ + k4Es + k5 → parabola with optimum log P₀; judge by n, r², s, F; about five compounds per parameter.
9. Free–Wilson = additive substituent contributions; CoMFA = 3D-QSAR contour maps.
10. **Pharmacophore** = 3D arrangement of donor, acceptor, positive/negative ionisable, hydrophobic and aromatic features; used for virtual screening and scaffold hopping.
11. **Docking** = search algorithm (poses) + scoring function (force-field, empirical, knowledge-based); lock-and-key versus induced fit.
12. **Lipinski:** MW ≤ 500, log P ≤ 5, donors ≤ 5, acceptors ≤ 10. **Combinatorial:** solid phase (resin–linker, wash), split-and-mix gives xⁿ compounds, one per bead; parallel synthesis gives one known compound per well.

## Five facts that win one mark each

1. Penicillin's three chiral centres are 3S, 5R, 6R; cephalosporin's are 6R, 7R.
2. Amikacin = kanamycin A acylated with aminohydroxybutyric acid on N1 of deoxystreptamine.
3. Chloramphenicol is the **D-(−)-threo (1R,2R)** isomer only.
4. Levofloxacin is the **(S)-enantiomer** of ofloxacin; ethambutol must be the **(S,S)** isomer.
5. Co-trimoxazole is **one part trimethoprim to five parts sulfamethoxazole**.
