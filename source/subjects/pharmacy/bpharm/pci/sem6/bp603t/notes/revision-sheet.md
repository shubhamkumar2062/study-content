# Last-night revision sheet

## Unit 1 — Herbs as raw materials and ISM

- **Herb** = crude plant part (whole, cut, powdered). **Herbal material** = herbs + juices, gums, oils, resins. **Herbal preparation** = extracts, tinctures, powders, expressed juices. **Finished herbal product** = dosage form for the patient.
- Sources: wild (cheap, variable, endangers species) vs cultivated (uniform, traceable, GAP).
- Authentication: voucher specimen, macroscopy, microscopy, ash/extractive values, TLC/HPTLC fingerprint, DNA barcoding; quarantine until passed.
- Processing: harvest → clean → **dry** (most critical) → garble → cut → pack → store.
- **Biodynamic** (Rudolf Steiner, 1924): preparation 500 horn manure, 501 horn silica, 502–507 compost herbs; sowing calendar.
- **GACP**: identity, site, seed, cultivation, crop protection, harvest, processing, storage, personnel, documentation. **Organic**: no synthetic inputs; compost, biofertilisers; NPOP certification.
- **Biopesticides**: azadirachtin (neem), pyrethrins, rotenone, nicotine, *Bacillus thuringiensis*, *Beauveria*, *Trichoderma*, NPV.
- **Ayurveda**: panchamahabhuta; tridosha (vata, pitta, kapha); 7 dhatus; agni; prakriti; rasa–guna–virya–vipaka–prabhava.
- **Siddha**: 3 humours, 96 thathuvas, 8-fold diagnosis, metals/minerals. **Unani**: 4 humours (dam, balgham, safra, sauda), mizaj, 6 essentials. **Homoeopathy** (Hahnemann): similia, proving, single remedy, minimum dose, potentisation.
- **Asava** (cold) / **Arishta** (decoction) + jaggery + **dhataki** flowers → self-produced alcohol; test alcohol, methanol absent, sugars, pH, TLC.
- **Churna** (fine sieved powder), **Gutika** (pills), **Lehya** (jam: jaggery, ghee, honey — Chyawanprash), **Bhasma** (shodhana → bhavana → marana; rekhapurnatva, varitara, nishchandratva, apunarbhava).

## Unit 2 — Nutraceuticals and interactions

- Nutraceutical (DeFelice, 1989) = food with medical benefit; regulated by **FSSAI** as food.
- Diabetes: fenugreek, fibre; CVS: omega-3, garlic, phytosterols; cancer: lycopene, curcumin, EGCG; IBS: probiotics, psyllium, peppermint oil.
- Alfalfa (*Medicago sativa*, L-canavanine) · Chicory (inulin, coffee substitute) · Ginger (gingerols, shogaols) · Fenugreek (galactomannan, 4-hydroxyisoleucine, diosgenin) · Garlic (alliin → allicin, alliinase) · Honey (invert sugar; not for infants) · Amla (vitamin C, emblicanins) · Ginseng (ginsenosides, adaptogen) · Ashwagandha (withanolides) · Spirulina (protein, phycocyanin, GLA).
- **Hypericum**: hyperforin induces CYP3A4/P-gp → ↓ cyclosporine, digoxin, OCs, warfarin; serotonin syndrome with SSRIs; photosensitivity.
- **Kava**: liver toxicity, sedation with alcohol/benzodiazepines. **Ginkgo**: PAF antagonist → bleeding. **Ginseng**: ↓ warfarin, mania with MAOIs. **Garlic**: bleeding, ↓ saquinavir. **Pepper**: piperine inhibits CYP3A4/P-gp → ↑ phenytoin, theophylline (Trikatu). **Ephedra**: hypertensive crisis with MAOIs, arrhythmias.
- Grapefruit juice inhibits CYP3A4 (food–drug).

## Unit 3 — Cosmetics, excipients, formulations

- Oils: coconut, olive, almond, castor; waxes: beeswax, carnauba (hardest), candelilla; jojoba = liquid wax.
- Gums: acacia, tragacanth, karaya, guar, xanthan (bacterial). Colours: lawsone (henna), curcumin, bixin (annatto), crocin, betanin, indigo.
- Perfumes: rose (geraniol), sandalwood (santalols), lavender (linalool), vetiver (fixative).
- Bleaching: tyrosinase blockers — glabridin, arbutin, kojic acid, vitamin C. Antioxidants: tocopherol, rosemary, green tea.
- Hair: shikakai, reetha (saponins), amla, bhringraj, henna. Oral: neem, babool, clove (eugenol), peppermint, miswak.
- Natural excipients: + safe, cheap, biodegradable; − variable, microbial load. Sweeteners: stevioside, glycyrrhizin, thaumatin. Disintegrants: starch, sodium starch glycolate.
- Syrup (sugar, preservative, extract) · mixture (suspending agent, F = Hu/Ho) · tablet (wet granulation; adsorbent for sticky extracts).
- **Phytosome** = phosphatidylcholine–polyphenol complex (Indena); better bioavailability; silybin phytosome; differs from liposome (bonded, low ratio).

## Unit 4 — Evaluation, patents, regulation

- WHO QC: identity, foreign matter, ash, extractives, moisture, swelling/foaming/bitterness, pesticides, heavy metals (Pb 10 mg/kg, Cd 0.3 mg/kg suggested), microbes, aflatoxins.
- ICH Q1A(R2): long-term 25 °C/60% RH or 30 °C/65% RH; accelerated 40 °C/75% RH, 6 months; India zone IVb 30 °C/75% RH. Herbal stability = markers + fingerprint.
- Biopiracy = TK/bioresource used without consent or benefit sharing. Patents Act 3(c), 3(p); Biological Diversity Act 2002 (NBA); TKDL.
- **Turmeric**: US patent (wound healing) → CSIR re-examination → revoked 1997. **Neem**: EPO patent (W. R. Grace + USDA, fungicide) → opposed → revoked 2000.
- Chapter IV-A; **ASU DTAB** Sec 33C (technical); **ASU DCC** Sec 33D (uniformity); licence Form 24-D/25-D; Schedule E(1) poisonous drugs.
- **Schedule Z** / licensing guidelines: classical → patent/proprietary → new ASU drugs (increasing evidence).

## Unit 5 — Industry and Schedule T

- Industry: classical ASU, proprietary, extracts, essential oils, nutraceuticals, cosmetics, crude drugs. India leads in isabgol and senna.
- Institutions: AYUSH, NMPB, PCIM&H (Ghaziabad), CCRAS, CIMAP/NBRI/CDRI (Lucknow), IIIM (Jammu), IHBT (Palampur), DMAPR (Anand), TBGRI, FRLHT, TKDL.
- **Schedule T** (GMP for ASU): Part I — location, buildings, water, waste, container cleaning, stores (quarantine), working space, machinery, SOPs/master formula, health and hygiene, medical services, batch and distribution records, complaints, QC. Part II — machinery and minimum area per dosage form.
