# Last-night revision sheet

## Unit 1: Body, cells, tissues, cell injury

- Anatomy = structure; physiology = function; pathophysiology = disordered function. Aetiology = cause; pathogenesis = mechanism.
- Levels: chemical → cellular → tissue → organ → system → organism. 11 body systems.
- Homeostasis by feedback: receptor → control centre → effector. Negative (BP, glucose, temperature) restores; positive (labour, clotting) amplifies.
- Organelles: mitochondria (ATP), rough ER (proteins), smooth ER (lipids, drug metabolism), Golgi (packaging), lysosomes (digestion), nucleus (DNA).
- Transport: passive (diffusion, facilitated, osmosis, filtration); active (Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase 3 out/2 in; secondary symport/antiport); vesicular (endocytosis, exocytosis). 0.9% NaCl isotonic.
- Mitosis PMAT → 2 diploid cells; meiosis → 4 haploid gametes.
- Junctions: tight (seal), desmosome (anchor), gap (communication).
- Signalling: contact-dependent, paracrine, synaptic, endocrine.
- Tissues: epithelial (squamous, cuboidal, columnar, stratified, transitional), connective, muscular (skeletal, cardiac, smooth), nervous.
- Injury causes: hypoxia/ischaemia, physical, chemical, infection, immune, genetic, nutritional.
- Mechanisms: membrane damage (free radicals, Ca²⁺), mitochondria (ATP falls, pump fails), ribosomes (less protein → fatty change), nucleus (DNA damage).
- Adaptations: atrophy, hypertrophy, hyperplasia, metaplasia (bronchi in smokers), dysplasia (pre-cancerous).
- Cell swelling = earliest reversible change. Necrosis (groups, swelling, inflammation) vs apoptosis (single cells, shrinkage, no inflammation).

## Unit 2: Skin, bones, muscle, joints

- Epidermis layers (deep → surface): basale, spinosum, granulosum, lucidum, corneum. Dermis has glands, vessels, nerves.
- Skin functions: protection, temperature, sensation, vitamin D, excretion, absorption.
- Psoriasis: IL-23/Th17, TNF → fast turnover, silvery plaques, Auspitz sign, Koebner.
- Dermatitis: atopic (barrier + Th2), irritant (direct damage), allergic contact (type IV, nickel).
- Leprosy: *M. leprae*, skin + Schwann cells; tuberculoid (strong CMI, few bacilli) vs lepromatous (weak CMI, many).
- Wound healing: haemostasis → inflammation → proliferation (granulation tissue) → remodelling; first vs second intention.
- 206 bones: axial 80, appendicular 126. Vertebrae 7C 12T 5L 5S 4Co. Ribs: 7 true, 3 false, 2 floating.
- Sarcomere: Z to Z; contraction: Ca²⁺ → troponin → tropomyosin moves → cross-bridges → power stroke; ATP to detach.
- NMJ: ACh on nicotinic receptors; acetylcholinesterase ends it; myasthenia gravis attacks receptors.
- Joints: fibrous, cartilaginous, synovial (hinge, pivot, ball and socket, saddle, condyloid, plane).
- RA: autoimmune, pannus, symmetrical small joints, RF and anti-CCP. Osteoporosis: resorption > formation, oestrogen loss, fractures. Gout: urate crystals, IL-1β, big toe.

## Unit 3: Blood, lymph, inflammation, anaemias

- Body water 60%: ICF 2/3, ECF 1/3. Blood 5–6 L, plasma 55%, pH 7.35–7.45.
- RBC 4.5–5.5 million/µL, life 120 days; WBC 4,000–11,000/µL; platelets 1.5–4 lakh/µL.
- Haemopoiesis in red marrow; erythropoietin from kidney; needs iron, B12, folate.
- Hb = 4 globins + 4 haem (Fe²⁺); HbA α2β2; haem → biliverdin → bilirubin.
- Coagulation: extrinsic (III, VII; PT) + intrinsic (XII, XI, IX, VIII; aPTT) → X → Xa + Va → thrombin → fibrin → XIII stabilises. Vitamin K: II, VII, IX, X.
- ABO: O donor, AB recipient. Rh: anti-D prevents haemolytic disease of newborn.
- Lymph: 3 L/day; thoracic duct → left subclavian vein. Organs: marrow, thymus, nodes, spleen, MALT.
- Acute inflammation: vasodilatation, permeability → exudate; margination, rolling, adhesion, diapedesis, chemotaxis, phagocytosis.
- Mediators: histamine, 5-HT, prostaglandins (COX), leukotrienes (LOX), bradykinin, complement (C3a, C5a, C3b), cytokines (IL-1, TNF), NO.
- Iron deficiency: microcytic, koilonychia, low ferritin. B12/folate: megaloblasts, macrocytes; B12 also nerve damage.
- Sickle cell: Glu → Val at β6, HbS polymerises → haemolysis + vaso-occlusion.
- Thalassaemia: reduced globin chains; β-major → severe anaemia, marrow expansion, iron overload.
- Haemolytic: hereditary (spherocytosis, G6PD) vs acquired (autoimmune, malaria). Haemophilia A (VIII), B (IX), X-linked, aPTT prolonged, haemarthrosis.

## Unit 4: PNS and special senses

- Sympathetic T1–L2, short pre/long post, NA on α/β; parasympathetic cranial III, VII, IX, X + S2–S4, long pre/short post, ACh on M.
- Spinal nerves 31 pairs (8C 12T 5L 5S 1Co); dorsal root sensory, ventral root motor; plexuses cervical, brachial, lumbar, sacral.
- Cranial nerves: I olfactory, II optic, III oculomotor, IV trochlear, V trigeminal, VI abducens, VII facial, VIII vestibulocochlear, IX glossopharyngeal, X vagus, XI accessory, XII hypoglossal.
- Eye: cornea, sclera, choroid, ciliary body, iris, lens, retina (rods, cones, fovea), aqueous and vitreous humour.
- Ear: external, middle (ossicles, Eustachian tube), inner (cochlea for hearing; vestibule and semicircular canals for balance).
- Smell: olfactory epithelium → olfactory bulb. Taste: VII anterior 2/3, IX posterior 1/3; five tastes.
- Glaucoma: blocked aqueous outflow → raised IOP → optic nerve cupping; open-angle (silent) vs angle-closure (painful emergency).
- Cataract: lens opacity; crystallin aggregation, sorbitol in diabetes.
- Myopia: long eyeball, focus in front of retina, concave lens.
- Otitis externa: canal, Pseudomonas, swimmer's ear. Otitis media: Eustachian tube block in children, bulging drum.
- Vertigo: BPPV, Ménière's, vestibular neuritis. Anosmia: conductive vs sensorineural.

## Unit 5: Cardiovascular system

- Heart: 4 chambers, valves tricuspid, mitral, pulmonary, aortic; chordae tendineae; coronary flow in diastole.
- Conduction: SA node (60–100/min) → AV node (delay) → bundle of His → branches → Purkinje.
- Cardiac cycle 0.8 s; SV 70 mL; EF 55–70%; CO = SV × HR ≈ 5 L/min; Frank–Starling.
- BP = CO × TPR; normal 120/80; baroreceptor reflex (IX, X); RAAS; ADH; ANP.
- ECG: P atrial depolarisation, QRS ventricular depolarisation, T ventricular repolarisation; PR 0.12–0.20 s.
- Hypertension: ≥140/90; 90–95% essential; kidney, sympathetic, RAAS, endothelium; target organs.
- Arrhythmias: abnormal automaticity, triggered activity, re-entry, block; AF → stroke; VF → death.
- Heart failure: sympathetic + RAAS + remodelling (vicious cycle); left → pulmonary oedema, orthopnoea; right → oedema, raised JVP, liver.
- Atherosclerosis: endothelial injury → oxidised LDL → foam cells → fatty streak → fibrous plaque → rupture → thrombus.
- Angina: temporary ischaemia, relieved by rest; MI: necrosis, pain > 30 min, ST elevation, raised troponin.
