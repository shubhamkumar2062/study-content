**Pairs that get confused**

| This | Not this |
| --- | --- |
| Sensation = detecting | Perception = interpreting |
| Negative reinforcement increases behaviour | Punishment decreases it |
| Empathy = feeling with | Sympathy = feeling for |
| Hearing = passive | Listening = active |
| Adherence = agreed behaviour | Compliance = obedience |
| Psychosomatic = real disease worsened by stress | Somatic symptom disorder = distressing symptoms plus excessive worry |
| Clinical psychology = mental disorders | Health psychology = physical health and behaviour |
| Open question = "How do you take it?" | Closed question = "Do you take it after food?" |

**Facts worth remembering**

- First psychology laboratory: Wundt, Leipzig, 1879.
- Gate control theory of pain: Melzack and Wall, 1965.
- WHO definition of health: complete physical, mental and social well-being, not merely absence of disease.
- Depression: low mood or loss of interest for at least two weeks.
- Mental Healthcare Act, 2017: rights to dignity, confidentiality and care; attempted suicide presumed due to severe stress.
- eSanjeevani = national telemedicine service; ABHA number under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission.
- World Mental Health Day: 10 October.
