# Last-night revision sheet

## Unit 1 — Fundamentals

- Pharmacognosy = pharmakon (drug) + gnosis (knowledge); term credited to Seydler (1815). Dioscorides wrote *De Materia Medica*.
- Sources: plants, animals (honey, lanolin, gelatin), microbes (penicillin, streptomycin), marine (agar, trabectedin, ziconotide), minerals (kaolin, talc), plant tissue culture (shikonin).
- Milestones: morphine (Sertürner, first alkaloid) · quinine (Pelletier & Caventou, 1820) · aspirin (salicin of willow → Hoffmann, 1897) · warfarin (dicoumarol of spoiled sweet clover, Link) · penicillin (Fleming, 1928; Florey & Chain) · cephalosporin (Brotzu, Sardinia) · taxol (*Taxus brevifolia*, microtubule stabiliser) · artemisinin (Tu Youyou, *Artemisia annua*, Nobel 2015).
- Pharmacopoeias: IP (Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission), BHP, USP herbal and dietary supplement monographs, API and UPI (PCIM&H, Ministry of AYUSH), AHP (independent, USA).
- Official = in pharmacopoeia; codified = written in classical traditional texts; non-codified = folk/oral.
- Classifications: alphabetical · morphological (organised/unorganised) · taxonomical · chemical · pharmacological · chemotaxonomic.

## Unit 2 — Cultivation and conservation

- Propagation: seeds (cheap, variable) vs vegetative (cuttings, layering, grafting, budding, rhizomes, bulbs, micropropagation — true to type).
- Factors: altitude, temperature, rainfall, light, soil, fertiliser, pests, genetics.
- Collection: leaves at flowering · barks in spring · roots after aerial parts die · flowers as buds · gums in dry season.
- Drying stops enzymes and microbes; shade-dry aromatic drugs. Store cool, dry, dark, airtight.
- WHO GACP (2003): identity, seed, site, cultivation, harvest, processing, packing, personnel, documentation.
- Hormones: auxins (rooting), gibberellins (elongation, germination), cytokinins (shoots), ABA (dormancy), ethylene (ripening); jasmonate = elicitor.
- Colchicine → polyploidy; *Acorus calamus* ploidy changes β-asarone; mutation raised penicillin yield; peppermint is a natural hybrid.
- In-situ (reserves, sacred groves, MPCAs) vs ex-situ (gardens, seed banks, cryopreservation at −196 °C).
- Eco-pharmacognosy: sustainable sourcing. Kutki (*Picrorhiza kurroa*, picrosides) and chirata (*Swertia chirayita*, amarogentin, swertiamarin).

## Unit 3 — Quality control

- Adulteration: inferior varieties, look-alikes, exhausted drugs, artificial articles, heavy matter, synthetic principles, other parts (clove stalks, saffron stamens, safflower).
- Evaluation: organoleptic · microscopic · physical · chemical · biological.
- Stomatal index = S × 100/(E + S); vein-islet number, vein-termination number, palisade ratio; lycopodium ≈ 94,000 spores/mg.
- Stains: phloroglucinol + HCl (lignin, pink), iodine (starch, blue), Sudan III (oil, red), ruthenium red (mucilage).
- Ash: total (≤ 450 °C), acid-insoluble (sand), water-soluble. Extractive values: alcohol, water, ether.
- Foreign organic matter ≤ 2% (WHO general). Moisture: LOD at 105 °C; toluene distillation for oil drugs.
- Foaming index = 1000/a · haemolytic activity = 1000a/b · bitterness value vs quinine HCl (200,000) · swelling index = ml per 1 g.
- Acid value / saponification value = mg KOH per g oil. Optical rotation (polarimeter), refractive index (Abbe).
- DNA barcoding: rbcL, matK, ITS2, trnH–psbA — identity only, not potency.

## Unit 4 — Metabolites and traditional systems

- Primary (carbohydrates, proteins, lipids) vs secondary (alkaloids, glycosides, flavonoids, tannins, terpenoids, volatile oils, resins).
- Tests: Mayer's/Dragendorff's (alkaloids) · Borntrager's (anthraquinones) · Keller–Killiani (cardiac) · Shinoda (flavonoids) · FeCl3 + gelatin (tannins) · Molisch (sugars) · Biuret (protein) · Salkowski (sterols).
- Tannins: hydrolysable (gallic/ellagic; blue-black) vs condensed (catechin; green-black, phlobaphenes).
- Terpenoids: C10, C15 (artemisinin), C20 (taxol), C30 (boswellic acids), C40 (carotenoids).
- Resins: oleoresin, gum-resin, oleo-gum-resin, balsam (benzoic/cinnamic acid), glycoresin.
- Ayurveda: panchamahabhuta, tridosha (vata, pitta, kapha), panchakarma, rasayana. Unani: four humours, mizaj. Siddha: vatham, pitham, kabam. Homoeopathy: like cures like, potentisation. TCM: yin–yang, qi, five phases, acupuncture.
- Ayurvedic forms: swarasa, kalka, kwatha, hima, phanta, churna, vati, avaleha, asava/arishta (self-fermented), ghrita, taila, bhasma.
