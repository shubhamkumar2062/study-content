# Last-night revision sheet — MPH204T

## Unit 1: Regulation

- Cosmetic (section 3(aaa), D&C Act 1940): rubbed, poured, sprinkled, sprayed, introduced into or applied to the human body for cleansing, beautifying, promoting attractiveness, altering appearance; includes components. Disease or body-function claim → drug.
- Cosmetics Rules, 2020: G.S.R. 763(E), 15 Dec 2020; replaced Form 32, Schedule M-II, Schedule S of the 1945 Rules. CLA (CDSCO) = import, new cosmetics; SLA = manufacture, sale.
- New cosmetic = novel ingredient; COS-12 → COS-3; safety tests per IS 4011:2018.
- Import: COS-1 → COS-2 (within 6 months; perpetual with retention fee every 5 years; 2%/month late fee up to 180 days). Registered product imported by others: COS-4 → COS-4A (3 years). Changes reported in 15 days; constitution change → fresh registration in 180 days.
- Import bans (Rule 18): banned in country of origin; use-before ≤ 6 months from import; hexachlorophene; tested on animals after 12 Nov 2014. Personal-use baggage allowed.
- Manufacture: COS-5 / COS-6 (loan) + COS-7 GMP self-declaration → COS-8 / COS-9 in 45 days; upload on CDSCO site; SLA inspects within 30 days; inspection at least once in 3 years (risk-based).
- Technical person (whole-time): D.Pharm / registered pharmacist / Intermediate with chemistry / B. Cosmetic Technology. Test every batch of raw material and product (own lab or NABL-accredited approved lab). Batch records 3 years after expiry. Changes reported in 30 days. Constitution change: licence valid max 6 months.
- Loan licence: use another licensee's site; COS-9 falls with the host's COS-8.
- Label (Rule 34): name; manufacturer and address; use before; batch "B."; licence "M."; net contents (outer); hazards; INGREDIENTS > 1% in descending order; imports "RC No." + importer; stickering in bonded warehouse. Hair dye caution + 24 h patch test (Rule 37); fluoride ≤ 1000 ppm (Rule 38).
- 17C misbranded (wrong label/colour/claim), 17D spurious (wrong identity), 17E adulterated (filthy, insanitary, poisonous container, non-permitted colour, harmful substance). Section 18 prohibitions; 27A: spurious/adulterated up to 3 years + fine; others up to 1 year or fine.
- Rule 39: BIS standards; IS 4707 Part 2 Annex A banned; colours Tenth Schedule (As 2 ppm, Pb 20 ppm, heavy metals 100 ppm); hexachlorophene only soap ≤ 1%; mercury 70 ppm (eye) / 1 ppm; no lead/arsenic colours; no animal testing.

## Unit 2: Biology

- Stratum corneum bricks (corneocytes) + mortar (ceramides, cholesterol, FFA); NMF from filaggrin (amino acids, PCA, lactate, urea); TEWL; acid mantle pH 4.5–5.5.
- Dry skin: lipid/NMF loss → abnormal desquamation. Acne: sebum, hyperkeratinisation, C. acnes, inflammation.
- Melanin: tyrosine → DOPA → dopaquinone (tyrosinase) → eumelanin / pheomelanin; UV → α-MSH → MC1R; melasma, PIH, lentigines.
- Miliaria: blocked eccrine ducts – crystallina, rubra, profunda. Photoageing: UV → ROS → MMPs; glycation.
- Body odour: apocrine secretion + Corynebacterium/Staphylococcus → 3-methyl-2-hexenoic acid, thioalcohols.
- Hair: cuticle/cortex/medulla; disulphide, hydrogen, salt bonds; IEP about 3.7; negative charge; anagen 2–7 y, catagen 2–3 wk, telogen ~3 mo. Dandruff = Malassezia; AGA = DHT.
- Teeth: caries below pH ~5.5; gingivitis from plaque; halitosis = VSCs; sensitivity = hydrodynamic theory.

## Unit 3: Building blocks

- Surfactants: anionic (SLS, SLES, SCI, soaps), cationic (quats – conditioners, CPC), amphoteric (CAPB), non-ionic (polysorbates, ethoxylates, APGs). HLB 3–6 w/o, 8–18 o/w, 15–18 solubilisers; packing parameter.
- Emollients: hydrocarbons (petrolatum most occlusive), triglycerides, esters, fatty alcohols/acids, silicones, lanolin, waxes, ceramides.
- Rheology: gums, cellulose ethers, carbomers (neutralise), associative, NaCl for shampoos, clays/silica, fatty alcohols. Shear-thinning + yield value.
- Preservative efficacy: pH/pKa, partition, non-ionic binding, interactions, packaging, aw, load, structure, synergy (EDTA). Challenge test ISO 11930: P. aeruginosa, S. aureus, E. coli, C. albicans, A. brasiliensis, 28 days.
- Vanishing cream: stearic acid 15–25% partly neutralised → o/w, matt, pearly. Cold cream: beeswax + borax + mineral oil + water → w/o, cooling.
- Shampoo: SLES + CAPB + polyquaternium + salt + pearliser + ZPT; coacervate. Toothpaste: abrasive + humectant + binder + SLS + fluoride + flavour; CaCO3 with MFP.
- Soap pH 9–10, scum, TFM 76/70/60%; syndet (SCI) pH 5.5–7, mild; combars.
- Perfume: origin, families (floral, fresh, oriental, woody, chypre, fougère), concentration (parfum 20–30%, EDP 15–20%, EDT 5–15%, cologne 2–5%), notes top/heart/base; IFRA.
- EU allergens: 26 (24 chemicals + oakmoss, treemoss), label > 0.001% leave-on, > 0.01% rinse-off; expanded to ~80 (Reg. 2023/1545); HICC, atranol, chloroatranol, lilial banned.
- Parabens: methyl/ethyl 0.4% single / 0.8% mix; propyl + butyl ≤ 0.14%, not in nappy-area leave-on < 3 y; 5 parabens banned. Formaldehyde releasers: "releases formaldehyde" > 0.001% (Reg. 2022/1181). 1,4-Dioxane: ethoxylation by-product; vacuum stripping.

## Unit 4: Cosmeceutical design and evaluation

- UVB 290–320, UVA2 320–340, UVA1 340–400 nm. Organic filters (octinoxate, octisalate, homosalate, octocrylene, oxybenzone, avobenzone, bemotrizinol, bisoctrizole, ecamsule) vs inorganic (ZnO, TiO2).
- SPF = MED protected/unprotected at 2 mg/cm² (ISO 24444); SPF 15/30/50 ≈ 93/97/98% UVB; λc ≥ 370 nm; EU UVA-PF ≥ ⅓ SPF, bands low/medium/high/very high; PA+ to PA++++ (PPD 2/4/8/16). India & EU cosmetic; USA OTC drug.
- Dry skin: glycerin, urea, PCA, HA + petrolatum/dimethicone + ceramide–cholesterol–FFA + niacinamide.
- Acne: salicylic acid, niacinamide, zinc, retinol, non-comedogenic gels. Pigmentation: kojic acid, arbutin, glabridin, vitamin C, niacinamide (transfer), sunscreen + iron oxides.
- Wrinkles: retinol, vitamin C/E, ferulic acid, peptides, AHAs, HA, sunscreen. Prickly heat: talc/starch + ZnO/calamine + menthol.
- Deodorant (antimicrobial, fragrance, zinc ricinoleate) vs antiperspirant (aluminium chlorohydrate plugs ducts). Dandruff: ZPT, piroctone olamine, climbazole, selenium sulphide.
- Oral: fluoride (NaF, MFP, SnF2); gums: SnF2, zinc citrate, CPC, essential oils; halitosis: zinc, CPC, ClO2; sensitivity: KNO3 (nerve), SnF2/strontium/arginine/bioactive glass (occlusion).
- Safety: MoS = NOAEL/SED ≥ 100; OECD 439 (irritation), 431 (corrosion), 437 BCOP/492 (eye), 442C/D/E (sensitisation), 432 (phototox), 428 (absorption); HRIPT; IS 4011:2018.
- Efficacy: corneometer, TEWL, sebumeter, cutometer, chromameter/Mexameter, profilometry, combing force; claims truthful (Rule 36).

## Unit 5: Herbal cosmetics

- Hair: amla, bhringraj (wedelolactone), hibiscus, henna (lawsone), shikakai/reetha (saponins), neem, methi, rosemary, aloe.
- Skin: turmeric (curcumin), aloe, neem, sandalwood (santalols), saffron (crocin), liquorice (glabridin), green tea (EGCG), centella (asiaticoside), papaya (papain), tea tree (terpinen-4-ol), rose, multani mitti.
- Oral: neem, babool (tannins), clove (eugenol), miswak (Salvadora persica), mint, thyme (thymol), liquorice, bakul.
- No legal "herbal/natural/organic" definition in Indian cosmetic law; Ayurvedic with medicinal claims = ASU drug. ISO 16128 indices.
- COSMOS (BDIH, Cosmebio, Ecocert, ICEA, Soil Association): ORGANIC ≥ 20% organic (≥ 10% rinse-off/aqueous/mineral-rich), ≥ 95% PPAI organic, ≤ 2% petrochemical moieties; NATURAL; CERTIFIED and APPROVED raw materials. No ethoxylation/propoxylation, GMO, irradiation, mineral oil, silicones.
- COSMOS preservatives: organic acids + salts (benzoic, sorbic, dehydroacetic, salicylic), benzyl alcohol; not parabens, formaldehyde releasers, isothiazolinones, phenoxyethanol. Emulsifiers: glyceryl stearate, sucrose/polyglyceryl esters, glucosides, olivates, lecithin. Foamers: APGs, glutamates, betaines, saponins, soaps. Thickeners: xanthan, guar, gellan, clays.
- Challenges: variability, adulteration, microbes, preservation, heavy metals/pesticides, stability, colour/odour, solubility, sensory, allergens and phototoxicity, evidence, regulation, sustainability.
