## Unit 1: Preformulation, stability, dispersions, parenterals

- Preformulation = physicochemical + biopharmaceutic profiling before formulation; feeds QbD (CMAs → CQAs).
- Key tests: pKa, S0 and pH–solubility, log P/log D, intrinsic dissolution (Wood's apparatus), polymorphs (DSC, PXRD, TGA), hygroscopicity (DVS), flow (Carr's index, Hausner ratio).
- BCS high solubility: highest single therapeutic dose dissolves in ≤ 250 ml over pH 1.2–6.8 at 37 °C (ICH M9).
- Interactions: Maillard (lactose + amine), magnesium stearate + aspirin, peroxides in povidone/PEG/polysorbate, aldehydes cross-link gelatin. Screens: DSC (fast), isothermal stress testing + HPLC (confirms), FTIR, PXRD, microcalorimetry.
- Kinetics: first-order t½ = 0.693/k, t90 = 0.105/k; zero-order t90 = 0.1C0/k0; suspensions apparent zero order.
- Arrhenius ln k = ln A − Ea/RT. Example: k 0.0042/0.0105/0.0250 day⁻¹ at 50/60/70 °C → Ea ≈ 82 kJ/mol, k25 = 3.2 × 10⁻⁴ day⁻¹, t90 ≈ 326 days.
- ICH Q1A(R2): long term 25 °C/60% RH or 30 °C/65% RH (12 months); intermediate 30/65; accelerated 40 °C/75% RH (6 months); fridge 5 ± 3 °C. India zone IVb 30 °C/75% RH. Q1B: 1.2 million lux h + 200 W·h/m² UV.
- Significant change: 5% assay change, degradant over limit, dissolution failure, pH failure, appearance failure.
- Emulsion theories: interfacial tension, oriented wedge, interfacial film (mono-, multimolecular, solid particle). DLVO: VT = VA + VR; secondary minimum = loose flocs.
- Suspensions: Stokes v ∝ d²; F = Vu/V0; β = F/F∞; controlled flocculation + structured vehicle.
- HLB blend: 72% Tween 80 + 28% Span 80 for required HLB 12.
- SMEDDS: oil + surfactant (HLB > 12) + co-surfactant; < 100 nm; Pouton types I–IV; pseudo-ternary phase diagram.
- SVP ≤ 100 ml, LVP > 100 ml (USP); no preservative in LVP or intrathecal; 0.9% NaCl ≈ 308 mOsm/L; particulate LVP ≤ 25/ml ≥ 10 µm, ≤ 3/ml ≥ 25 µm.

## Unit 2: Optimization

- OFAT misses interactions. Factors, levels, responses, effects, interactions, aliasing, resolution.
- Screening: Plackett–Burman (N − 1 factors in N runs), fractional factorial, Taguchi.
- Full factorial 2^k; CCD = 2^k + 2k + n0 (13 runs for k = 2 with 5 centre points); rotatable α = (2^k)^¼ = 1.414 (k = 2), 1.682 (k = 3); BBD 15 runs (3 factors).
- Mixture designs: simplex lattice, simplex centroid, extreme vertices; Scheffé model.
- Effect = (Σ+ − Σ−)/2^(k−1); coefficient = effect/2. Example 2³: A +3.45, B −2.35, C +1.85 min.
- 2² example: Y = 84 − 10X1 + 4X2 + 2X1X2 → HPMC 14.5% gives 85% release.
- RSM steps: design → model → ANOVA/lack of fit → contour and 3-D plots → optimum → check-point batches. Desirability 0–1; overlay plot = design space.

## Unit 3: Validation

- Validation = documented evidence of consistency; calibration = traceable to standards; qualification = equipment/facilities.
- VMP: policy, organisation, list and status, schedule, change control, documentation.
- URS → DQ → (FAT/SAT) → IQ → OQ → PQ; V-model pairs.
- Types: prospective, concurrent, retrospective, revalidation. FDA 2011 lifecycle: design → PPQ → CPV.
- Cp = (USL − LSL)/6σ; Cpk = min(USL − x̄, x̄ − LSL)/3σ; example Cp 1.39, Cpk 1.22; target ≥ 1.33.
- Cleaning validation: worst case, PDE / 10 ppm / 1/1000 dose criteria, swab and rinse with recovery. Media fills for aseptic processes.
- ICH Q2(R2): specificity, linearity, range, accuracy, precision, LOD 3.3σ/S, LOQ 10σ/S, robustness.
- Regulations: revised Schedule M (Dec 2023), 21 CFR 210/211, EU Annex 15, WHO TRS, PIC/S.

## Unit 5: Compression and compaction

- Compression (volume ↓) + consolidation (strength ↑) = compaction.
- Stages: rearrangement → elastic/plastic/brittle deformation → bonding → elastic recovery → ejection.
- Plastic (MCC, NaCl): speed- and lubricant-sensitive. Brittle (DCP, sucrose): insensitive. Elastic (paracetamol): capping.
- Bonding: solid bridges (fusion, recrystallisation), intermolecular forces / cold welding, mechanical interlocking.
- σt = 2F/(πDt) → 80 N, 10 × 4 mm = 1.27 MPa. Porosity example 15.1%.
- R = FL/FU (0.85 example); FA = FL + FD; FL = FA e^(−4µKH/D).
- Dwell time 15.5 ms at 30 rpm, 7.8 ms at 60 rpm (410 mm PCD, 10 mm flat). SRS = (Py high − Py low)/Py high.
- Profiles: force–time, force–displacement (net work, plasticity factor E2/(E2 + E3)), radial vs axial, ejection, elastic recovery (5.9% example).
