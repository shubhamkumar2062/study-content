**Unit 2 — Product approval requirements**

- API: ICH Q7; DMF; registration and listing (21 CFR 207); Q3A/Q3C/Q3D/M7.
- BLA 351(a): safe, pure, potent; 21 CFR 600–680. Biosimilar 351(k): no clinically meaningful difference; interchangeable; 12-year reference exclusivity; Purple Book.
- Expedited: fast track (rolling review), breakthrough, accelerated approval (surrogate + confirmatory), priority (6 months), RMAT; orphan < 200,000.
- NDA: 505(b)(1)/(b)(2); 356h; Type A/B/C meetings; 60-day filing; day-74 letter; 10/6 months; PLR label; REMS.
- Foreign registration: DUNS → establishment registration + listing (US agent) → eCTD + fees → PAI → import admissibility; 483, warning letter, import alert.
- India: CT-04 → CT-06; CT-18 import; CT-21 manufacture; 90/30 working days; SUGAM; SEC.

**Unit 4 — Non-clinical and global submissions**

- Safety pharmacology core battery: CNS, CVS, respiratory (S7A); QT S7B/E14.
- Toxicology: single/repeat dose in rodent + non-rodent; S2 genotoxicity battery; S1, S5, S6 (relevant species), S9; timing M3(R2).
- GLP: 21 CFR 58; OECD; NGCMA India. First dose: NOAEL → HED → ÷ safety factor (default 10); MABEL.
- IND 312.23(a): 1571, contents, IB, protocols, CMC, pharm/tox, prior human experience; 1572; 30 days; clinical hold.
- EU CTR 536/2014: 31 Jan 2022; CTIS compulsory 31 Jan 2023; transition end 31 Jan 2025; Part I / Part II.
- IMPD: quality (S + P) + summaries; phase-appropriate; simplified IMPD.
- IB: summary, introduction, properties, non-clinical, effects in humans, guidance; RSI; reviewed at least yearly.
