# Last-night revision sheet

If you have only one hour, learn these with full answers: **limit test for iron and arsenic**, **types of titration (non-aqueous, complexometric, precipitation, redox)**, **antacids**, **chlorpromazine, phenytoin, diazepam**, **propranolol and atropine**, **INH and chloroquine**, **penicillin nucleus and amoxicillin**, **aspirin and paracetamol**, **cyclophosphamide and cisplatin**.

## Analysis (Units 1–2)

| Test / titration | Key reagent | Indicator / effect | Key point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Limit test chloride | AgNO3 + dilute HNO3 | Opalescence | Away from light |
| Limit test sulphate | BaCl2 reagent + dilute HCl | Turbidity | Alcohol + K2SO4 seeding |
| Limit test iron | Thioglycollic acid + citric acid + NH3 | Purple | Reduces Fe³⁺ to Fe²⁺ |
| Limit test heavy metals | H2S / sulphide, pH 3–4 | Brown | ppm of lead |
| Limit test arsenic | Zn + HCl, KI, SnCl2 | Stain on HgCl2 paper | Lead acetate cotton traps H2S |
| Acid–base | NaOH / HCl | Phenolphthalein, methyl orange | Weak acid → phenolphthalein |
| Non-aqueous (bases) | HClO4 in glacial acetic acid | Crystal violet | Mercuric acetate for halides |
| Non-aqueous (acids) | Sodium methoxide | Thymol blue | DMF solvent |
| Mohr | AgNO3, neutral | K2CrO4 → red-brown | Direct |
| Volhard | Excess AgNO3, back with NH4SCN | Ferric alum → red | Acid; nitrobenzene in modified |
| Fajans | AgNO3 | Fluorescein, eosin | Adsorption |
| Complexometric | EDTA (1 : 1) | Eriochrome black T wine red → blue | pH 10 buffer |
| Permanganometry | KMnO4 + H2SO4 | Self indicator | Not HCl |
| Iodimetry / iodometry | I2 / thiosulphate | Starch | Direct / indirect |

- **Accuracy** = near true value (systematic errors). **Precision** = repeats agree (random errors).
- **Primary standards:** KHP, Na2CO3, oxalic acid, K2Cr2O7, NaCl.
- **Gravimetry:** precipitate → digest → filter → wash → ignite → weigh; gravimetric factor.

## Nomenclature (Unit 4)

Hantzsch–Widman: oxa > thia > aza; stems -irine (3), -ete (4), -ole (5), -ine (6), -epine (7); saturated N rings -iridine, -etidine, -olidine, -inane.

## Classic adverse effects (one word each)

Phenytoin – gum hypertrophy · Valproate – liver, neural tube defects · Chlorpromazine – photosensitivity · Haloperidol – EPS · Captopril – dry cough · Amiodarone – thyroid/lung · Frusemide – hypokalaemia, ototoxicity · Spironolactone – gynaecomastia · Metformin – lactic acidosis, B12 · Glibenclamide – hypoglycaemia · Aspirin – Reye's syndrome · Paracetamol – liver (NAPQI) · INH – neuropathy (B6) · Ethambutol – optic neuritis · Rifampicin – orange urine · Primaquine/dapsone – G6PD haemolysis · Chloroquine – retinopathy · Tetracycline – teeth staining · Chloramphenicol – grey baby, aplastic anaemia · Clindamycin – pseudomembranous colitis · Cyclophosphamide – haemorrhagic cystitis · Cisplatin – nephrotoxicity · Doxorubicin – cardiotoxicity · Vinblastine – fatal intrathecal.

## Storage hot-list

- **Protect from light:** catecholamines, chlorpromazine, frusemide, nifedipine, silver nitrate, sulphacetamide drops, cisplatin, amphotericin B.
- **Keep dry / airtight:** aspirin, ferrous sulphate, ferric ammonium citrate, sodium valproate, carbamazepine, bleaching powder.
- **Refrigerate (2–8 °C):** insulin (never freeze), amphotericin B, reconstituted amoxicillin syrup, chloramphenicol eye drops (before opening), vinblastine.
- **Do not refrigerate:** fluorouracil, cisplatin solution, mannitol 20%.
