# Assay of ferrous sulphate (redox titration)

**Aim:** To determine the percentage purity of the given ferrous sulphate by titration with standard 0.1 N potassium permanganate.

> The current IP uses 0.1 M ceric ammonium sulphate with ferroin indicator; the KMnO4 method below has the same factor and is widely used in teaching labs. Follow your manual.

## Principle

In acid solution ferrous ions are oxidised to ferric ions by permanganate:

MnO4⁻ + 5Fe²⁺ + 8H⁺ → Mn²⁺ + 5Fe³⁺ + 4H2O

KMnO4 acts as its own indicator. The equivalent weight of FeSO4·7H2O is its molecular weight, 278.0 (one electron per Fe).

**Factor:** each ml of 0.1 N KMnO4 ≡ 0.02780 g of FeSO4·7H2O.

## Requirements

Ferrous sulphate sample, standardised 0.1 N KMnO4, 1 M (dilute) H2SO4, freshly boiled and cooled water, burette, conical flask, balance.

## Procedure

1. Weigh accurately about 1 g of ferrous sulphate (W).
2. Dissolve in a mixture of about 20 ml dilute H2SO4 and 80 ml freshly boiled and cooled water (removes dissolved oxygen that would oxidise Fe²⁺).
3. Titrate at once with 0.1 N KMnO4 until a faint permanent pink appears.
4. Repeat for concordant readings.

## Observations

| No. | Weight of sample (g) | Burette reading (ml) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1.002 | 35.6 |
| 2 | 1.000 | 35.5 |

## Calculation

% purity = (V × 0.02780 × actual N ÷ 0.1 × 100) ÷ W

Example: V = 35.5 ml, N = 0.1, W = 1.000 g → 35.5 × 0.02780 × 100 ÷ 1.000 = 98.7%.

## Result

The given sample contains ____% w/w of FeSO4·7H2O. (IP requirement for ferrous sulphate is close to 100%; check the monograph limits.)

## Precautions

1. Titrate immediately; Fe²⁺ is oxidised by air.
2. Use freshly boiled and cooled water.
3. Acidify with H2SO4 only.
4. Read the upper meniscus.

## Viva questions

1. **Why is boiled and cooled water used?** To remove dissolved oxygen.
2. **What is the indicator?** KMnO4 itself (self-indicator).
3. **Why does old ferrous sulphate give low results?** Part has oxidised to ferric sulphate.
4. **What is the equivalent weight of ferrous sulphate here?** 278.0 (equal to its molecular weight).
5. **Which indicator is used with ceric ammonium sulphate?** Ferroin (o-phenanthroline ferrous complex).
