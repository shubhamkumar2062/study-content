# Assay of calcium gluconate (complexometric titration)

**Aim:** To determine the percentage purity of the given calcium gluconate by complexometric titration with 0.05 M disodium edetate.

> Follow the monograph in your IP edition; the method below is the classical replacement titration.

## Principle

Calcium forms a stable 1 : 1 complex with EDTA at pH 10. Mordant black II (Eriochrome black T) gives a poor end point with calcium alone, so a known volume of **magnesium sulphate** is added. At the end point EDTA removes the last magnesium from the Mg–indicator complex and the colour changes from **wine red to blue**. The volume of MgSO4 added is subtracted.

Ca²⁺ + H2Y²⁻ → CaY²⁻ + 2H⁺ (buffer neutralises the H⁺)

**Factor:** each ml of 0.05 M disodium edetate ≡ 0.02242 g of calcium gluconate (C12H22CaO14·H2O, MW 448.4).

## Requirements

Calcium gluconate, 0.05 M disodium edetate (standardised), 0.05 M magnesium sulphate, strong ammonia–ammonium chloride buffer (pH 10), mordant black II mixture, burette, conical flask, water bath.

## Procedure

1. Weigh accurately about 0.5 g sample (W); dissolve in 50 ml warm water and cool.
2. Add 5.0 ml of 0.05 M MgSO4 and 10 ml ammonia–ammonium chloride buffer.
3. Add a small quantity (about 0.1 g) of mordant black II mixture – wine red colour.
4. Titrate with 0.05 M disodium edetate until the colour changes to blue.
5. Subtract 5.0 ml (volume of MgSO4 added, since both are 0.05 M) from the burette reading.

## Observations and calculation

| Burette reading (ml) | Corrected volume V = reading − 5.0 (ml) |
| --- | --- |
| 27.2 | 22.2 |

% purity = (V × 0.02242 × M ÷ 0.05 × 100) ÷ W

Example: V = 22.2, M = 0.05, W = 0.500 → 22.2 × 0.02242 × 100 ÷ 0.500 = 99.5%.

## Result

The given sample contains ____% of calcium gluconate.

## Precautions

1. Maintain pH 10 with buffer; the indicator works only at this pH.
2. Use metal-free distilled water.
3. Add indicator in small amount; excess gives a dull end point.
4. Titrate slowly near the end point; the change is gradual.

## Viva questions

1. **Why is MgSO4 added?** Mordant black II gives a sharper end point with magnesium (replacement titration).
2. **In what ratio does EDTA bind metals?** 1 : 1.
3. **Why is buffer needed?** H⁺ is released during complexation; the indicator needs pH 10.
4. **Name another indicator for calcium.** Calcon or murexide (hydroxynaphthol blue).
5. **Use of calcium gluconate?** Calcium supplement; IV in hypocalcaemic tetany and hyperkalaemia.
