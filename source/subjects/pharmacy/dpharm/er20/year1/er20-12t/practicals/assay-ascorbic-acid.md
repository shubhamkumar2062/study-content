# Assay of ascorbic acid (iodometry / iodimetry)

**Aim:** To determine the percentage purity of the given ascorbic acid by titration with 0.05 M iodine.

> This is a direct titration with iodine (iodimetry); the syllabus lists it as iodometry. Titrate quickly because ascorbic acid is oxidised by air.

## Principle

Ascorbic acid is a reducing agent. Iodine oxidises it to dehydroascorbic acid; the first excess iodine gives a **blue colour with starch**.

C6H8O6 + I2 → C6H6O6 + 2HI

One mole of ascorbic acid (176.1 g) reacts with one mole of iodine.

**Factor:** each ml of 0.05 M iodine ≡ 0.008806 g of C6H8O6.

## Requirements

Ascorbic acid, 0.05 M iodine (standardised against sodium thiosulphate), 1 M sulphuric acid, starch solution, freshly boiled and cooled water, burette, conical flask.

## Procedure

1. Weigh accurately about 0.1 g ascorbic acid (W).
2. Dissolve in a mixture of 100 ml freshly boiled and cooled water and 25 ml 1 M H2SO4.
3. Titrate **immediately** with 0.05 M iodine, adding 1 ml starch solution near the end point (when the yellow colour of iodine persists faintly).
4. Continue until a permanent **blue** colour appears.
5. Repeat for concordant readings.

## Observations and calculation

| No. | Weight (g) | Volume of 0.05 M iodine (ml) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0.1002 | 11.3 |

% purity = (V × 0.008806 × M ÷ 0.05 × 100) ÷ W

Example: 11.3 × 0.008806 × 100 ÷ 0.1002 = 99.3%.

## Result

The given sample contains ____% of ascorbic acid.

## Precautions

1. Use freshly boiled and cooled water (dissolved oxygen oxidises ascorbic acid).
2. Titrate immediately after dissolving.
3. Keep iodine in amber bottles; iodine is volatile.
4. Acid medium slows air oxidation of ascorbic acid.

## Viva questions

1. **Difference between iodimetry and iodometry?** Iodimetry is direct titration with iodine; iodometry titrates liberated iodine with thiosulphate.
2. **Indicator used?** Starch (blue with iodine).
3. **Why is sulphuric acid added?** Ascorbic acid is more stable to air oxidation in acid.
4. **How is iodine solution prepared?** Iodine dissolved in potassium iodide solution (forms soluble KI3).
5. **Use of ascorbic acid?** Scurvy, antioxidant, enhances iron absorption.
