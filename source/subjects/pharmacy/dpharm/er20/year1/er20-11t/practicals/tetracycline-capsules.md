# Tetracycline Capsules (hard gelatin)

**Aim:** To fill and dispense 10 hard gelatin capsules each containing 250 mg of tetracycline hydrochloride.

## Principle

Hard gelatin capsules are filled with powder by the **punch (hand-filling) method** or with a **capsule filling board**. A diluent is added if needed to fill the capsule body completely so that every capsule has the same volume of powder. **Calcium-containing diluents (such as dicalcium phosphate) must not be used** because calcium forms a complex with tetracycline and reduces its absorption; lactose is suitable. A small amount of lubricant helps flow.

## Formula (for 10 capsules, with one extra for loss)

| Ingredient | Per capsule | For 11 capsules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tetracycline hydrochloride | 250 mg | 2.75 g |
| Lactose (diluent) | as needed to fill the shell | q.s. |
| Magnesium stearate | about 1% of fill | q.s. |

**Selecting the capsule size:** fill one empty shell of the chosen size (usually size 1 or 0) with the drug powder, weigh, and calculate the diluent needed to make the fill weight equal for all capsules.

## Requirements

Tetracycline hydrochloride, lactose, magnesium stearate, empty hard gelatin capsules, mortar and pestle, spatula, glass slab or capsule filling board, balance, gloves, airtight amber container.

## Procedure

1. Weigh the drug and diluent; mix by geometric dilution in a mortar; add lubricant and mix lightly.
2. Spread the powder on a clean slab to a uniform depth of about half the body length.
3. Hold the capsule body and press (punch) it repeatedly into the powder until filled; fit the cap. (With a filling board: place bodies in the holes, spread powder over, level with a spatula, then replace caps.)
4. Weigh each filled capsule and adjust to the calculated weight.
5. Wipe the capsules with a clean cloth to remove adhering powder.
6. Pack in a clean, dry, airtight container.

## Result

10 capsules of tetracycline hydrochloride 250 mg were filled and dispensed.

## Label

Tetracycline Capsules 250 mg, 10 capsules. Take as directed with water, not with milk or antacids. Complete the course. Store in a cool, dry place, protected from light.

## Precautions

1. Wear gloves; do not touch capsules with bare hands.
2. Avoid calcium salts as diluents.
3. Work in a dry atmosphere; shells soften in humidity and become brittle when too dry.

## Viva questions

1. **Why not take tetracycline with milk or antacids?** Calcium, magnesium and aluminium chelate tetracycline and reduce absorption.
2. **Largest and smallest capsule sizes?** 000 and 5.
3. **Composition of hard capsule shell?** Gelatin, water (13–16%), colour, opacifier.
4. **Disintegration time limit for capsules?** 30 minutes.
