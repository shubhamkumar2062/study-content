# Calamine Lotion

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 100 ml of Calamine Lotion.

## Principle

Calamine lotion is a **suspension** of insoluble powders (calamine and zinc oxide) for external use. **Bentonite** is the suspending agent and **sodium citrate** acts as a **flocculating agent**, giving a loose sediment that is easy to redisperse. Glycerin helps the powder stick to the skin, and liquefied phenol is an antipruritic and preservative. The lotion is mildly astringent, soothing and cooling and is used for itching, sunburn and insect bites.

## Formula (BP type)

| Ingredient | Quantity for 100 ml |
| --- | --- |
| Calamine | 15 g |
| Zinc oxide | 5 g |
| Bentonite | 3 g |
| Sodium citrate | 0.5 g |
| Liquefied phenol | 0.5 ml |
| Glycerin | 5 ml |
| Purified water (freshly boiled and cooled) | to 100 ml |

## Requirements

All ingredients, mortar and pestle, sieve, measuring cylinder, bottle.

## Procedure

1. Weigh calamine, zinc oxide and bentonite, sieve them and mix in a mortar.
2. Dissolve sodium citrate in about 70 ml of water.
3. Triturate the mixed powders with the glycerin and a little of the citrate solution to a smooth paste.
4. Add the rest of the citrate solution gradually with trituration.
5. Add the liquefied phenol and mix.
6. Transfer to a measuring cylinder and make up to 100 ml; pour into a bottle.

## Observations

Pink, smooth suspension. On standing a loose sediment forms which redisperses on gentle shaking.

## Result

100 ml of Calamine Lotion was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Calamine Lotion, 100 ml. **For external use only. Shake well before use.** Apply to the affected area.

## Precautions

1. Sieve powders to remove grit.
2. Make a smooth paste before diluting.
3. Handle phenol carefully (caustic).

## Viva questions

1. **What is calamine?** Zinc carbonate (or zinc oxide) coloured pink with a little ferric oxide.
2. **Role of bentonite?** Suspending agent.
3. **Role of sodium citrate?** Flocculating agent; gives loose, easily redispersed sediment.
4. **Role of glycerin?** Helps adhesion to the skin and wetting of powders.
5. **Lotion vs liniment?** Lotion is applied without friction; liniment with rubbing.
