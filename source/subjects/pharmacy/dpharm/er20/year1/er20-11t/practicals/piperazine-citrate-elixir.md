# Piperazine Citrate Elixir

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 50 ml of Piperazine Citrate Elixir.

> The formula below is a typical official-type formula used in D.Pharm laboratories. Use the formula and flavour given in your lab manual where they differ.

## Principle

An **elixir** is a clear, sweetened, flavoured liquid for oral use. Piperazine citrate is freely soluble in water; glycerin and syrup sweeten it and add body. It is an **anthelmintic** used against roundworm and threadworm.

## Formula

| Ingredient | For 100 ml | For 50 ml |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Piperazine citrate | 18.75 g | 9.375 g |
| Glycerin | 5 ml | 2.5 ml |
| Syrup | 62.5 ml | 31.25 ml |
| Flavour (lemon/orange) and colour | q.s. | q.s. |
| Purified water | to 100 ml | to 50 ml |

## Requirements

Piperazine citrate, glycerin, syrup, flavour, purified water, beaker, measuring cylinder, glass rod, filter paper, funnel, amber bottle.

## Procedure

1. Weigh the piperazine citrate and dissolve it in about 10 ml of purified water.
2. Add the glycerin and syrup and mix well.
3. Add flavour (and colour if used) and stir.
4. Make up the volume to 50 ml with purified water.
5. Filter if not clear and transfer to a clean bottle.

## Observations

Clear, sweet, flavoured liquid.

## Result

50 ml of Piperazine Citrate Elixir was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Piperazine Citrate Elixir, 50 ml. Dose: as directed by the physician. Store in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Weigh the drug accurately (potent anthelmintic).
2. Dissolve the drug completely before adding syrup.
3. Use a clean amber bottle.

## Viva questions

1. **What is an elixir?** A clear, sweetened, flavoured hydroalcoholic or aqueous liquid for oral use.
2. **Use of piperazine?** Anthelmintic for roundworm and threadworm.
3. **Role of glycerin?** Sweetener, solvent and adds viscosity.
4. **Difference between syrup and elixir?** Syrup is a concentrated sugar solution; elixir is less sweet, clear and usually contains alcohol.
