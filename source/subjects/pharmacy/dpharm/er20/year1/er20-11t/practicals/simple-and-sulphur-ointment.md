# Simple Ointment base and Sulphur Ointment

**Aim:** (a) To prepare 50 g of Simple Ointment (base) by fusion. (b) To prepare 20 g of Sulphur Ointment by incorporation (trituration).

## Principle

**Simple Ointment** is an oleaginous/absorption base. It is made by the **fusion method**: ingredients are melted together, starting with the one of highest melting point, and stirred until cool so the product is smooth. Wool fat helps the base take up some water; cetostearyl alcohol makes it stiffer and helps emulsify.

**Sulphur Ointment** contains 10% precipitated sulphur in Simple Ointment. Sulphur is insoluble, so it is incorporated by **levigation (trituration)** on an ointment slab. It is used for scabies, acne and fungal skin infections (keratolytic and parasiticide).

## Formula

**(a) Simple Ointment**

| Ingredient | For 100 g (master) | For 50 g |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wool fat | 5 g | 2.5 g |
| Hard paraffin | 5 g | 2.5 g |
| Cetostearyl alcohol | 5 g | 2.5 g |
| White (or yellow) soft paraffin | 85 g | 42.5 g |

**(b) Sulphur Ointment**

| Ingredient | For 100 g | For 20 g |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Precipitated sulphur (finely sifted) | 10 g | 2 g |
| Simple Ointment | 90 g | 18 g |

## Requirements

Ingredients, evaporating dish, water bath, glass rod, ointment slab, spatula, sieve, ointment jars.

## Procedure

**(a) Simple Ointment**

1. Weigh all ingredients.
2. Melt hard paraffin and cetostearyl alcohol together on a water bath.
3. Add wool fat and soft paraffin and melt.
4. Remove from the bath and stir continuously until the base cools and sets.
5. Fill into a jar.

**(b) Sulphur Ointment**

1. Sift the sulphur through a fine sieve.
2. Place sulphur on the ointment slab and levigate with a small amount of Simple Ointment using a spatula to make a smooth paste.
3. Add the rest of the base in portions (geometric dilution) and mix until uniform.
4. Fill into a jar, smooth the surface and close.

## Observations

Simple Ointment: smooth, white/yellowish, greasy semisolid. Sulphur Ointment: uniform pale yellow ointment, free from grit.

## Result

50 g of Simple Ointment and 20 g of Sulphur Ointment were prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Sulphur Ointment, 20 g. **For external use only.** Apply as directed.

## Precautions

1. Melt in decreasing order of melting point; avoid overheating.
2. Stir until set, otherwise the ointment becomes granular.
3. Sift the sulphur and levigate well so there are no gritty particles.

## Viva questions

1. **Method for simple ointment?** Fusion.
2. **Method for sulphur ointment?** Trituration/incorporation (levigation).
3. **Uses of sulphur ointment?** Scabies, acne, fungal infections.
4. **Classes of ointment bases?** Oleaginous, absorption, emulsion (w/o, o/w), water-soluble.
5. **Why stir till cool?** To get a smooth, uniform product.
