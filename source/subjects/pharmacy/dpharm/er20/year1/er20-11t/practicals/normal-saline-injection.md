# Sodium Chloride Injection (Normal Saline, 0.9% w/v)

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 100 ml of Sodium Chloride Injection IP (0.9% w/v).

## Principle

A **0.9% w/v** solution of sodium chloride is **isotonic** with blood (freezing point about −0.52 °C). It is used to replace fluid and electrolytes, as a vehicle for other injections, and to flush IV lines. Every injection must be **sterile, pyrogen-free, clear (free from particles)** and isotonic. It is prepared in **Water for Injections**, filtered, filled into clean containers, sealed and sterilised by **autoclaving at 121 °C for 15 minutes**.

## Formula

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Sodium chloride (injection grade) | 0.9 g |
| Water for Injections | to 100 ml |

## Requirements

Sodium chloride, Water for Injections, beaker, volumetric flask, sintered glass or membrane filter assembly, clean glass vials/ampoules or infusion bottle with closures, autoclave.

## Procedure

1. Wash containers with detergent, rinse with purified water and finally with Water for Injections; sterilise/dry as directed.
2. Weigh 0.9 g sodium chloride and dissolve in about 80 ml Water for Injections.
3. Make up to 100 ml in a volumetric flask.
4. Filter through a clean sintered glass or membrane filter until perfectly clear.
5. Fill into the containers (a slight excess over labelled volume), seal ampoules or close and crimp vials/bottles.
6. Sterilise in an autoclave at **121 °C for 15 minutes**.
7. Inspect for clarity against black and white backgrounds and do a leakage test for ampoules.

## Observations

Clear, colourless solution, free from visible particles.

## Result

100 ml of Sodium Chloride Injection (0.9% w/v) was prepared, sterilised and dispensed.

## Label

Sodium Chloride Injection IP 0.9% w/v, 100 ml. Sterile. For intravenous use. Batch number, date of manufacture and expiry. Discard any unused portion.

## Precautions

1. Use injection-grade materials and Water for Injections only.
2. Work in a clean area; avoid particles and fibres.
3. Check autoclave temperature and time.

## Viva questions

1. **Why 0.9%?** It is isotonic with blood.
2. **What is Water for Injections?** Pyrogen-free water prepared by distillation (or equivalent process), meant for making injections.
3. **Sterilisation used?** Autoclaving at 121 °C for 15 minutes.
4. **What are pyrogens?** Fever-producing substances, mainly bacterial endotoxins.
5. **Leakage test?** Ampoules placed in a dye solution under vacuum; leaking ones become coloured.
