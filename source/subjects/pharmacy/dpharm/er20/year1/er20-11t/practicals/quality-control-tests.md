# Quality control tests of common dosage forms

**Aim:** To demonstrate quality control tests and evaluation of tablets, capsules, emulsions and sterile injections as per the monographs.

## Principle

Pharmacopoeial tests check that each batch has uniform dose, releases the drug properly and is physically acceptable. Simple tests such as weight variation, hardness, friability, disintegration, emulsion type, clarity and leakage can be done in the laboratory.

## Requirements

Tablets, capsules, emulsion, ampoules; balance, hardness tester (Monsanto), Roche friabilator, disintegration apparatus, water bath, dye solution, black and white inspection board, vacuum desiccator.

## A. Tablets

**1. Uniformity of weight:** weigh 20 tablets together and individually; calculate the average and the % deviation of each.

```text
% deviation = (individual weight − average weight) ÷ average weight × 100
```

IP limits: ≤80 mg ±10%; 80–250 mg ±7.5%; ≥250 mg ±5%. Not more than 2 outside the limit and none outside double the limit.

**2. Hardness:** place a tablet between the anvils of the Monsanto tester and turn the screw until it breaks; read the force (kg). Test 5–10 tablets.

**3. Friability:** take tablets of about 6.5 g (or 10 tablets if each weighs more than 650 mg), dedust, weigh (W1); rotate 100 times at 25 rpm; dedust and weigh (W2).

```text
% friability = (W1 − W2) ÷ W1 × 100        Limit: not more than 1%
```

**4. Disintegration:** one tablet in each of the 6 tubes; water at 37 ± 2 °C; run the apparatus; note the time when no residue remains on the mesh. Uncoated: within 15 minutes.

## B. Capsules

1. **Uniformity of weight:** weigh 20 intact capsules individually, empty each, weigh the shells, find the content weight; limits ±10% (below 300 mg), ±7.5% (300 mg or more).
2. **Disintegration:** as for tablets (discs if specified); limit 30 minutes.

## C. Emulsion

1. **Type tests:** dilution test, dye test (amaranth/methylene blue), conductivity test.
2. **Stability:** keep a sample in a cylinder and note creaming or separation over days; centrifuge test.
3. **Globule size:** observe under a microscope.

## D. Sterile injections (ampoules)

1. **Clarity test:** hold each ampoule against black and white backgrounds under good light, swirl gently and check for particles.
2. **Leakage test:** dip ampoules (tips down) in a dye solution (e.g. 1% methylene blue) in a vacuum desiccator; apply and release vacuum; leaking ampoules become coloured and are rejected.
3. **pH and fill volume** as per monograph. (Sterility and pyrogen/endotoxin tests are demonstrated or explained.)

## Precautions

1. Dedust tablets before friability weighing.
2. Maintain 37 ± 2 °C in the disintegration bath.
3. Use calibrated balances and instruments.

## Viva questions

1. **Official tests for tablets?** Uniformity of weight, content uniformity, disintegration, dissolution, assay.
2. **Non-official tests?** Hardness, friability, thickness.
3. **Media for sterility test?** Fluid thioglycollate and soybean–casein digest media.
4. **What does the LAL test detect?** Bacterial endotoxins.
5. **Why leakage test only for ampoules?** Ampoules are sealed by fusion; a tiny crack lets contamination in.
