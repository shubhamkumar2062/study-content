# Aqueous Iodine Solution (Lugol's Solution)

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 50 ml of Aqueous Iodine Solution.

## Principle

Iodine is only very slightly soluble in water, but dissolves readily in a concentrated solution of potassium iodide because it forms the soluble **tri-iodide ion** (complexation):

```text
I2 + KI → KI3 (soluble)
```

So potassium iodide is first dissolved in a **small** amount of water, and the iodine is dissolved in this concentrated solution before dilution.

## Formula

| Ingredient | For 100 ml | For 50 ml |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iodine | 5 g | 2.5 g |
| Potassium iodide | 10 g | 5 g |
| Purified water | to 100 ml | to 50 ml |

## Requirements

Iodine, potassium iodide, purified water, glass-stoppered weighing bottle, beaker, measuring cylinder, glass rod, amber glass-stoppered bottle.

## Procedure

1. Weigh 5 g of potassium iodide and dissolve it in about 5 ml of purified water.
2. Weigh 2.5 g of iodine in a glass weighing bottle (iodine attacks metal pans and paper).
3. Add the iodine to the concentrated KI solution and stir until completely dissolved.
4. Make up to 50 ml with purified water and mix.
5. Transfer to an amber bottle with a glass stopper.

## Observations

Deep brown, clear solution with the smell of iodine.

## Result

50 ml of Aqueous Iodine Solution was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Aqueous Iodine Solution, 50 ml. Store in a cool place, protected from light, in a glass-stoppered bottle.

## Precautions

1. Dissolve KI in minimum water first, otherwise iodine will not dissolve.
2. Do not weigh iodine on paper or metal; use glass.
3. Avoid rubber or cork closures (iodine attacks them).

## Viva questions

1. **Why is KI added?** It forms soluble KI3 with iodine (complexation).
2. **Uses?** Iodine supplement, before thyroid surgery to reduce gland vascularity, in thyrotoxicosis.
3. **Why amber bottle?** To protect from light.
4. **Other name?** Lugol's solution.
