# Paracetamol Tablets (wet granulation)

**Aim:** To prepare paracetamol tablets (500 mg) by the wet granulation method and evaluate them.

> Batch size and excipient quantities are typical. Use the formula in your lab manual.

## Principle

Paracetamol powder flows and compresses poorly, so it is **granulated**. In wet granulation the drug and diluent/disintegrant are massed with a **binder solution** (starch paste), screened, dried, rescreened, lubricated and compressed. Starch acts as disintegrant; magnesium stearate and talc act as lubricant and glidant.

## Formula (for 50 tablets of 500 mg paracetamol)

| Ingredient | Per tablet | For 50 tablets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paracetamol | 500 mg | 25 g |
| Starch (disintegrant, intragranular) | 40 mg | 2 g |
| Starch paste 10% (binder) | q.s. | q.s. |
| Starch (disintegrant, extragranular) | 20 mg | 1 g |
| Magnesium stearate | 5 mg | 0.25 g |
| Talc | 10 mg | 0.5 g |

## Requirements

Ingredients, mortar and pestle or planetary mixer, sieves (no. 10/12 and no. 16/20), tray dryer or oven, tablet machine (single punch), hardness tester, friabilator, disintegration apparatus.

## Procedure

1. **Starch paste:** disperse starch in cold water and heat with stirring until a translucent paste forms; cool.
2. **Dry mixing:** sieve paracetamol and starch; mix.
3. **Wet massing:** add starch paste gradually and knead until a damp mass forms that holds together when squeezed but crumbles when pressed (snow-ball test).
4. **Wet screening:** pass through sieve no. 10–12.
5. **Drying:** dry granules at about 50–60 °C until moisture is suitably low.
6. **Dry screening:** pass through sieve no. 16–20.
7. **Lubrication:** add the extragranular starch, talc and magnesium stearate (sieved) and mix gently.
8. **Compression:** set the die fill for the target weight and compress on a single punch machine.
9. Evaluate weight variation, hardness, friability and disintegration.

## Observations

| Test | Result | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Average weight | | ±5% (250 mg or more) |
| Hardness | | about 4–6 kg |
| Friability | | not more than 1% |
| Disintegration | | 15 minutes (uncoated) |

## Result

Paracetamol tablets 500 mg were prepared by wet granulation and evaluated.

## Label

Paracetamol Tablets IP 500 mg. Dose as directed; do not exceed the stated daily dose.

## Precautions

1. Do not over-wet the mass (hard granules) or under-wet it (fines, capping).
2. Dry granules properly to avoid sticking and picking.
3. Mix lubricants gently and briefly.

## Viva questions

1. **Why granulate?** To improve flow, compressibility and content uniformity.
2. **Role of starch?** Binder (as paste), disintegrant (dry), diluent.
3. **Causes of capping?** Air entrapment, too many fines, too dry granules.
4. **Friability limit?** Not more than 1% loss.
