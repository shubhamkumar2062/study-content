# Demonstration of the stages of tablet manufacture

**Aim:** To study the stages of tablet manufacturing and the equipment used at each stage.

## Principle

A tablet is made by a chain of unit operations. In industry each stage is done with dedicated equipment under GMP, with in-process checks so that every tablet has the right weight, strength, hardness and appearance.

## Stages and equipment

```text
DISPENSING → SIFTING → DRY MIXING → WET GRANULATION → WET MILLING
   → DRYING → DRY MILLING / SIZING → LUBRICATION (BLENDING)
   → COMPRESSION → (COATING) → INSPECTION → PACKING
```

| Stage | Equipment | What to observe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dispensing | Calibrated balances, dispensing booth with dust extraction | Weighing checked by a second person |
| Sifting | Vibratory sifter with mesh | Removal of lumps and foreign matter |
| Dry mixing / wet granulation | Rapid mixer granulator (impeller + chopper), planetary mixer | Binder added; end point of wet mass |
| Wet milling | Multi-mill or oscillating granulator | Uniform wet granules |
| Drying | Fluidized bed dryer or tray dryer | Loss on drying (moisture) checked |
| Dry milling / sizing | Multi-mill, sifter | Granule size distribution |
| Lubrication / blending | Double cone, octagonal or bin blender | Short blending time with lubricant |
| Compression | Rotary tablet press (die table, punches, cams, pressure rollers) | Weight, hardness, thickness, friability, disintegration checked at intervals |
| Coating | Coating pan or perforated pan, spray guns | Weight gain, appearance |
| Inspection | Inspection belt | Remove defective tablets (capping, chipping, spots) |
| Packing | Strip and blister packing machines | Seal integrity, batch printing |

## Procedure (demonstration)

1. Observe or watch each stage in the lab or on a demonstration model/video, or during the industrial visit.
2. Note the principle and main parts of each machine.
3. Note the in-process checks done at each stage.
4. Draw a flow chart and a labelled diagram of a rotary tablet press.

## Observations

Record the machines seen, their function and the in-process tests in a table like the one above.

## Result

The stages of tablet manufacture and the equipment and in-process controls used at each stage were studied.

## Precautions

1. Wear gloves, mask and cap in the processing area.
2. Do not touch moving machine parts.
3. Follow the instructions of the supervisor.

## Viva questions

1. **What is a rapid mixer granulator?** A mixer with an impeller and chopper used for dry mixing and wet granulation.
2. **How is drying end point checked?** By loss on drying (moisture content).
3. **Parts of a rotary press?** Hopper, feed frame, die table (turret), upper and lower punches, cams, pressure rollers.
4. **Why is lubricant blended briefly?** Over-mixing coats particles, softening tablets and slowing dissolution.
5. **What are in-process controls?** Checks during manufacture such as weight, hardness, thickness, friability and disintegration.
