# Cod Liver Oil Emulsion

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 100 ml of Cod Liver Oil Emulsion.

> Quantities are typical for a student batch. Follow your lab manual where it differs.

## Principle

Cod liver oil is a **fixed oil** rich in **vitamins A and D**. It is emulsified with acacia (primary emulsion **4 : 2 : 1**) to make an **o/w emulsion**, which masks the fishy taste and odour and is easier to take. A sweetener and flavour improve palatability. Vitamins A and D are sensitive to light and air, so the product is stored in a well-filled amber bottle.

## Formula

| Ingredient | Quantity for 100 ml |
| --- | --- |
| Cod liver oil | 40 ml |
| Acacia | 10 g |
| Purified water (primary emulsion) | 20 ml |
| Syrup | 10 ml |
| Flavour (e.g. cinnamon or peppermint) | q.s. |
| Preservative (as per manual) | q.s. |
| Purified water | to 100 ml |

## Requirements

Cod liver oil, acacia, syrup, flavour, preservative, purified water, dry mortar and pestle, measuring cylinders, amber bottle.

## Procedure

1. Place 40 ml cod liver oil in a dry mortar and add 10 g acacia; triturate briefly.
2. Add 20 ml water at once and triturate rapidly until a white, clicking primary emulsion forms.
3. Dilute gradually with water while triturating.
4. Add syrup, preservative and flavour.
5. Make up to 100 ml with water, transfer to an amber bottle and shake.

## Observations

White, creamy emulsion; dilutes easily with water (o/w). On standing it may cream; shaking redisperses it.

## Result

100 ml of Cod Liver Oil Emulsion was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Cod Liver Oil Emulsion, 100 ml. Shake well before use. Store in a cool place, protected from light.

## Precautions

1. Use a dry mortar.
2. Do not delay adding the water for the primary emulsion.
3. Fill the bottle well and protect from light and air.

## Viva questions

1. **Vitamins present in cod liver oil?** Vitamins A and D.
2. **Why amber bottle?** Vitamins are light-sensitive and the oil can go rancid.
3. **Creaming or cracking – which is reversible?** Creaming.
4. **Test for type of emulsion?** Dilution, dye, conductivity tests.
