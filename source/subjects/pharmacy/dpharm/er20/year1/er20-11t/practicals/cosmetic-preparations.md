# Common cosmetic preparations

**Aim:** To prepare at least five commonly used cosmetic preparations: cold cream, vanishing cream, shampoo, toothpaste and a hand/body lotion.

> Cosmetic formulas vary widely. The formulas below are typical laboratory formulas; use those in your manual where they differ. Each preparation is about 50 g or 50 ml.

## 1. Cold cream (w/o)

**Principle:** borax reacts with the free fatty acids (cerotic acid) of beeswax to form a **sodium soap** in situ, which emulsifies water in the oil phase, giving a **w/o** cream. Water evaporating from the skin gives a cooling feel.

| Ingredient | For 50 g |
| --- | --- |
| White beeswax | 8 g |
| Liquid paraffin | 25 g |
| Borax | 0.5 g |
| Purified water | 16.5 g |
| Perfume (rose) | q.s. |

**Procedure:** melt beeswax with liquid paraffin at about 70 °C. Dissolve borax in water heated to the same temperature. Add the borax solution slowly to the oil phase with stirring; stir until cool; add perfume below about 40 °C; fill into a jar.

## 2. Vanishing cream (o/w)

**Principle:** part of the **stearic acid** is neutralised by alkali to form a soap (potassium stearate), which emulsifies the rest of the stearic acid as an **o/w** cream. It spreads and "vanishes" leaving a thin non-greasy film.

| Ingredient | For 50 g |
| --- | --- |
| Stearic acid | 8 g |
| Potassium hydroxide | 0.5 g |
| Glycerin | 4 g |
| Purified water | to 50 g |
| Perfume, preservative | q.s. |

**Procedure:** melt stearic acid at about 70–75 °C. Dissolve KOH in water, add glycerin and heat to the same temperature. Add the aqueous phase to the melted acid with stirring; stir until cool and creamy; add perfume.

## 3. Shampoo (clear liquid)

| Ingredient | For 50 ml |
| --- | --- |
| Sodium lauryl ether sulphate (detergent) | 15 g |
| Coconut diethanolamide (foam booster, thickener) | 1.5 g |
| Sodium chloride (thickener) | about 0.5 g |
| Citric acid (to adjust pH about 5.5–6.5) | q.s. |
| Preservative, colour, perfume | q.s. |
| Purified water | to 50 ml |

**Procedure:** dissolve the detergent in most of the water with slow stirring (avoid foaming); add diethanolamide, preservative, colour and perfume; adjust pH; add salt slowly to thicken; make up to volume.

## 4. Toothpaste

| Ingredient | For 50 g | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calcium carbonate or dicalcium phosphate | 22 g | Abrasive (cleaning) |
| Glycerin or sorbitol | 12 g | Humectant (keeps paste moist) |
| Sodium carboxymethylcellulose | 0.6 g | Binder / thickener |
| Sodium lauryl sulphate | 0.75 g | Foaming agent, detergent |
| Saccharin sodium | 0.05 g | Sweetener |
| Peppermint oil | q.s. | Flavour |
| Preservative | q.s. | |
| Purified water | to 50 g | |

**Procedure:** disperse sodium CMC in glycerin, add water and allow to swell into a gel; add saccharin and preservative; add the abrasive in portions with thorough mixing; add SLS and flavour last with gentle mixing; fill into a tube.

## 5. Hand and body lotion (o/w)

| Ingredient | For 50 ml |
| --- | --- |
| Stearic acid | 1.5 g |
| Cetyl alcohol | 0.5 g |
| Liquid paraffin | 3 g |
| Triethanolamine | 0.5 g |
| Glycerin | 2.5 g |
| Preservative, perfume | q.s. |
| Purified water | to 50 ml |

**Procedure:** heat the oil phase (stearic acid, cetyl alcohol, liquid paraffin) and the aqueous phase (water, glycerin, triethanolamine) separately to about 70 °C; add aqueous to oil phase with stirring (triethanolamine stearate forms as emulsifier); stir until cool; add perfume.

## Result

Cold cream, vanishing cream, shampoo, toothpaste and hand lotion were prepared and packed.

## Precautions

1. Heat oil and water phases to the same temperature before mixing.
2. Add perfumes and volatile ingredients only after cooling.
3. Avoid air entrapment and excessive foaming.
4. Use clean, dry containers and add preservatives.

## Viva questions

1. **Emulsifier in cold cream?** Sodium soap formed from borax and free fatty acids of beeswax.
2. **Type of vanishing cream?** Oil-in-water.
3. **Role of humectant in toothpaste?** Keeps it moist and prevents hardening.
4. **Function of sodium lauryl sulphate?** Detergent and foaming agent.
5. **Why is shampoo pH kept slightly acidic?** To suit the scalp and hair and reduce irritation.
