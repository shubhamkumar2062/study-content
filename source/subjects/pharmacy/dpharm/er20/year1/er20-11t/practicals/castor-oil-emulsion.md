# Castor Oil Emulsion

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense 100 ml of Castor Oil Emulsion by the dry gum method.

> Quantities are typical for a student batch. Follow your lab manual where it differs.

## Principle

Castor oil is a **fixed oil**. With acacia as the emulsifying agent it forms an **oil-in-water** emulsion. In the dry gum method, the primary emulsion is made in the ratio **oil : water : gum = 4 : 2 : 1** for fixed oils. Acacia forms a protective film around each oil globule and prevents them from joining. Castor oil is a **stimulant laxative**; the emulsion masks its unpleasant taste and oiliness.

## Formula

| Ingredient | Quantity for 100 ml |
| --- | --- |
| Castor oil | 40 ml |
| Acacia (powdered) | 10 g |
| Purified water (for primary emulsion) | 20 ml |
| Syrup | 10 ml |
| Flavour (e.g. peppermint or cinnamon water) | q.s. |
| Purified water | to 100 ml |

## Requirements

Castor oil, acacia, syrup, flavour, purified water, dry porcelain mortar and pestle, measuring cylinders, bottle.

## Procedure

1. Measure 40 ml castor oil into a **dry** mortar.
2. Add 10 g acacia and triturate lightly just to disperse it (do not over-mix).
3. Add all 20 ml of water **at once** and triturate rapidly in one direction until a thick, white primary emulsion forms with a **clicking (crackling) sound**.
4. Add more water slowly with trituration to dilute.
5. Add syrup and flavour and mix.
6. Transfer to a measuring cylinder, rinse the mortar with water, and make up to 100 ml.
7. Transfer to a bottle and shake.

## Observations

Thick, white, creamy liquid. **Dilution test:** a drop mixes readily with water → o/w emulsion.

## Result

100 ml of Castor Oil Emulsion (o/w) was prepared and dispensed.

## Label

Castor Oil Emulsion, 100 ml. Shake well before use. Store in a cool place.

## Precautions

1. Mortar, pestle and measuring cylinder for oil must be dry.
2. Add the water for the primary emulsion all at once.
3. Triturate quickly in one direction until the click is heard.

## Viva questions

1. **Ratio for a fixed oil?** 4 : 2 : 1 (oil : water : gum).
2. **Type of emulsion?** Oil-in-water.
3. **Why is the mortar dry?** Water would wet the gum first and form lumps.
4. **Wet gum method?** Gum is made into mucilage with water first, then oil is added gradually.
5. **Use of castor oil?** Stimulant laxative.
