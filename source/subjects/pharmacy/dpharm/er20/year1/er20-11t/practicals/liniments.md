# Turpentine Liniment and White Liniment

**Aim:** To prepare and dispense (a) 50 ml of Turpentine Liniment and (b) 50 ml of White Liniment.

## Principle

Liniments are liquid preparations rubbed into the skin with friction. Both these liniments are **emulsions** containing **turpentine oil**, a counter-irritant and rubefacient used for muscle pain, sprains and stiffness.

- **Turpentine Liniment:** soft soap (potassium soap) is the emulsifying agent; camphor adds counter-irritant action.
- **White Liniment:** oleic acid reacts with ammonia to form **ammonium oleate** soap **in situ**, which emulsifies the turpentine oil; ammonium chloride helps give a creamy, stable product.

## Formula

**(a) Turpentine Liniment**

| Ingredient | For 1000 ml (master) | For 50 ml |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camphor | 50 g | 2.5 g |
| Soft soap | 75 g | 3.75 g |
| Turpentine oil | 650 ml | 32.5 ml |
| Purified water | 225 ml | 11.25 ml |

**(b) White Liniment**

| Ingredient | For 1000 ml (master) | For 50 ml |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ammonium chloride | 12.5 g | 0.625 g |
| Dilute ammonia solution | 45 ml | 2.25 ml |
| Oleic acid | 85 ml | 4.25 ml |
| Turpentine oil | 250 ml | 12.5 ml |
| Purified water | 625 ml | 31.25 ml |

## Requirements

Ingredients, mortar and pestle, measuring cylinders, bottles (coloured, fluted if available).

## Procedure

**(a) Turpentine Liniment**

1. Dissolve camphor in the turpentine oil.
2. Triturate the soft soap with the water in a mortar to a smooth liquid.
3. Add the camphor–turpentine solution in small portions with vigorous trituration (or shaking in a bottle) until a creamy emulsion forms.
4. Transfer to a bottle.

**(b) White Liniment**

1. Mix the oleic acid with the turpentine oil in a bottle.
2. Dissolve the ammonium chloride in the water and add the dilute ammonia solution.
3. Add this aqueous solution to the oily mixture and shake vigorously until a white creamy emulsion forms.

## Observations

Both form white, creamy emulsions with a turpentine smell.

## Result

50 ml each of Turpentine Liniment and White Liniment were prepared and dispensed.

## Label

**For external use only. Not to be applied to broken or inflamed skin. Shake well before use.** Rub into the affected part. Flammable – keep away from flame.

## Precautions

1. Turpentine oil is flammable and irritant – no open flames.
2. Add the oil phase gradually with vigorous mixing.
3. Use a coloured, fluted (poison-type) bottle as per lab practice.

## Viva questions

1. **What is a liniment?** Liquid or semi-liquid preparation applied with friction.
2. **Emulsifier in white liniment?** Ammonium oleate formed in situ from oleic acid and ammonia.
3. **Emulsifier in turpentine liniment?** Soft soap.
4. **Action of turpentine oil?** Counter-irritant and rubefacient.
5. **Why not on broken skin?** Causes irritation and absorption of irritants.
