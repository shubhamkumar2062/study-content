## Unit operations (Unit 3)

| Equipment | Principle | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hammer mill | Impact | Milling granules, crude drugs |
| Ball mill | Impact + attrition; best at 1/2–2/3 of critical speed | Fine grinding, wet or dry |
| Cyclone separator | Centrifugal force | Separating solids from air |
| Double cone blender | Tumbling | Dry powder blending |
| Turbine mixer | High-speed impeller | Liquids |
| Triple roller mill | Shear between rollers at different speeds | Ointments, pastes |
| Silverson | Rotor–stator high shear | Emulsions, creams |
| Membrane filter | Sieving; 0.22 µm sterilises | Sterile filtration |
| Sintered glass filter | Fused glass particles; grades 1–5 | Corrosive liquids; grade 5 holds bacteria |
| Fluidized bed dryer | Hot air suspends granules | Fast drying of granules |
| Freeze dryer | Sublimation below triple point | Heat-sensitive biologicals |

IP powder grades: coarse 10/44, moderately coarse 22/60, moderately fine 44/85, fine 85, very fine 120.
Extraction: infusion, decoction, digestion, maceration (7 days), percolation, Soxhlet; solvent = menstruum, residue = marc.

## Tablets and capsules (Unit 4)

| Test | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Weight variation (IP) | ≤80 mg ±10%; 80–250 mg ±7.5%; ≥250 mg ±5% (20 tablets; ≤2 outside, none at double) |
| Disintegration | Uncoated 15 min, film 30 min, sugar 60 min, dispersible 3 min; enteric: intact 2 h in 0.1 M HCl, then 60 min in pH 6.8 |
| Friability | 25 rpm, 100 revolutions; loss ≤1% |
| Hardness | About 4–6 kg |
| Dissolution | 900 ml, 37 ± 0.5 °C; basket 100 rpm, paddle 50 rpm |
| Capsule weight variation | <300 mg ±10%; ≥300 mg ±7.5% |
| Capsule disintegration | 30 min |

Defects: capping/lamination (air, fines, dry granules), picking/sticking (moisture, low lubricant), mottling (colour migration). Sugar coating: seal → subcoat → smooth → colour → polish → print. Capsule sizes 000 (largest) → 5; softgels by rotary die with plasticiser (glycerin).

## Liquids and topicals (Unit 5)

- Simple syrup 66.7% w/w (IP); elixir = sweet hydroalcoholic.
- Emulsion primary ratio (oil : water : gum): fixed 4:2:1, volatile 2:2:1, mineral 3:2:1.
- o/w dilutes with water and conducts; w/o does not.
- Creaming reversible, cracking irreversible; Stokes: v = 2r²(ρ1 − ρ2)g / 9η.
- Flocculated: fast settling, loose sediment, no cake.
- Ointment bases: oleaginous, absorption, emulsion (w/o, o/w), water-soluble (PEG).
- Liniment with rubbing; lotion without rubbing; paste = high solids.
- Suppository bases: theobroma oil (melts 30–35 °C, polymorphism), glycerinated gelatin, PEG; displacement value.

## Powders, sterile and immunological (Unit 6)

- Insufflation = powder blown into cavities; sterile dusting powder for wounds.
- Effervescent granules: citric : tartaric 1 : 2; fusion uses citric acid's water of crystallisation.
- Injections: sterile, pyrogen-free, particle-free, isotonic (0.9% NaCl, 5% dextrose; −0.52 °C).
- Sterilisation: autoclave 121 °C/15 min; dry heat 160 °C/2 h; filtration 0.22 µm; ethylene oxide; gamma rays.
- QC: sterility (thioglycollate, soybean–casein digest), pyrogen (rabbit), endotoxin (LAL), leakage (dye), clarity.
- Eye drops sterile, isotonic, preserved; discard 4 weeks after opening.
- Vaccines = active immunity (live, killed, toxoid, subunit); sera = passive immunity (horse antitoxins, ASV).
- Toxoid = toxin + formaldehyde, adsorbed on aluminium salts; store vaccines 2–8 °C, never freeze adsorbed vaccines.

## Plant, QC, QA (Unit 7)

- Schedule M = GMP in India. One-way flow; sterile Grades A–D.
- QC = testing (product, reactive); QA = all systems (process, proactive). QA ⊃ GMP ⊃ QC.
- Calibration = instrument vs standard; validation = documented evidence of consistent results.
- Validation types: prospective, concurrent, retrospective, revalidation; DQ, IQ, OQ, PQ.

## NDDS (Unit 8)

- Aim: control rate, duration or site of release; steady levels.
- Examples: OROS (osmotic), transdermal patches (nitroglycerin, nicotine, fentanyl, scopolamine), Ocusert (pilocarpine), levonorgestrel implant/IUD, liposomal amphotericin B.
- Challenges: cost, dose dumping, fixed dose, toxicity cannot be stopped quickly.
