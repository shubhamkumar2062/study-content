# Microscopic examination of tissues (permanent slides)

**Aim:** To study epithelial tissue, cardiac muscle, smooth muscle, skeletal muscle, connective tissue and nervous tissue under the microscope using pre-prepared slides.

## Principle

Thin stained sections show the shape, arrangement and special features of the cells of each tissue. Haematoxylin stains nuclei blue-purple and eosin stains cytoplasm and fibres pink (H and E stain).

## Requirements

Compound microscope, permanent slides of the tissues, immersion oil, lens paper, record book and pencil.

## Procedure

1. Focus each slide first under low power (10×) to see the general pattern.
2. Change to high power (40×) to see cell details.
3. Draw a labelled diagram in pencil and note identifying features.

## Observations

| Slide | Identifying features | Location / function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Simple squamous epithelium** | Single layer of flat cells with central flat nuclei, like floor tiles | Alveoli, blood vessels; diffusion |
| **Simple cuboidal epithelium** | Single layer of square cells with round central nuclei | Kidney tubules, glands; secretion |
| **Simple columnar epithelium** | Tall cells, oval nuclei at the base, goblet cells | Stomach, intestine; absorption |
| **Stratified squamous epithelium** | Many layers; basal cells cuboidal, surface cells flat | Skin, oesophagus; protection |
| **Skeletal muscle** (longitudinal section) | Long unbranched fibres, cross-striations, many peripheral nuclei | Attached to bones; voluntary |
| **Cardiac muscle** | Branched striated fibres, one central nucleus, dark intercalated discs | Heart; involuntary |
| **Smooth muscle** | Spindle-shaped cells, one central nucleus, no striations | Gut, vessels; involuntary |
| **Areolar connective tissue** | Loose network of pink collagen and thin elastic fibres with scattered cells | Under the skin; binds organs |
| **Hyaline cartilage** | Glassy matrix, chondrocytes in lacunae, often in pairs | Trachea, joint surfaces |
| **Compact bone (transverse section)** | Haversian systems – central canal, concentric lamellae, lacunae with canaliculi | Skeleton |
| **Nervous tissue (neurons)** | Large multipolar cell bodies with processes; nucleus with prominent nucleolus | Brain, spinal cord |

## Precautions

1. Begin with low power before high power.
2. Handle slides by the edges; do not break cover slips.
3. Draw exactly what is seen; label with straight lines.

## Viva questions

1. **How do you identify cardiac muscle?** Branched striated fibres with central nuclei and intercalated discs.
2. **What are goblet cells?** Mucus-secreting cells in columnar epithelium.
3. **What is a Haversian system?** The unit of compact bone – central canal with rings of lamellae and osteocytes.
4. **Which epithelium lines the alveoli?** Simple squamous.
5. **What does eosin stain?** Cytoplasm and fibres pink.
