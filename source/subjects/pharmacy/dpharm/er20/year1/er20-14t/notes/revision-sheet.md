## Unit 1 – Cell and tissues

- Anatomy = structure; physiology = function; homeostasis mostly by **negative feedback** (positive: labour, clotting).
- Mitochondria = ATP; ribosome = protein; Golgi = packing; lysosome = digestion; nucleus = control; smooth ER = lipids, detox.
- 4 tissues: epithelial, connective, muscular, nervous. Tendon = muscle–bone; ligament = bone–bone.
- Transitional epithelium = bladder; pseudostratified ciliated = trachea; stratified squamous keratinised = skin.

## Unit 2 – Bones, joints, muscle

- 206 bones (axial 80 + appendicular 126). Vertebrae 7-12-5 + sacrum + coccyx. Ribs 7 true, 3 false, 2 floating.
- Osteoblast builds, osteoclast breaks, osteocyte maintains.
- Joints: fibrous (sutures), cartilaginous (discs), synovial (free). Ball and socket = hip; hinge = knee; pivot = atlas–axis.
- Gout = urate crystals; RA = autoimmune; OA = wear.
- Contraction: ACh → Ca2+ from SR → troponin → cross-bridges → I band and H zone shorten. Myasthenia gravis = anti-ACh-receptor antibodies.

## Unit 3 – Blood and lymph

- Plasma 55 %, cells 45 %; serum = plasma − fibrinogen. Haemopoiesis in red marrow; erythropoietin from kidney.
- Clotting: prothrombin activator → thrombin → fibrinogen → fibrin; Ca2+ and vitamin K (II, VII, IX, X). Haemophilia A = factor VIII.
- O universal donor, AB universal recipient; Rh − mother + Rh + baby → anti-D.
- Lymph: less protein; thoracic duct → left subclavian vein. Spleen = graveyard of RBCs (left upper abdomen).

## Unit 4 – Heart and circulation

- RA → tricuspid → RV → pulmonary valve → lungs → LA → mitral → LV → aortic valve → body.
- SA node (pacemaker) → AV node → bundle of His → branches → Purkinje fibres.
- Cycle 0.8 s: atrial systole 0.1, ventricular systole 0.3, diastole 0.4. S1 = AV valves close, S2 = semilunar valves close.
- ECG: P atria depolarise, QRS ventricles depolarise, T ventricles repolarise; PR 0.12–0.20 s.
- BP regulation: baroreceptors (carotid sinus, aortic arch) → medulla; RAAS, ADH, adrenaline raise; ANP lowers.

## Unit 5 – Respiration

- Right lung 3 lobes, left 2. Surfactant (type II cells) prevents collapse.
- Inspiration active (diaphragm, external intercostals); quiet expiration passive.
- O2 97 % as oxyHb; CO2 70 % as HCO3− (carbonic anhydrase, chloride shift).
- Medulla = rhythm; pons = pneumotaxic/apneustic; CO2 = strongest stimulus.
- VC = TV + IRV + ERV; TLC = VC + RV; FRC = ERV + RV; IC = TV + IRV.

## Unit 6 – Digestion

- Wall: mucosa, submucosa (Meissner), muscularis (Auerbach), serosa.
- Parietal cells → HCl + intrinsic factor; chief cells → pepsinogen; G cells → gastrin.
- Villi + microvilli; lacteals absorb fat (chylomicrons); sugars and amino acids → portal blood.
- Liver: bile, glycogen, urea, plasma proteins, detoxification. Bile has no enzyme; emulsifies fat; CCK contracts gall bladder.
- Pancreatic juice alkaline: trypsin (enterokinase), amylase, lipase.

## Unit 7 – Nervous system

- CNS + PNS (12 cranial, 31 spinal pairs). Dendrite → soma → axon; myelin → saltatory conduction.
- Frontal (motor, Broca), parietal (sensory), temporal (hearing, Wernicke), occipital (vision).
- Cerebellum = balance, coordination; hypothalamus = temperature, thirst, hunger, ADH, pituitary; medulla = vital centres; basal ganglia = smooth movement (Parkinson's).
- Spinal cord ends L1–L2; dorsal root sensory, ventral root motor; reflex arc: receptor → sensory → centre → motor → effector.
- Cranial nerves: Olfactory, Optic, Oculomotor, Trochlear, Trigeminal, Abducens, Facial, Vestibulocochlear, Glossopharyngeal, Vagus, Accessory, Hypoglossal.
- Sympathetic: thoracolumbar, noradrenaline, fight or flight. Parasympathetic: craniosacral, ACh, rest and digest.

## Unit 8 – Sense organs

- Eye: sclera/cornea, choroid/ciliary body/iris, retina. Rods = dim light, rhodopsin (vit A); cones = colour, fovea. Blind spot = optic disc. Myopia → concave lens.
- Ear: pinna, canal | eardrum, malleus, incus, stapes, Eustachian tube | cochlea (organ of Corti – hearing), vestibule + semicircular canals (balance).
- Skin: epidermis (keratin, melanin), dermis (glands, receptors), vitamin D, temperature control.
- Taste: VII front two-thirds, IX back third; 5 tastes. Smell: nerve I, olfactory bulb.

## Unit 9 – Urinary system

- Nephron: glomerulus + Bowman's capsule, PCT, loop of Henle, DCT, collecting duct.
- Filtration (NFP ≈ 10 mmHg) → reabsorption (PCT takes all glucose) → secretion (H+, K+, drugs).
- ADH → water in collecting duct; aldosterone → Na+ in, K+ out.
- RAAS: renin → angiotensin I → ACE → angiotensin II → vasoconstriction + aldosterone.
- Clearance C = UV/P; inulin = GFR; creatinine ≈ GFR; PAH = renal plasma flow.
- Micturition reflex: S2–S4, parasympathetic contracts detrusor.

## Unit 10 – Endocrine

- Anterior pituitary: GH, TSH, ACTH, FSH, LH, prolactin. Posterior: ADH, oxytocin (made in hypothalamus).
- Thyroid T3/T4 (iodine, BMR) + calcitonin (lowers Ca2+). PTH raises Ca2+; lack → tetany.
- Adrenal cortex: aldosterone (salt), cortisol (sugar), androgens (sex). Medulla: adrenaline.
- Insulin (β) lowers glucose; glucagon (α) raises it. Diabetes: polyuria, polydipsia, polyphagia.
- Disorders: gigantism/acromegaly, diabetes insipidus, cretinism, myxoedema, Graves', goitre, Addison's, Cushing's.

## Unit 11 – Reproduction

- Testis: seminiferous tubules (sperm), Leydig cells (testosterone); epididymis stores and matures sperm.
- 1 primary spermatocyte → 4 sperm; 1 primary oocyte → 1 ovum + polar bodies.
- Fertilisation in ampulla of uterine tube; implantation day 6–7.
- Menstrual cycle: menses 1–5, proliferative (FSH, oestrogen), ovulation day 14 (LH surge), secretory (progesterone).
- Parturition: oxytocin positive feedback; stages – dilation, expulsion, placental.
