# Last-night revision sheet

If you have only an hour, revise these five areas with full answers: **evaluation of crude drugs and adulteration**, **classification of drugs**, **monographs of senna, digitalis, cinchona, rauwolfia, opium and clove**, **tests for alkaloids and glycosides**, and **Ayurvedic dosage forms (asava–arishta, bhasma, taila)**.

## Chapter 1–3 in ten lines

- Pharmacognosy = *pharmakon* (drug) + *gnosis* (knowledge); Schmidt 1811, Seydler 1815.
- Organised drugs have cells (leaf, bark, root); unorganised drugs do not (gum, resin, latex, oil).
- Six classifications: alphabetical (pharmacopoeias), taxonomical, morphological, pharmacological, chemical, chemo-taxonomical.
- Evaluation: morphological, microscopical, physical, chemical, biological.
- Stomatal index = S ÷ (S + E) × 100. Lycopodium spores ≈ 94,000 per mg.
- Acid-insoluble ash = sand and earth; water-soluble ash helps detect exhausted drugs.
- Bioassay: digitalis (pigeon/frog heart), ergot.

## Chapter 6–12 key points

- **Cotton**: seed hairs of *Gossypium*, cellulose, twisted ribbons. **Silk**: fibroin + sericin, no sulphur. **Wool**: keratin with sulphur, scaly. **Viscose**: regenerated cellulose.
- **Catgut**: collagen from sheep intestine submucosa; absorbable; chromic = slower.
- **Ayurveda**: tridosha (vata, pitta, kapha), panchamahabhuta. **Unani**: four humours (dam, balgham, safra, sauda). **Siddha**: 96 thathuvas, Agastya. **Homoeopathy**: Hahnemann 1796, similia similibus curentur.
- **Asava** = juice, no boiling, fermented; **Arishta** = decoction, fermented; fermenting agent Dhataki flowers. **Bhasma** = shodhana + marana; tests rekhapurnatva, varitara. **Taila** kalka : oil : drava = 1 : 4 : 16.
- **Exports**: isabgol, senna, guar gum, mint oil, spices; NMPB (2000).
- **Nutraceutical** coined by DeFelice (1989). Probiotics = live microbes; prebiotics = inulin, FOS. Omega-3: ALA, EPA, DHA. Spirulina protein-rich. Garlic: alliin → allicin.
- **Herbal formulations**: traditional, modern, novel (phytosomes); Schedule T = GMP for ASU drugs.
- **Herbal cosmetics**: aloe gel (acemannan), almond oil (oleic), lavender (linalyl acetate), olive (oleic, squalene), rosemary (cineole, camphor), sandalwood (santalols).
- **Phytochemical investigation**: authenticate → extract (increasing polarity) → screen → chromatograph → purify → UV, IR, NMR, MS. Rf = spot distance ÷ solvent front distance.

## Common MCQ traps

- Clove is a **flower bud**, not a fruit; mother clove is the fruit.
- Black catechu = **heartwood** extract (Leguminosae); pale catechu = **leaf** extract (Rubiaceae).
- Ergot is a **fungal sclerotium** on rye.
- Ephedrine has **nitrogen outside the ring** (protoalkaloid).
- Colchicine is almost **neutral**, not strongly basic.
- Aloin is a **C-glycoside**, so ordinary Borntrager's test needs prior ferric chloride hydrolysis.
- Lanolin is a **wax**, not a glyceride.
- Silk has **no sulphur**; wool does.
- Asava is **not boiled**; arishta uses a **decoction**.
