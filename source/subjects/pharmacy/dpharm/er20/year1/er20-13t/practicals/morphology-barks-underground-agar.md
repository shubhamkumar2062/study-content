# Morphology of barks, underground parts and agar: Cinnamon, Cinchona, Ginger, Rauwolfia, Agar

**Aim:** To identify cinnamon, cinchona, ginger, rauwolfia and agar by their morphological characters.

> Use museum specimens and compare with authentic samples. Follow your lab manual where it differs.

## Principle

Barks are described by shape (flat, curved, quill, double quill, compound quill), thickness, outer and inner surface, fracture, odour and taste. Roots and rhizomes are described by shape, branching, surface marks, fracture and the appearance of the cut surface. Agar, an unorganised drug, is described by form, colour, texture and behaviour in water.

## Requirements

Specimens of cinnamon, cinchona, ginger, rauwolfia and agar; hand lens; scale; blade; white paper; beaker; hot water bath; test tube.

## Procedure

1. Note the shape and size of each drug; measure the bark thickness.
2. Examine the outer and inner surfaces of the barks with a hand lens.
3. Break a piece and note the fracture (short, fibrous, splintery, granular).
4. Cut ginger and rauwolfia transversely and look at the cut surface.
5. Smell and taste (tiny amount; spit out).
6. For agar, note the form; soak a piece in cold water; boil 1 g in 100 ml water, then cool and observe.
7. Record and sketch.

## Observations

| Feature | Cinnamon | Cinchona |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shape | **Compound quills** (several thin quills packed one inside another) | Quills, curved or channelled pieces |
| Thickness | Thin, about 0.5 mm | Thicker, about 2–6 mm |
| Outer surface | Light yellowish-brown with fine wavy lines and small scars | Greyish-brown (reddish-brown in red bark), often with lichens; longitudinal and transverse cracks |
| Inner surface | Darker brown, finely striated | Reddish-brown, finely striated |
| Fracture | Splintery | Short in outer part, fibrous in inner part |
| Odour | Fragrant, sweet | Slight |
| Taste | Warm, sweet and aromatic | Intensely **bitter** and astringent |

| Feature | Ginger | Rauwolfia |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Part | Rhizome | Root |
| Shape | Branched in one plane like fingers of a hand ("hands" or "races"), laterally flattened | Cylindrical, slightly tortuous pieces, few rootlets |
| Size | About 5–15 cm long | About 5–15 cm long, up to about 2 cm thick |
| Surface | Buff; scraped ginger smooth with longitudinal lines; unscraped shows cork and leaf scars | Greyish-yellow to light brown; longitudinally wrinkled; bark peels easily |
| Fracture | Short and fibrous; cut surface shows a ring (endodermis) and yellowish dots of oil cells | Short; thin bark and dense pale-yellow wood |
| Odour | Aromatic | Indistinct |
| Taste | Pungent, warm | Bitter |

| Feature | Agar |
| --- | --- |
| Form | Thin translucent strips, flakes, sheets or powder |
| Colour | Yellowish-white to pale yellow |
| Texture | Tough when damp, brittle when dry; surface wrinkled |
| Odour / taste | Odourless; mucilaginous taste |
| Behaviour in water | Insoluble in cold water but swells; dissolves in boiling water; a 1% solution sets into a firm gel on cooling |

## Result

The drugs were identified as cinnamon (compound quills), cinchona (bitter bark), ginger (rhizome), rauwolfia (root) and agar (algal product).

## Precautions

1. Measure bark thickness at several places.
2. Do not confuse cinnamon with cassia bark, which is thicker, coarser and in single quills.
3. Taste only a tiny amount; rauwolfia is bitter and pharmacologically active — spit out and rinse.
4. Use clean glassware for the agar gel test.

## Viva questions

1. **What is a compound quill?** A bark rolled into a quill with several smaller quills packed inside it, as in cinnamon.
2. **What is the main adulterant of cinnamon?** Cassia bark (*Cinnamomum cassia*).
3. **Name the alkaloids of cinchona.** Quinine, quinidine, cinchonine and cinchonidine.
4. **What is Grahe's test?** Cinchona bark heated in a dry test tube gives red tarry vapours.
5. **Why are ginger pieces called hands?** The rhizome branches in one plane like the fingers of a hand.
6. **Give the source of agar.** Red algae such as *Gelidium amansii* and *Gracilaria* species.
7. **Give one use of agar in a laboratory.** Solid culture medium for bacteria.
8. **Name the antihypertensive alkaloid of rauwolfia.** Reserpine.
