# Physical and chemical tests: Asafoetida and Benzoin

**Aim:** To identify asafoetida (an oleo-gum-resin) and benzoin (a balsamic resin) by physical and chemical tests.

> Quantities are typical for a student batch. Follow your lab manual and your teacher where they differ. Work in a fume cupboard for heating and sublimation tests.

## Principle

- **Asafoetida** contains resin (ferulic acid esters), gum and a sulphur-containing volatile oil. Its gum forms an emulsion with water. On hydrolysis with HCl it gives **umbelliferone**, which shows a **blue fluorescence** in alkaline solution.
- **Benzoin** is a balsam containing free **benzoic acid** (and, in Sumatra benzoin, free **cinnamic acid**). Benzoic acid **sublimes** on heating. Cinnamic acid is oxidised by potassium permanganate to **benzaldehyde**, which has a bitter-almond odour.

## Requirements

Asafoetida, Sumatra and Siam benzoin; mortar and pestle; test tubes; dry test tube with a cooled watch glass or cotton plug for sublimation; filter paper; spirit lamp; water bath; reagents — conc. H2SO4, conc. HCl, dilute ammonia, alcohol (90%), 1% potassium permanganate, dilute H2SO4; UV lamp (365 nm).

## Procedure and observations

**A. Asafoetida**

| No. | Test | Observation | Inference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Note colour, odour and taste (do not swallow) | Yellowish-brown to reddish tears or mass; strong, garlic-like, unpleasant odour; bitter acrid taste | Characteristic of asafoetida |
| 2 | Triturate 0.5 g with 5 ml water | Orange-yellow (milky) **emulsion** forms | Gum + resin present |
| 3 | Put a drop of conc. H2SO4 on a freshly broken surface | **Red to reddish-brown** colour | Asafoetida resin |
| 4 | Boil 0.5 g with 5 ml conc. HCl for about a minute, filter, dilute the filtrate with water, make alkaline with dilute ammonia, view under UV light | **Blue fluorescence** | Umbelliferone formed on hydrolysis |
| 5 | Shake 1 g with 10 ml alcohol and filter; pour filtrate into water | Milky turbidity | Resin present |

**B. Benzoin**

| No. | Test | Sumatra benzoin | Siam benzoin | Inference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Appearance and odour | Greyish-brown masses of whitish tears in a reddish-brown matrix; odour like storax | Yellowish-brown tears, flattened; odour like vanilla | Two commercial types |
| 2 | Heat about 0.5 g in a dry test tube with a cool watch glass on top | White **crystalline sublimate** on the cool surface | White crystalline sublimate | **Benzoic acid** present |
| 3 | Warm 0.5 g with 10 ml 1% KMnO4 (slightly acidified) | **Odour of benzaldehyde** (bitter almond) | No benzaldehyde odour | **Cinnamic acid** present only in Sumatra benzoin |
| 4 | Dissolve 0.5 g in 5 ml alcohol, filter, pour into water | Milky emulsion | Milky emulsion | Resin present |

**Comparison table**

| Feature | Asafoetida | Benzoin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type | Oleo-gum-resin | Balsam (balsamic resin) |
| Source | *Ferula assa-foetida* (Umbelliferae) | *Styrax benzoin* / *S. tonkinensis* (Styracaceae) |
| Key test | Blue fluorescence (umbelliferone); emulsion with water | Sublimate of benzoic acid; KMnO4 test for cinnamic acid |

## Result

- The sample forming an emulsion with water, giving red with H2SO4 and blue fluorescence after HCl hydrolysis is **asafoetida**.
- The sample giving a sublimate of benzoic acid is **benzoin**; the one also giving benzaldehyde odour with KMnO4 is **Sumatra benzoin**, and the one without it is **Siam benzoin**.

## Precautions

1. Perform heating and sublimation in a fume cupboard; do not inhale fumes.
2. Use a perfectly dry test tube for sublimation.
3. Use a freshly broken surface of asafoetida for the H2SO4 test.
4. View fluorescence in a dark area under UV light; never look directly at the UV source.
5. Warm KMnO4 mixtures gently; do not boil over.

## Viva questions

1. **What is an oleo-gum-resin? Give two examples.** A resin with gum and volatile oil — asafoetida, myrrh (also guggul).
2. **Give the biological source of asafoetida.** Oleo-gum-resin from the rhizome and roots of *Ferula assa-foetida* and other *Ferula* species; Umbelliferae.
3. **Which compound causes the blue fluorescence in the asafoetida test?** Umbelliferone.
4. **Name common adulterants of asafoetida.** Gum arabic, rosin, gypsum, red clay, chalk, flour, stones.
5. **How do you differentiate Sumatra and Siam benzoin?** Sumatra contains cinnamic acid and gives benzaldehyde odour with KMnO4; Siam does not.
6. **What is Friar's balsam?** Compound benzoin tincture, used as an inhalation in cough and as a skin protective.
7. **Why is benzoin called a balsam?** It contains benzoic and/or cinnamic acid and their esters.
8. **What gives asafoetida its smell?** Sulphur compounds (disulphides) of its volatile oil.
