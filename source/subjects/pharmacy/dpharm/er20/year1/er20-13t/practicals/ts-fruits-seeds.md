# Transverse section of fruits and seeds: Ajwain, Coriander and Nux-vomica

**Aim:** To prepare and study the transverse sections of ajwain fruit, coriander fruit and nux-vomica seed, and to note their diagnostic characters.

> Follow your lab manual for the staining schedule. Nux-vomica is very poisonous (strychnine): never taste it, and wash hands after handling.

## Principle

Fruits of the Umbelliferae (ajwain, coriander) are cremocarps; a TS of one mericarp shows the pericarp with **vittae** (oil canals), vascular bundles at the ridges, the seed coat and a large oily endosperm. The number and position of vittae and the presence of sclerenchyma help to tell them apart. Nux-vomica seed has a testa of characteristic **lignified hairs** and a hard horny endosperm.

## Requirements

Ajwain and coriander fruits (soaked overnight), nux-vomica seeds (soaked or boiled until soft — they are very hard), razor blade, potato or pith block, chloral hydrate, phloroglucinol (1% alcoholic), conc. HCl, iodine solution, dilute glycerine, slides, cover slips, microscope.

## Procedure

1. Soften the fruits and seeds (soaking; nux-vomica needs long soaking or boiling).
2. Fix a single mericarp (or seed) in a potato block and cut thin transverse sections through the middle.
3. Clear in warm chloral hydrate.
4. Stain with phloroglucinol + conc. HCl; for nux-vomica also test a section with iodine (protein and aleurone stain yellow-brown).
5. Mount in dilute glycerine and observe under low and high power.
6. Draw labelled diagrams.

## Observations

| Tissue | Ajwain (*Trachyspermum ammi*) fruit | Coriander (*Coriandrum sativum*) fruit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outline of mericarp | Pentagonal; surface with many small **papillae** (warty outgrowths) | Curved arc (half of a globular fruit); wavy outline from ridges |
| Epicarp | Single layer of cells, papillose | Single layer with cuticle; small crystals of calcium oxalate |
| Mesocarp | Parenchyma with **vittae**: usually 4 on the dorsal side and 2 on the commissural side | Thick band of **lignified sclerenchyma** (fibres) — characteristic; dorsal vittae absent or reduced; **2 vittae on the commissural side** |
| Vascular bundles | Small bundles under each primary ridge | Small bundles at the primary ridges |
| Endocarp | Thin-walled cells | Thin, elongated cells |
| Seed coat | Brown layer of thin cells | Brown thin layer |
| Endosperm | Large, polygonal cells with fixed oil and aleurone grains | Curved (concave towards the commissure), cells with fixed oil, aleurone grains and small **rosette crystals** of calcium oxalate |
| Embryo | Small, at the top of the endosperm | Small |

| Tissue | Nux-vomica (*Strychnos nux-vomica*) seed |
| --- | --- |
| Testa (epidermis) | Each epidermal cell grows into a long, thick-walled, **lignified covering hair**, bent at the base and lying flat, with longitudinal thickening bars; stains pink with phloroglucinol |
| Below epidermis | Collapsed, brown layer of cells |
| Endosperm | Large, thick-walled cells with walls of hemicellulose, showing fine **plasmodesmata**; contains oil globules and aleurone grains; no starch |
| Embryo | Small, in a cavity in the endosperm |

## Result

The sections were identified as ajwain fruit (papillose epicarp, six vittae), coriander fruit (thick sclerenchyma band, two commissural vittae, curved endosperm) and nux-vomica seed (lignified bent hairs, thick-walled endosperm with plasmodesmata).

## Precautions

1. Cut sections through the middle of the fruit or seed so that all tissues appear.
2. Do not taste nux-vomica; it contains strychnine and brucine.
3. Clear well before staining; wipe off excess acid.
4. Use a fresh, sharp blade for the hard nux-vomica seed.

## Viva questions

1. **What are vittae?** Schizogenous oil canals in the pericarp of Umbelliferous fruits.
2. **How many vittae are seen in a coriander mericarp?** Two, on the commissural side; dorsal vittae are absent or rudimentary.
3. **Which tissue of coriander stains pink with phloroglucinol?** The sclerenchyma band in the mesocarp.
4. **What is the volatile oil constituent of ajwain?** Thymol.
5. **Give the alkaloids of nux-vomica.** Strychnine and brucine.
6. **What are plasmodesmata?** Fine strands of cytoplasm passing through pores in cell walls, seen in the endosperm of nux-vomica.
7. **What is the nature of the hairs on nux-vomica seed?** Lignified, thick-walled covering trichomes bent at the base.
8. **What is the commissural surface?** The flat inner surface where the two mericarps of a cremocarp are joined.
