# Family planning devices

**Aim:** To identify and study family planning devices and contraceptives, and to counsel a couple on choosing a method.

## Principle

Contraceptives prevent pregnancy by blocking sperm (barrier), preventing ovulation (hormonal), preventing fertilisation or implantation (IUD, centchroman), or by permanent closure of the vas or tubes. The pharmacist explains use, side effects and what to do when a dose is missed.

## Requirements

Demonstration samples or pictures: male and female condom, diaphragm, Cu-T 380A and Cu-T 375 (demonstration pieces), combined oral pill pack, centchroman tablets, DMPA injection vial, emergency contraceptive pill, a pelvic model, chart paper.

## Procedure

1. Display each device and label it with its name, type and mode of action.
2. Prepare a chart table of the methods (below).
3. Demonstrate correct use of a male condom on a model (check expiry, open carefully, pinch tip, roll on, remove and dispose in a dustbin).
4. Show how a 28-day pill pack is followed (21 hormone tablets + 7 iron tablets) and explain missed-pill advice as per the leaflet.
5. Role play counselling of a newly married couple and of a mother with a 3-month-old baby.

## Observations

| Method | Type | Action | Duration / use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Male condom | Barrier | Stops sperm entry; also prevents STDs/HIV | Each act |
| Diaphragm | Barrier | Covers cervix, used with spermicide | Each act |
| Cu-T 380A | IUD | Copper toxic to sperm, prevents implantation | About 10 years |
| Cu-T 375 | IUD | As above | About 5 years |
| Combined oral pill (Mala-N) | Hormonal | Stops ovulation | One tablet daily |
| Centchroman (Chhaya) | Non-hormonal pill | Prevents implantation | Twice weekly for 3 months, then weekly |
| DMPA (Antara) | Injectable progestin | Stops ovulation | Every 3 months |
| Levonorgestrel 1.5 mg | Emergency pill | Delays ovulation | Within 72 hours; not regular use |
| Vasectomy / tubectomy | Permanent | Blocks vas / tubes | Permanent |

## Result

Family planning devices were identified, their mode of action and use were studied, and counselling was practised.

## Precautions

- Maintain privacy and a non-judgemental attitude.
- Check expiry dates; store condoms away from heat.
- Combined pills are not advised for breastfeeding mothers in the first months, smokers over 35, or women with a history of clots; refer such women.
- IUD insertion and sterilisation are done only by trained providers.

## Viva questions

1. **Which method also protects against STDs?** The condom.
2. **How long is Cu-T 380A effective?** About 10 years.
3. **What is LAM?** Lactational amenorrhoea method — natural contraception with exclusive breastfeeding, baby under 6 months, no periods.
4. **When should an emergency pill be taken?** As early as possible, within 72 hours of unprotected sex.
5. **Which is the simpler permanent method?** Vasectomy (no-scalpel), done for the male partner.
