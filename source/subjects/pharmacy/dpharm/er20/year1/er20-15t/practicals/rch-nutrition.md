# Reproductive and child health (RCH): nutritional aspects and national programmes

**Aim:** To study the nutritional aspects of reproductive and child health and the related national programmes, and to prepare a chart or presentation for community education.

## Principle

The health of a child depends on the mother's nutrition before and during pregnancy, breastfeeding, and the child's diet in the first years ("first 1000 days"). The RCH approach, now part of RMNCH+A under NHM, covers adolescents, pregnant and lactating women, newborns and children with services including nutrition supplements.

## Requirements

Chart paper or slides, sample IFA tablets and syrup (or pictures), ORS and zinc packets, a growth chart (from the Mother and Child Protection card), MUAC tape.

## Procedure

1. Divide into groups: adolescent girls, pregnant women, lactating mothers, infants (0–6 months), young children (6 months–5 years).
2. For each group list the main nutritional needs, common deficiencies and the government service that addresses them.
3. Prepare a chart linking each service to its programme.
4. Demonstrate a growth chart entry and MUAC measurement on a doll or volunteer.
5. Role play counselling of a pregnant woman on IFA and diet.

## Observations — chart content

| Group | Needs / problems | Services and programmes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adolescents | Anaemia, early marriage | Weekly IFA (Anemia Mukt Bharat / RKSK), deworming, nutrition education, menstrual hygiene |
| Pregnant women | Extra energy, protein, iron, folic acid, calcium | Daily IFA from second trimester, calcium, Td, ANC (PMSMA), take-home ration from Anganwadi, JSY/JSSK |
| Lactating mothers | Extra energy and protein | Supplementary nutrition (ICDS), counselling on breastfeeding |
| Infants 0–6 months | Breast milk only | Early and exclusive breastfeeding, home-based newborn care by ASHA |
| Children 6 months–5 years | Complementary feeding, growth | Anganwadi supplementary nutrition, growth monitoring, IFA syrup, vitamin A, deworming, ORS + zinc, immunisation |
| School children | Nutrition, worms | PM POSHAN (mid-day meal), National Deworming Day, RBSK screening |

**Complementary feeding tips (from 6 months):** start with thick mashed foods (dal-rice, khichdi, mashed banana), add a little oil or ghee, increase frequency and variety with age, continue breastfeeding.

## Result

The nutritional needs at each stage of the RCH life cycle and the programmes that meet them were studied and presented as a chart.

## Precautions

- Give only current government guidance on doses of IFA and vitamin A; check with the programme guidelines.
- Use simple local-language messages; respect privacy when counselling.

## Viva questions

1. **What does RMNCH+A stand for?** Reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health.
2. **When should breastfeeding begin?** Within one hour of birth.
3. **What is ICDS?** Integrated Child Development Services, delivered through Anganwadi centres (supplementary nutrition, growth monitoring, pre-school education).
4. **Why give IFA in pregnancy?** To prevent maternal anaemia and low birth weight; folic acid prevents neural tube defects.
5. **What is MUAC?** Mid-upper arm circumference; a quick screen for acute malnutrition in children.
