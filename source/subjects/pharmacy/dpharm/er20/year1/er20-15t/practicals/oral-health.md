# Oral health and hygiene

**Aim:** To demonstrate correct oral hygiene practices and prepare awareness material on oral diseases and their prevention.

## Principle

Dental plaque — a sticky film of bacteria on teeth — turns sugars into acid that dissolves enamel (**dental caries**) and irritates gums (**gingivitis**, which can progress to **periodontitis** and tooth loss). Regular brushing with fluoride toothpaste, cleaning between teeth and limiting sugary snacks remove plaque and strengthen enamel. Tobacco and areca nut cause oral cancer and pre-cancerous patches.

## Requirements

Tooth model or large jaw model, soft-bristled toothbrush, fluoride toothpaste, dental floss, tongue cleaner, disclosing tablets (optional), mirror, chart paper with pictures of caries, gingivitis, oral thrush and leukoplakia.

## Procedure

1. Show a healthy tooth and a decayed tooth on a chart; explain how plaque forms.
2. Demonstrate brushing on the model: pea-sized amount of fluoride toothpaste; brush at a 45° angle to the gum line with gentle short strokes; clean outer, inner and chewing surfaces; about 2 minutes; twice a day (morning and before bed).
3. Demonstrate flossing between teeth and cleaning of the tongue.
4. (Optional) Chew a disclosing tablet to colour plaque, then brush and see the difference.
5. Prepare an awareness poster: "Brush twice, floss once, cut down sugar, quit tobacco, see a dentist yearly."
6. Role play counselling for a child with tooth decay and an adult chewing gutkha.

## Observations

| Practice | Correct method | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brushing | Twice daily, 2 minutes, soft brush, fluoride paste | Removes plaque, prevents caries |
| Toothbrush | Change every 3 months or when bristles fray | Effective cleaning |
| Flossing | Once daily | Cleans between teeth |
| Diet | Fewer sticky and sugary snacks between meals; rinse after sweets | Less acid attack |
| Tobacco/areca nut | Avoid | Prevents oral cancer and gum disease |
| Dental check-up | At least once a year | Early detection |

**Common oral problems:** dental caries, gingivitis, periodontitis, bad breath, oral ulcers, oral thrush, dental fluorosis (excess fluoride in water), oral cancer.

## Result

Correct brushing and flossing were demonstrated and awareness material on oral hygiene was prepared.

## Precautions

- Use only a pea-sized amount of toothpaste for children and supervise to avoid swallowing.
- Avoid hard brushes and very hard brushing, which wear gums.
- Refer any non-healing ulcer or white/red patch in the mouth to a dentist.

## Viva questions

1. **What is dental plaque?** A sticky film of bacteria and food residue on teeth.
2. **How does fluoride protect teeth?** It strengthens enamel and helps repair early decay.
3. **What is gingivitis?** Inflammation of the gums, with bleeding on brushing.
4. **Name two habits causing oral cancer.** Chewing tobacco/gutkha and smoking (also areca nut, alcohol).
5. **How often should a toothbrush be changed?** About every 3 months.
