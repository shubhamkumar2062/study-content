# Last-night revision sheet

If you only have an hour, master these: **WHO definition and dimensions of health**, **three-tier health system and NHM**, **family planning methods**, **breastfeeding vs bottle feeding**, **types of immunity and vaccines with cold chain**, **balanced diet and deficiency diseases**, **food adulteration**, **epidemiology terms**, **TB, malaria, dengue, cholera/diarrhoea, HIV**, **NTEP and NVBDCP**, and **the four types of pharmacoeconomic analysis**.

**Unit 1 — Introduction**

| Item | Key point |
| --- | --- |
| Social Pharmacy | Social, behavioural, economic factors in medicine use + pharmacist's public-health role |
| WHO health (1948) | Complete physical, mental and social well-being, not merely absence of disease |
| Dimensions | Physical, mental, social, spiritual, emotional, vocational |
| Determinants | Biology, lifestyle, environment, socio-economic, health services |
| IMR | Infant deaths < 1 yr per 1000 live births; best single health indicator |
| MMR | Maternal deaths per 1,00,000 live births |
| Population norms | Sub-centre 5,000 / PHC 30,000 / CHC 1,20,000 (hilly 3,000 / 20,000 / 80,000) |
| NHM | NRHM 2005 + NUHM 2013; ASHA, JSY, JSSK, RBSK, RKSK |
| NHP 2017 | Wellness, Health and Wellness Centres, free drugs and diagnostics |
| Goals | MDGs 8 (2000–15); SDGs 17 (2015–30), SDG 3 = health; FIP DGs 21 (2020) |

| Item | Key point |

**Unit 4 — Diseases at a glance**

| Disease | Agent | Spread | Key feature | Key prevention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chickenpox | Varicella-zoster virus | Droplet, contact | Itchy crops of vesicles | Varicella vaccine |
| Measles | Measles virus | Airborne | Koplik's spots, 3 Cs | MR vaccine, vitamin A |
| Diphtheria | C. diphtheriae | Droplet | Grey membrane, bull neck | Pentavalent/DPT |
| Pertussis | B. pertussis | Droplet | Whoop | Pentavalent/DPT |
| TB | M. tuberculosis | Droplet nuclei | Cough ≥ 2 weeks | BCG, case finding, 2HRZE/4HRE |
| Polio | Poliovirus | Faeco-oral | Acute flaccid paralysis | OPV, IPV |
| Cholera | V. cholerae | Water | Rice-water stools | Safe water, ORS |
| Typhoid | S. Typhi | Food, water | Step-ladder fever | Safe water, vaccine |
| Dengue | DENV 1–4 | Aedes (day) | Break-bone fever, low platelets | No NSAIDs, remove breeding sites |
| Malaria | Plasmodium | Anopheles (night) | Fever with chills | Nets, ACT / chloroquine + primaquine |
| Filariasis | W. bancrofti | Culex | Elephantiasis | MDA (DEC + albendazole) |
| Tetanus | C. tetani | Soil into wounds | Lockjaw | Td, clean delivery |
| Leprosy | M. leprae | Prolonged contact | Anaesthetic patch | MDT |
| HIV | Retrovirus | Sex, blood, mother–child | CD4 loss, AIDS | Condoms, ART, PPTCT |

Isolation = sick people; quarantine = healthy contacts. Triad = agent, host, environment. Diarrhoea = low-osmolarity ORS (245) + zinc 14 days. Trachoma = SAFE.

**Unit 6 — Pharmacoeconomics**

| Analysis | Outcome unit | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CMA | Equal outcomes | Pick the cheaper |
| CEA | Clinical units; ICER | Same type of outcome |
| CBA | Money; benefit-cost ratio | Different outcomes |
| CUA | QALY | Quality of life matters |

Costs = direct medical, direct non-medical, indirect, intangible. Outcomes = ECHO. QALY = years × utility.

**Role of pharmacist (write in every long answer):** educate, counsel, screen and refer, supply preventive products, support national programmes, report ADRs and cases, promote rational use and generics, safe disposal.
