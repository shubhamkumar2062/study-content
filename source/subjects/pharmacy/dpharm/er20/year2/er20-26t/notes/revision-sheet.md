## NDPS and Poisons (Unit 5)

- Narcotic drug = coca leaf, cannabis, opium, poppy straw, manufactured drugs. Charas = resin; ganja = flowering tops.
- UN conventions 1961, 1971, 1988. Authorities: Narcotics Commissioner, NCB.
- Small quantity: up to 1 year RI or ₹10,000; intermediate: up to 10 years + ₹1 lakh; commercial: 10–20 years RI + ₹1–2 lakh.
- Poisons Act: Centre controls import; States control possession and sale; poison register.

## Advertisements and animals (Unit 6)

- DMR Act bans ads for: miscarriage/contraception, sexual capacity, menstrual disorders, Schedule diseases; misleading ads; magic remedies (talisman, mantra, kavacha, charm).
- Exempt: RMP signboard, scientific book, confidential to RMPs, Government ads, ads with Government sanction.
- PCA Act: CPCSEA; IAEC with CPCSEA nominee; animals from registered breeders; 3Rs.

## Prices (Unit 7)

- DPCO 2013 under Essential Commodities Act 1955; NPPA enforces.
- Scheduled formulation = NLEM medicine. Ceiling price = average PTR (brands ≥1% share) + 16%.
- Yearly WPI revision; non-scheduled up to 10% a year. NLEM 2022 = 384 medicines (P, S, T).

## Ethics (Unit 8)

Job (scope, conduct of pharmacy, prescriptions, drugs, apprentices) · Trade (fair price, fair trading, genuine purchase, no hawking, dignified ads) · Medical profession (no diagnosis/prescribing, no clandestine arrangements, liaison) · Profession (vigilance, law-abiding, associations, decorum) · Oath.
