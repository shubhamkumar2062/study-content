# Use of community pharmacy software and digital health tools

**Aim:** To use community pharmacy software for billing, inventory and records, and to become familiar with digital health tools useful in community practice.

## Principle

Pharmacy management software keeps sales, purchases, stock, expiry, accounts and patient records in one computer system. It reduces errors, saves time and gives reports that help in management. Digital health tools – e-prescriptions, health record apps, reminder apps, teleconsultation platforms and connected devices – extend the pharmacist's services beyond the counter.

## Requirements

Computer with community pharmacy software (demo or trial version), barcode scanner and label printer if available, dummy invoices and prescriptions, smartphone with a medication reminder app, practical record book.

## Procedure

### A. Pharmacy software

1. **Master data:** create item records (name, generic name, strength, pack, manufacturer, HSN code, GST rate, schedule, storage condition, reorder level) and supplier records.
2. **Purchase entry:** enter a dummy invoice with batch numbers, expiry dates, quantity, rate and MRP; check that stock increases.
3. **Billing:** prepare a sale bill for a simulated prescription; note how the software selects the batch with the earliest expiry (FEFO) and blocks expired batches.
4. **Scheduled drugs:** bill a Schedule H1 item and view the automatic H1 register entry with patient and prescriber details.
5. **Patient record:** create a patient profile with allergies and long-term medicines; check any interaction alert.
6. **Reports:** print stock, reorder, near-expiry, sales by date and supplier dues reports.
7. **Accounts:** view day book, cash book and GST summary prepared by the software.
8. **Backup:** take a data backup and note the method.

### B. Digital health tools

1. Set a medication reminder for a simulated patient in a reminder app.
2. Study how an e-prescription or teleconsultation prescription is presented and verified.
3. Note how health records can be linked under the national digital health mission (ABHA health ID) – study only, using demonstration material; do not enter real personal data.
4. Use a connected glucometer or BP app if available to record and share readings.
5. List privacy and data-security precautions.

## Result

Billing, purchase entry, inventory reports and scheduled-drug records were carried out using pharmacy software, and selected digital health tools were studied.

## Precautions

1. Use dummy data only; never enter real patient information in a demo system.
2. Keep passwords private; log out after use.
3. Take regular backups.
4. Check software output (bill, label) before giving it to the patient.

## Viva questions

1. **Two advantages of pharmacy software?** Fast accurate billing; automatic expiry and reorder alerts.
2. **How does software help with Schedule H1?** It creates the register entry automatically.
3. **What is FEFO?** First expiry, first out.
4. **What is ABHA?** Ayushman Bharat Health Account – a digital health ID under ABDM.
5. **Why is data backup important?** To prevent loss of records if the computer fails.
