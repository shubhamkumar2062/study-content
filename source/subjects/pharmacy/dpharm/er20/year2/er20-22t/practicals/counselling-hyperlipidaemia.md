# Counselling a simulated patient with hyperlipidaemia

**Aim:** To counsel a simulated patient with hyperlipidaemia on the condition, lipid-lowering medicines and diet and lifestyle changes.

## Principle

Hyperlipidaemia is raised blood fats (cholesterol and/or triglycerides). High LDL ("bad") cholesterol and low HDL ("good") cholesterol increase the risk of heart attack and stroke. It causes no symptoms, so patients often stop treatment. **Statins** (atorvastatin, rosuvastatin, simvastatin) lower LDL cholesterol and reduce cardiovascular risk. Lifestyle change is part of every treatment plan.

## Requirements

Counselling area, simulated prescription (for example atorvastatin 10 mg once daily), sample lipid report, food chart, leaflet, assessment form.

## Procedure

1. **Introduction** and prime questions.
2. **Condition:** explain cholesterol, LDL and HDL in simple words; it is silent but blocks arteries over years.
3. **Explain the lipid report:**

| Test | Desirable (adults) |
| --- | --- |
| Total cholesterol | Below 200 mg/dL |
| Triglycerides | Below 150 mg/dL |
| HDL cholesterol | 40 mg/dL or more (men), 50 mg/dL or more (women) |
| LDL cholesterol | Target set by the doctor according to the patient's risk |

4. **Statin counselling:**
   - Take once daily, every day; atorvastatin and rosuvastatin at any fixed time; simvastatin is usually taken in the evening.
   - Report unexplained muscle pain, weakness or dark urine.
   - Liver tests as advised.
   - Not to be used in pregnancy or breast-feeding.
   - Avoid large amounts of grapefruit juice with simvastatin and atorvastatin; limit alcohol.
   - Continue long term; benefit is in preventing heart attack, not in feeling better.
5. **Diet:** less saturated fat (ghee, butter, fried and fast foods), avoid trans fats (vanaspati, bakery items), more fibre (whole grains, oats, pulses, fruits, vegetables), use small amounts of healthy oils, control sugar and refined carbohydrates (raise triglycerides).
6. **Lifestyle:** regular exercise, weight control, stop smoking, limit alcohol, control BP and diabetes.
7. **Follow-up:** repeat lipid test as advised.
8. **Closing:** summary, teach-back, leaflet, documentation.

## Result

The simulated patient was counselled on hyperlipidaemia, statin use and lifestyle changes.

## Precautions

1. Give practical Indian diet examples.
2. Stress long-term adherence even without symptoms.
3. Refer muscle pain or yellowing of eyes promptly.

## Viva questions

1. **Which cholesterol is called "good"?** HDL.
2. **Main side effect to report with statins?** Unexplained muscle pain or weakness.
3. **Why is simvastatin given in the evening?** Cholesterol production is higher at night and it acts for a short time.
4. **Can statins be used in pregnancy?** No.
5. **Name two foods rich in trans fats.** Vanaspati and many bakery and fried snack items.
