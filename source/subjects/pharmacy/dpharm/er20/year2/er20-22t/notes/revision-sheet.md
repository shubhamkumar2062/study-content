# Last-night revision sheet

If you have only a few hours, learn these with full points: **parts of a prescription and prescription handling**, **labelling and ancillary labels**, **dispensing errors**, **stages and barriers of counselling**, **counselling points for chronic diseases**, **adherence factors and strategies**, **health screening values**, **OTC and self-medication**, **minor ailments with referral points**, **legal requirements to open a pharmacy**, **inventory control (ABC, VED, EOQ)** and **day book and cash book**.

## Unit 1: Practice, responsibilities, GPP, SOPs

| Fact | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Community pharmacy | Pharmacy serving the public directly – medicines plus advice |
| Pharmaceutical care concept | Hepler and Strand, 1990 |
| Pharmacy Act / Drugs and Cosmetics Act / Rules | 1948 / 1940 / 1945 |
| Pharmacy Practice Regulations | PCI, 2015 |
| GPP | FIP 1993; joint FIP/WHO guidelines 2011; four roles: supply, therapy management, professional performance, public health |
| SOP | Written, approved step-by-step instruction; parts – purpose, scope, responsibility, procedure, records, approval, review date |

## Unit 2: Prescription

- Parts: prescriber details, date, patient details, **Rx (superscription)**, **inscription** (drug, strength), **subscription** (to pharmacist), **signa** (to patient), refill, signature.
- Abbreviations: od, bd, tds, qid, hs, ac, pc, stat, sos, prn.
- Handling: receive → legality → completeness → clinical check → clarify → prepare → label → final check → counsel → record.
- Label: pharmacy, patient, drug + strength, directions, quantity, date, prescriber, "keep out of reach of children".
- Ancillary: shake well; external use only; may cause drowsiness; avoid alcohol; with food; swallow whole; complete course; refrigerate 2–8 °C.
- Errors: wrong drug (LASA), strength, form, dose, quantity, patient; prevent by tall-man lettering, separate storage, double check, counselling, error reporting.

| Schedule | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| H | Prescription only; Rx on label |
| H1 | Separate register, kept 3 years |
| X | Duplicate prescription, copy kept 2 years, lock and key |

## Unit 3: Communication

- Process: sender → message → channel → receiver → feedback; noise.
- Types: verbal, non-verbal, written, visual, electronic.
- SOLER: Square, Open, Lean, Eye contact, Relaxed. SBAR for talking to doctors.
- Questions: open, closed, probing, reflective; avoid leading.
- WWHAM: Who, What symptoms, How long, Action taken, Medicines.
- ASMETHOD: Age, Self, Medication, Extra medicines, Time, History, Other symptoms, Danger symptoms.

## Unit 4: Counselling

- Stages: introduction → content → process → closing (summary, teach-back, follow-up, document).
- Prime questions: what is it for, how to take it, what to expect.
- Barriers: patient (language, literacy, hearing), pharmacist (time, knowledge), environment (noise, privacy), system.

| Disease | Must-say points |
| --- | --- |
| Hypertension | Daily lifelong; less salt; exercise; do not stop suddenly; avoid NSAIDs/decongestants |
| Diabetes | Hypoglycaemia signs and 15–20 g sugar; insulin at 2–8 °C, rotate sites; foot care; HbA1c |
| Asthma | Reliever vs preventer; inhaler technique; rinse mouth after steroid; action plan |
| TB | Full 6-month course; orange urine (rifampicin); report jaundice; contraceptive interaction |
| COPD | Stop smoking; inhalers; vaccination; early care for flare-ups |
| AIDS | Very high ART adherence; interactions; safe sex; prophylaxis |

- PPI: manufacturer's patient leaflet in pack – required in EU, some products in USA (Medication Guides); not generally in India.
- PIL: simple leaflet on disease/medicine, often made by the pharmacist.

## Unit 5: Adherence and screening

- Adherence (WHO 2003); compliance is the older term; concordance = shared agreement.
- WHO factors: social/economic, health system, condition, therapy, patient.
- Fixes: counsel, simplify, FDCs, reminders, pill boxes, generics, family, DOT, follow-up.

| Screen | Refer at |
| --- | --- |
| BP | ≥140/90 mmHg repeated |
| Fasting glucose | ≥126 mg/dL (100–125 prediabetes) |
| Random glucose | ≥200 mg/dL |
| HbA1c | ≥6.5% |
| BMI (Asian) | 23–24.9 overweight; ≥25 obese |
| Waist | Men ≥90 cm, women ≥80 cm |
| SpO2 | <95% attention; <90% urgent |
| Peak flow | <80% of best warning; <50% danger |

BMI = kg ÷ m². A pharmacy reading is a screen, not a diagnosis.

## Units 6–7: OTC and minor ailments

- India has no legal OTC schedule; prescription drugs = H, H1, X; Schedule K exemptions (household remedies).
- Pharmacist: assess → decide (advise/supply/refer) → select → counsel → safety net → record.

| Ailment | First-line | Refer if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pain | Paracetamol; ibuprofen with food; RICE | Worst-ever headache, stiff neck, back pain with leg weakness |
| Cough | Dry – suppressant/demulcent; wet – expectorant | >2–3 weeks, blood, breathlessness |
| Cold | Fluids, steam, saline, antihistamine; decongestant drops ≤1 week | >10 days, ear/facial pain |
| Diarrhoea | ORS; zinc in children; loperamide adults | Blood, fever, dehydration |
| Constipation | Fibre, fluids, ispaghula, lactulose | Blood, weight loss, change of habit |
| Vomiting | ORS, small meals | Blood, >24–48 h, dehydration |
| Fever | Paracetamol, fluids, tepid sponge; no aspirin in children | >3 days, fits, non-fading rash |
| Sore throat | Gargles, lozenges | Breathing/swallowing difficulty, >1 week |
| Skin | Clotrimazole (fungal), permethrin 5% (scabies, treat contacts), benzoyl peroxide (acne) | Infection, widespread |
| Oral | Choline salicylate gel; chlorhexidine; dentist for toothache | Ulcer >3 weeks |

## Unit 8: Setting up and inventory

- Licences: Form 20 (other than C, C1, X), Form 21 (C, C1); wholesale 20B/21B; Schedule X separate.
- Conditions: registered pharmacist, ≥10 m² retail (≥15 m² retail + wholesale), refrigerator, records, display licence.
- Other: shop and establishment/trade licence, GST, NDPS if narcotics.
- Site: population, doctors nearby, competition, visibility, rent.
- Design: dispensing counter, counselling area, refrigerator, locked Schedule X cupboard, expired-goods area.
- Reorder level = daily use × lead time + safety stock. EOQ = √(2AO/H).
- ABC (value: A ≈ 10–20% items, 70–80% value), VED (vital/essential/desirable), FSN (fast/slow/non-moving); FEFO.

## Unit 9: Finance and management

- Fixed vs working capital; break-even sales = fixed costs ÷ gross margin ratio; DPCO 2013, NPPA.
- Day book = journal, book of original entry, date order; cash book: receipts Dr (left), payments Cr (right); single/double/triple column; petty cash.
- Software: billing, stock, expiry, accounts, H1 register, interactions.
- CRM: reminders, home delivery, loyalty, camps, complaints.
- Audit: financial, stock, regulatory, prescription, clinical, self-inspection; cycle – standard → measure → compare → change → re-audit.
- Digital health: ABDM, ABHA, Telemedicine Guidelines 2020; mHealth apps; e-pharmacy draft rules 2018 not finalised.
