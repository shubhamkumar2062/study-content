# Analgesic activity by the acetic acid writhing test

**Aim:** To study the analgesic activity of a drug (for example aspirin or diclofenac) by the acetic acid-induced writhing test in mice, by simulation.

## Principle

Intraperitoneal injection of a dilute **acetic acid** solution (commonly 0.6% v/v, 10 ml/kg) irritates the peritoneum and causes release of prostaglandins and other pain mediators. The mouse shows **writhing**: stretching of the body, contraction of the abdominal muscles and extension of the hind limbs. **Peripherally acting analgesics (NSAIDs)** that inhibit prostaglandin synthesis, and also opioids, reduce the number of writhes. The test is sensitive and simple, but not specific.

## Requirements

Simulation software; in the simulated set-up: mice, 0.6% acetic acid, aspirin or diclofenac (standard), test drug, saline, syringes, observation boxes, stopwatch.

## Procedure

1. Divide mice into control, standard and test groups.
2. Give saline, standard or test drug (orally or i.p.) and wait for the absorption time shown (e.g. 30 minutes for i.p., longer for oral).
3. Inject acetic acid i.p.
4. After a short latent period (about 5 minutes), count the number of writhes for a fixed period (e.g. 10 or 15 minutes).
5. Calculate the percentage inhibition of writhing.

```text
 % inhibition = (Mean writhes in control − Mean writhes in treated) ÷ Mean writhes in control × 100
```

## Result

The standard drug reduced the number of writhes by ___%, showing peripheral analgesic activity.

## Precautions

1. Use freshly prepared acetic acid of the correct strength.
2. Count writhes for exactly the same time in all groups.
3. Inject i.p. in the lower abdomen to avoid puncturing the gut.

## Viva questions

1. **What is writhing?** Abdominal contraction with body stretching and hind-limb extension due to peritoneal pain.
2. **Which mediators are involved?** Prostaglandins, bradykinin and others.
3. **Which drugs are detected by this test?** Peripheral analgesics (NSAIDs) and also central analgesics.
4. **What is the standard drug?** Aspirin or diclofenac (morphine for central analgesics).
5. **Why is a latent period allowed?** Writhing starts a few minutes after the acetic acid injection.
