# Effect of analgesics using an analgesiometer

**Aim:** To study the analgesic effect of a drug (for example morphine or pentazocine) in mice or rats using the **Eddy's hot plate** or **tail-flick (radiant heat) analgesiometer**, by simulation.

## Principle

Thermal pain tests measure the **reaction time** of an animal to a heat stimulus. **Centrally acting analgesics** (opioids such as morphine, pentazocine, tramadol) raise the pain threshold, so the reaction time increases. Peripherally acting NSAIDs act poorly in these tests.

- **Hot plate (Eddy's):** the animal is placed on a plate kept at a constant temperature (commonly about 55 °C); the time until it licks its paw or jumps is the reaction time.
- **Tail-flick:** a heated wire (radiant heat) under the tail; the time until the tail is flicked away is recorded.

A **cut-off time** (for example 15 seconds on the hot plate, 10–12 seconds for tail-flick) is fixed so the tissue is not injured.

## Requirements

Simulation software; in the simulated set-up: mice or rats, hot plate or tail-flick analgesiometer, morphine (standard), test drug, saline, syringes, stopwatch.

## Procedure

1. Divide the animals into groups: control (saline), standard (morphine) and test.
2. Record the **basal reaction time** of each animal (mean of two or three readings).
3. Give the drug by the route shown (s.c. or i.p.).
4. Record the reaction time at intervals (e.g. 15, 30, 60, 90 minutes), stopping at the cut-off time.
5. Calculate the increase in reaction time and the percentage analgesia.

## Precautions

1. Keep the plate temperature constant and check it before each test.
2. Never exceed the cut-off time.
3. Use animals of similar weight and sex; acclimatise them to the room.
4. Record readings at exact times.

## Viva questions

1. **Which analgesics are detected by the hot plate test?** Centrally acting analgesics such as opioids.
2. **Why is a cut-off time used?** To prevent tissue damage.
3. **What is the reaction time?** Time between applying the heat stimulus and the animal's response (paw licking, jumping or tail flick).
4. **Which receptor does morphine act on?** Mainly µ opioid receptors.
5. **Which test is used for peripheral analgesics?** Acetic acid writhing test.
