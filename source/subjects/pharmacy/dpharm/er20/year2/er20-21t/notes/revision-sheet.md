# Last-night revision sheet

If you have only a few hours, learn these topics with full classifications: **routes of administration and biotransformation**, **cholinergic and anticholinergic drugs (with OP poisoning)**, **adrenergic drugs and β-blockers**, **NSAIDs and paracetamol**, **antiepileptics and opioids**, **antihypertensives and antianginals**, **anticoagulants (heparin vs warfarin)**, **anti-ulcer drugs**, **diuretics**, **insulin and oral hypoglycaemics**, **corticosteroids**, **antihistamines**, **penicillins and anti-TB drugs**, **antimalarials**.

## General pharmacology (Unit 1)

| Topic | Key point |
| --- | --- |
| PK vs PD | ADME (body on drug) vs actions and mechanism (drug on body) |
| Fastest route / 100% bioavailability | IV |
| Avoid first-pass | Sublingual, transdermal, parenteral (partly rectal) |
| Bioavailability | AUC oral ÷ AUC IV × 100 |
| Phase I / Phase II | Oxidation, reduction, hydrolysis / conjugation (glucuronide commonest) |
| Enzyme inducers | Rifampicin, phenytoin, carbamazepine, phenobarbitone |
| Enzyme inhibitors | Erythromycin, ketoconazole, cimetidine, ciprofloxacin |
| Alkaline urine | Removes weak acids (aspirin, phenobarbitone) |
| Steady state | About 4–5 half-lives |
| Agonist / antagonist | Affinity + intrinsic activity / affinity only |

## Eye (Unit 3)

Miotic: pilocarpine. Mydriatic-cycloplegics: atropine (7–10 days) > homatropine > cyclopentolate > tropicamide (4–6 h). Phenylephrine: mydriasis without cycloplegia. Glaucoma: latanoprost, timolol, dorzolamide/acetazolamide, brimonidine, pilocarpine, mannitol. Mydriatics are contraindicated in angle-closure glaucoma.

## Autocoids (Unit 11)

Histamine from histidine (mast cells): H1 – bronchoconstriction, itching; H2 – acid. First-generation antihistamines sedate and are anticholinergic; cetirizine, fexofenadine are non-sedating. 5-HT from tryptophan (90% in gut); ondansetron blocks 5-HT3; sumatriptan (5-HT1B/1D agonist) for migraine. PGs from arachidonic acid (COX); misoprostol – NSAID ulcer; dinoprostone – labour; carboprost – PPH; alprostadil – patent ductus.

## Biologicals (Unit 14)

Made from living cells; injected; cold chain 2–8 °C; biosimilars = similar, not identical. Live vaccines not in pregnancy. Equine sera → serum sickness. -ximab chimeric, -zumab humanised, -umab human, -cept fusion protein. Rituximab (CD20), trastuzumab (HER2), infliximab/adalimumab (TNF-α; screen for TB), omalizumab (IgE).
