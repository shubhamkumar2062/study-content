# SOAP note for a clinical case of depression

**Aim:** To prepare and discuss a SOAP note for a clinical case of depression.

## Principle

Depression is treated with psychotherapy and antidepressants (SSRIs first line). Early drop-out is common because benefit takes 2–4 weeks while side effects come first. Assessment must include suicide risk, and counselling must be supportive and non-judgemental.

## Requirements

Case details, PHQ-9 or similar scale, thyroid test, prescription, references, SOAP sheet, a quiet private place for discussion.

## Procedure

1. Record mood symptoms (SIG E CAPS), duration, stressors, sleep, appetite, alcohol, previous episodes, family history.
2. **Ask directly about suicidal thoughts**; note risk.
3. Record PHQ-9 score, weight, TSH, other illnesses and drugs that can cause depression.
4. Review the antidepressant: dose, duration so far, side effects, interactions.
5. Identify DRPs; write plan and counselling.

## Observations (worked hypothetical case)

**Case:** Ms M, 29 years, software engineer, low mood, loss of interest, poor sleep and appetite for 6 weeks after a job loss. Started sertraline 10 days ago; stopped it because "it is not working and gives nausea". No suicidal plan but feels hopeless at times. Takes tramadol occasionally for headache.

| SOAP | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| **S** | Low mood, anhedonia, insomnia, poor appetite, hopelessness; stopped sertraline after 10 days due to nausea and "no effect"; occasional tramadol |
| **O** | PHQ-9 score 17 (moderately severe); weight loss 3 kg; TSH normal |
| **A** | Moderate–severe depression. DRPs: (1) **non-adherence** due to unrealistic expectation of quick effect and early side effects; (2) **interaction risk** – tramadol with SSRI (serotonin syndrome, seizures); (3) suicide risk needs monitoring |
| **P** | Restart sertraline after taking it with food (nausea settles in 1–2 weeks) – confirm with prescriber; refer for CBT; avoid tramadol – use paracetamol for headache; follow-up in 1–2 weeks, then regularly; give crisis contact numbers. Monitor PHQ-9, side effects, suicidal thoughts |

**Counselling:** medicine is not habit-forming; benefit begins in 2–4 weeks; nausea usually settles; take after breakfast; continue for months after recovery; never stop suddenly; avoid alcohol and tramadol; contact the doctor or a helpline immediately if thoughts of self-harm increase; regular exercise, routine and support from family or friends.

## Result

A SOAP note for depression was prepared; premature stopping of the SSRI and a tramadol interaction were identified, and supportive counselling with a monitoring plan was provided.

## Precautions

Assess suicide risk sensitively; maintain strict confidentiality; dispense limited quantities if risk is high; refer urgently if active suicidal intent.

## Viva questions

1. **First-line antidepressants?** SSRIs (fluoxetine, sertraline, escitalopram).
2. **When does the effect start?** About 2–4 weeks.
3. **How long to continue after remission?** At least 6–12 months.
4. **What is discontinuation syndrome?** Dizziness, irritability, flu-like symptoms on stopping suddenly.
5. **Drugs causing serotonin syndrome with SSRIs?** MAOIs, tramadol, linezolid, triptans.
