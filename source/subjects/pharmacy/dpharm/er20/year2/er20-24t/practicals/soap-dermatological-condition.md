# SOAP note for a clinical case of a dermatological condition (scabies)

**Aim:** To prepare and discuss a SOAP note for a dermatological condition; scabies is taken as the example (the same format suits psoriasis or eczema).

## Principle

Scabies is a contagious mite infestation. Cure needs correct application of a scabicide (permethrin 5%) to the whole body, a repeat after a week, simultaneous treatment of all close contacts and washing of clothes and bedding. Most "treatment failures" are due to wrong application or untreated contacts.

## Requirements

Case details, prescription, scabicide demonstration tube, references, SOAP sheet.

## Procedure

1. Record itching (night-time), duration, affected family members, previous treatment and how it was applied.
2. Record lesion sites (burrows, papules), secondary infection.
3. Check the drug, method, amount and repeat schedule; check contacts' treatment.
4. Identify DRPs; plan and counsel.

## Observations (worked hypothetical case)

**Case:** Master T, 9 years, severe itching at night for 3 weeks; mother and sister also itch. Permethrin cream was applied only on the itchy spots once and washed off after 1 hour. Lesions are in finger webs, wrists and around the waist, some with crusts and pus.

| SOAP | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| **S** | Nocturnal itch for 3 weeks; family members itching; incorrect permethrin use (spots only, 1 hour, once) |
| **O** | Burrows and papules in finger webs, wrists, waist; a few pustules (secondary infection) |
| **A** | Scabies with secondary bacterial infection. DRPs: (1) **incorrect application** (area, contact time, no repeat); (2) **contacts untreated**; (3) infection not treated |
| **P** | Re-apply permethrin 5% to the whole body from the neck down, leave 8–12 hours (overnight), wash off, repeat after 7 days; treat mother and sister at the same time; antibiotic for infected lesions as prescribed; antihistamine at night for itch; hot-wash clothes and bedding |

**Counselling:** apply to cool, dry skin after a bath, covering finger webs, under nails, between toes and genitals; re-apply to hands if washed; all family members treated the same night; wash clothes, towels and bedding in hot water and dry in sun, or seal in a bag for 3 days; itching may continue for 2–4 weeks after cure – do not keep applying cream.

## Result

A SOAP note for scabies was prepared; incorrect application and untreated contacts were identified as causes of failure, and correct use was counselled.

## Precautions

Avoid lindane; oral ivermectin not for young children or pregnancy; check for crusted scabies in institutions.

## Viva questions

1. **Causative organism?** Sarcoptes scabiei var. hominis.
2. **Drug of choice?** Permethrin 5% cream.
3. **Why repeat after 7 days?** To kill mites that hatch from eggs after the first application.
4. **Why treat contacts without itching?** They may be infested but not yet itchy (itch takes weeks to develop).
5. **Why does itching continue after treatment?** Allergic reaction to dead mites and their products.
