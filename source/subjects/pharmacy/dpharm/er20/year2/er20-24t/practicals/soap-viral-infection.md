# SOAP note for a clinical case of viral infection (COVID-19)

**Aim:** To prepare and discuss a SOAP note for a clinical case of a viral infection; mild COVID-19 is taken as the example (the same format suits influenza, herpes zoster or dengue).

## Principle

Most viral infections are self-limiting and need supportive care; specific antivirals exist only for some (oseltamivir for influenza, acyclovir for herpes, remdesivir in selected COVID-19 patients). Antibiotics do not act on viruses. In mild COVID-19 the key points are isolation, symptom relief, oxygen monitoring and recognising warning signs; steroids are not used in mild disease.

## Requirements

Case details, RT-PCR/antigen report, pulse oximeter, thermometer, prescription, references, SOAP sheet.

## Procedure

1. Record symptoms, onset date, contacts, vaccination status, high-risk conditions (age, diabetes, hypertension, lung disease).
2. Record temperature, pulse, respiratory rate, SpO2, test result.
3. Classify severity (mild, moderate, severe).
4. Review the prescription for unnecessary drugs and interactions.
5. Identify DRPs; write plan, monitoring and counselling.

## Observations (worked hypothetical case)

**Case:** Mr G, 40 years, fever, sore throat and body ache for 2 days; RT-PCR positive for SARS-CoV-2. No breathlessness. Known diabetic on metformin. A local clinic prescribed azithromycin, dexamethasone tablets and multivitamins.

| SOAP | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| **S** | Fever, sore throat, myalgia for 2 days, no breathlessness; diabetic; fully vaccinated |
| **O** | Temperature 38.4 °C; pulse 96/min; respiratory rate 18/min; SpO2 97% on room air; random glucose 190 mg/dL |
| **A** | Mild COVID-19 in a diabetic. DRPs: (1) **unnecessary antibiotic** (no bacterial infection); (2) **steroid in mild disease** – no benefit, raises blood sugar and risk of fungal infection (mucormycosis); (3) need for sugar monitoring |
| **P** | Suggest to prescriber: stop azithromycin and dexamethasone; paracetamol for fever; fluids, rest, home isolation; monitor SpO2 and temperature 3–4 times a day and blood sugar daily; seek care if SpO2 below about 94%, breathlessness, chest pain or confusion; continue diabetes medicines |

**Counselling:** isolate and wear a mask at home; paracetamol for fever; drink fluids; check oxygen with the pulse oximeter (warm finger, rest, wait for a steady reading); antibiotics and steroids are not needed for mild illness; return immediately with warning signs; complete recommended vaccination.

## Result

A SOAP note for mild COVID-19 was prepared; unnecessary antibiotic and steroid use were identified and replaced with supportive care and monitoring.

## Precautions

Follow current national clinical guidance (it changes); infection-control measures during counselling; refer high-risk patients early.

## Viva questions

1. **Receptor used by SARS-CoV-2?** ACE2.
2. **When are steroids used in COVID-19?** In patients needing oxygen (moderate–severe), not in mild disease.
3. **Antiviral for influenza?** Oseltamivir.
4. **Pain-killers to avoid in dengue?** Aspirin and NSAIDs.
5. **Gold-standard test for COVID-19?** RT-PCR.
