# SOAP note for a clinical case of COPD

**Aim:** To prepare and discuss a SOAP note for a clinical case of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

## Principle

COPD causes persistent, progressive airflow limitation, mainly from smoking and biomass smoke. Management: stop exposure, long-acting bronchodilators (LAMA/LABA), vaccination, pulmonary rehabilitation, and early treatment of exacerbations. Inhaler handling (especially capsule devices) is a frequent problem.

## Requirements

Case details, spirometry report, prescription, placebo dry powder (capsule) inhaler, pulse oximeter, references, SOAP sheet.

## Procedure

1. Record breathlessness (mMRC grade), cough, sputum, exacerbations in the past year, hospital admissions.
2. Record smoking (pack-years) and biomass exposure, other diseases (heart failure, diabetes).
3. Record spirometry (FEV1/FVC, FEV1 % predicted), SpO2, BMI.
4. Check inhaler device and technique; vaccination status.
5. Identify DRPs; write plan and counselling.

## Observations (worked hypothetical case)

**Case:** Mr H, 66 years, farmer, smoker of beedis for 40 years. Breathless on walking 100 m; morning cough with sputum. Prescribed tiotropium capsules for inhalation; he has been **swallowing the capsules**. Two exacerbations in the past year. Never vaccinated.

| SOAP | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| **S** | Progressive breathlessness, chronic productive cough, continuing smoker, swallowing inhalation capsules, two exacerbations/year |
| **O** | Post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC 0.58, FEV1 48% predicted; SpO2 93% at rest; BMI 19 |
| **A** | COPD, moderate–severe airflow limitation, frequent exacerbations. DRPs: (1) **wrong route** – inhalation capsule swallowed; (2) continued smoking; (3) no vaccination |
| **P** | Teach capsule inhaler use (load, pierce, breathe in deeply, hold breath); consider LAMA + LABA combination (prescriber); smoking cessation counselling with nicotine replacement; influenza and pneumococcal vaccines; pulmonary rehabilitation; nutrition support; written plan for early signs of exacerbation. Monitor symptoms, exacerbations, SpO2 |

**Counselling:** the capsule must be placed in the device and inhaled, never swallowed; stop smoking – the most important step; avoid smoke from fires; pursed-lip breathing; seek care early if sputum turns yellow-green or breathlessness worsens.

## Result

A COPD SOAP note was prepared; incorrect administration (swallowing inhalation capsules), smoking and missing vaccination were identified and addressed.

## Precautions

Use controlled oxygen in COPD; check for heart failure; demonstration devices only.

## Viva questions

1. **Spirometric criterion for COPD?** Post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC below 0.70.
2. **Most effective intervention?** Smoking cessation.
3. **Example of LAMA?** Tiotropium.
4. **Why are ICS used carefully in COPD?** Raise pneumonia risk; reserved for frequent exacerbators.
5. **Vaccines recommended?** Influenza (yearly) and pneumococcal.
