# SOAP note for a clinical case of rheumatoid arthritis

**Aim:** To prepare and discuss a SOAP note for a clinical case of rheumatoid arthritis, with emphasis on safe methotrexate therapy.

## Principle

Rheumatoid arthritis is an autoimmune polyarthritis. Early DMARD therapy (methotrexate as the anchor drug) prevents joint damage; NSAIDs and short courses of steroids only relieve symptoms. Methotrexate is given **once weekly** with folic acid and needs blood monitoring – dosing errors and missed monitoring are common drug-related problems.

## Requirements

Case sheet, prescription, CBC, liver and kidney function tests, ESR/CRP, RF and anti-CCP reports, references, SOAP sheet.

## Procedure

1. Record joint symptoms (which joints, symmetry, morning stiffness duration), function, fatigue.
2. Record other diseases, pregnancy plans, alcohol, infections (TB, hepatitis B history).
3. Record joint count, ESR/CRP, blood counts, liver and kidney tests.
4. Check DMARD schedule, folic acid, NSAID use with gastroprotection, steroid dose.
5. Identify DRPs, write the plan and counselling.

## Observations (worked hypothetical case)

**Case:** Mrs P, 38 years, school teacher, painful swollen fingers and wrists on both sides for 5 months with morning stiffness of 2 hours. Started on methotrexate; the strip label reads "once a week" but she has been taking one tablet every day for 6 days and now has mouth ulcers. She also takes diclofenac twice daily on an empty stomach.

| SOAP | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| **S** | Symmetrical small-joint pain, morning stiffness 2 hours, fatigue; mouth ulcers for 2 days; diclofenac twice daily without food; plans pregnancy next year |
| **O** | Tender, swollen MCP and PIP joints both hands; ESR 52 mm/h; CRP raised; RF and anti-CCP positive; WBC mildly low; liver enzymes mildly raised |
| **A** | Active RA. DRPs: (1) **methotrexate taken daily instead of weekly** – toxicity (mouth ulcers, low WBC, raised enzymes) – urgent; (2) NSAID without gastroprotection or food; (3) no folic acid; (4) pregnancy plan while on a teratogenic drug |
| **P** | Contact prescriber at once: stop methotrexate, assess toxicity (folinic acid rescue as decided by the doctor), repeat CBC and LFT. Restart weekly dosing when safe, with folic acid on another day. Take NSAID with food; add PPI if needed. Reliable contraception; plan pregnancy with the rheumatologist (methotrexate must be stopped months before). Physiotherapy and exercise |

**Counselling:** methotrexate is taken **only once a week on the same day** (mark it on a calendar); folic acid on a different day; regular blood tests; avoid alcohol; report sore throat, fever, mouth ulcers, breathlessness or bruising; avoid cotrimoxazole and extra pain-killers without advice; use contraception.

## Result

A serious medication error (daily methotrexate) and other DRPs were identified in an RA patient; an urgent plan and detailed counselling were prepared.

## Precautions

Methotrexate dosing errors are life-threatening – always confirm the weekly day; screen for TB and hepatitis B before biologics; no live vaccines on immunosuppressants.

## Viva questions

1. **Anchor DMARD in RA?** Methotrexate.
2. **Why folic acid with methotrexate?** Reduces side effects such as mouth ulcers and nausea.
3. **Antibodies seen in RA?** Rheumatoid factor and anti-CCP.
4. **Which joints are spared in RA?** Distal interphalangeal joints.
5. **Eye check is needed for which DMARD?** Hydroxychloroquine.
