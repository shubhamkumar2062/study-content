# SOAP note for a clinical case of diabetes mellitus

**Aim:** To prepare and discuss a SOAP note for a clinical case of type 2 diabetes mellitus.

## Principle

Type 2 diabetes is managed by life-style change, metformin first, and other drugs chosen by the patient's needs (heart or kidney disease, weight, cost, hypoglycaemia risk). Targets usually include HbA1c below 7%, BP and lipid control, and prevention of complications. Hypoglycaemia and poor foot care are common preventable problems.

## Requirements

Case details, prescription, glucometer, HbA1c, lipid, creatinine/eGFR, urine albumin reports, references, SOAP sheet.

## Procedure

1. Record symptoms, duration of diabetes, diet, activity, hypoglycaemic episodes, complications (eyes, feet, kidneys, heart).
2. Record weight, BMI, BP, FPG, PPG, HbA1c, lipids, kidney function, urine albumin.
3. Review each antidiabetic drug: timing in relation to meals, dose for kidney function, hypoglycaemia risk.
4. Identify DRPs; write plan, monitoring and counselling (diet, foot care, hypoglycaemia).

## Observations (worked hypothetical case)

**Case:** Mrs N, 55 years, type 2 diabetes for 6 years, on metformin and glimepiride. Skips lunch on fasting days and has had sweating and shaking twice. Walks barefoot at home; has a small painless crack on the heel. Also has hypertension (not on treatment).

| SOAP | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| **S** | Two episodes of sweating/tremor on fasting days; barefoot at home; painless heel crack; tingling feet |
| **O** | BMI 30; BP 148/92 mmHg; FPG 168 mg/dL; HbA1c 8.4%; eGFR normal; urine albumin raised; reduced foot sensation on monofilament test |
| **A** | Uncontrolled type 2 diabetes with neuropathy and early nephropathy; hypertension untreated. DRPs: (1) **hypoglycaemia** from sulfonylurea with skipped meals; (2) **untreated indication** – hypertension with albuminuria (needs ACEI/ARB); (3) foot-care risk |
| **P** | Suggest: replace glimepiride with a drug of low hypoglycaemia risk (DPP-4 inhibitor or SGLT2 inhibitor – kidney benefit) per prescriber; add ACE inhibitor/ARB; statin per risk. Diet plan with regular meals; walk 30 minutes daily. Foot care education and referral for the heel crack. Monitor: home glucose, HbA1c after 3 months, BP, kidney function and potassium |

**Counselling:** recognise hypoglycaemia and treat with 15 g sugar, recheck in 15 minutes; never skip meals on these drugs; take metformin with food; inspect feet daily, never walk barefoot, moisturise but not between toes, see a doctor for any wound; yearly eye and kidney check.

## Result

A SOAP note for uncontrolled type 2 diabetes was prepared; drug-induced hypoglycaemia, untreated hypertension with albuminuria and foot risk were identified and addressed.

## Precautions

Adjust metformin and other drugs for kidney function; check glucometer technique; confidentiality.

## Viva questions

1. **HbA1c target for most adults?** Below 7%.
2. **Drug class most likely to cause hypoglycaemia (oral)?** Sulfonylureas.
3. **Rule of 15?** 15 g fast sugar, recheck after 15 minutes.
4. **Antihypertensive preferred in diabetes with albuminuria?** ACE inhibitor or ARB.
5. **Adverse effect of long-term metformin?** Vitamin B12 deficiency.
